Accelerating the future of energy, together
The AES Corporation is a Fortune 500 energy company. With an outcome-focused approach, we partner with communities, utilities, and businesses to create energy solutions and integrate technologies that meet their rapidly growing need for reliable and clean power.
AES is the largest US-based global power company and a major source of energy innovation, changing the way the world creates, distributes, and stores electricity. With a diverse generation fleet, we are a top seller of clean energy to corporations and one of the largest energy providers to technology companies, powering the AI revolution and industries of the future.
Our portfolio
We are a leading investor, owner, and operator of power generation, utilities and LNG infrastructure. Our diverse generation fleet includes solar, hydro, wind, natural gas, coal and battery storage assets. With our diversified approach and wide portfolio of solutions, we meet the evolving energy needs of our customers.

ENERGY INNOVATION AT AES
Innovation that powers the future
At AES, our vast industry knowledge and insight together with our entrepreneurial culture enables us to push the boundaries of what’s possible in energy. We collaborate, test, prove, and scale breakthrough solutions with our partners and customers to meet their energy needs while accelerating the transition to a smarter energy future.
A legacy of innovation
AES has always taken on the biggest challenges in energy.
In the 2000s, we pioneered lithium-ion battery storage solutions, which enabled us to introduce the first utility-scale lithium-ion storage project. Then, in the 2010s, we scaled this technology into a multi-billion-dollar industry. In the 2020s, we accelerated the integration of different generation technologies, energy storage, and digital solutions to enhance grid resiliency and lower power costs.
Today, we continue pushing the boundaries of what’s possible, bringing transformative, tailored solutions to our customers and the world.
A proven model for innovation
Innovation at AES isn't just about ideas. We follow a structured, insights-driven approach that optimizes our operations, improves outcomes for our customers, and develops new business lines that shape the future of energy. Our proven framework grounds every breakthrough in strategy and execution.
Core: Automating energy forecasting
AES data scientists partnered with Google to develop the Farseer, an AI Generation Forecasting and Marketing Automation Program to help forecast renewable asset output, while establishing an automation framework for streamlining our operations. It has generated nearly $9 million in benefits since its creation.
New: Innovating for 24/7 carbon-free energy
Carbon-free energy is crucial for meeting the growing energy demand required to power AI. AES provides carbon-free energy 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, to customers like Google and beyond.
Exponential: Pioneering solar robotics
AES created Maximo, the world’s first AI-enabled solar robot, and Max IQ, AI-amplified intelligence. Skilled construction crews rely on Maximo to improve safety and speed by performing the heavy lifting of placing and attaching solar modules. Together they optimize project installation and power a full end-to-end mechanical solar module installation. Customers like Amazon benefit from Maximo by accelerating large-scale solar projects and helping meet workforce demand.
Innovation in our culture
Energy Star
AES' Energy Star program is our foundational transformation system. It enables us to rapidly implement and scale emerging technologies, including AI, across our business. This approach isn’t just about efficiency; it’s about fundamentally reshaping the way we operate, from renewables to supply chain and beyond.
AES Next
AES Next combines entrepreneurship and deep industry context to build and invest in a portfolio of high-growth businesses and strategic partnerships. From pioneering storage solutions to revolutionizing solar deployment, AES Next’s projects are at the forefront of driving energy innovation.
APEX
APEX is an AES initiative that aims to drive continuous improvement with methodologies and tools to solve problems and address areas for growth.
Innovation jobs
Our people are our power. All of our jobs prioritize innovation and encourage collaboration.
Energy Innovation Challenge
AES’ Energy Innovation Challenge is a competition that brings together teams of graduate students from diverse backgrounds to create solutions to relevant problems the energy industry is facing.
Advancing AI in the energy sector
At AES, artificial intelligence is woven into everything we do: enhancing efficiency in our internal operations, building new businesses, and driving smarter, more sustainable solutions for our customers.
Powering AI for the world: data centers
With the rapid growth of artificial intelligence, electricity demand is soaring. As corporations' number one clean energy provider, AES helps hyperscalers efficiently meet their increasing power needs.
Advancing AI for the industry: AI Fund
Through our partnership with AI Fund, we’re co-building AI-powered startups that accelerate the energy transition by optimizing renewables, enhancing worksite safety, and helping renewables projects access clean energy finance. By investing in cutting-edge AI solutions, we’re not just adapting to the future of energy; we’re creating it.
Leveraging AI for our business: Aurora
We launched Aurora, an initiative that enables our people to responsibly and efficiently incorporate artificial intelligence into our day-to-day operations. We’ve created an internal competition to promote AI-backed solution-building, built AI tools into our operating systems, and educated our people on how artificial intelligence can boost productivity and efficiency.

Political process commitments & contributions
AES engages in the political process and believes good government policy benefits all AES stakeholders including customers, employees and shareholders. We participate in the public policy process in the United States in a variety of ways including corporate government affairs programs designed to educate and inform elected officials on key public policy issues related to the company’s business. AES’ direct lobbying activities support our business objectives and carbon reduction targets, and are consistent with the goals of the Paris Agreement.
We provide an annual voluntary political contribution and expenditure report for the United States which can be found here:
Trade association memberships
AES maintains memberships in trade and other associations which help advance our business objectives. These associations provide a forum for members to share ideas and develop new ways to improve the industry and provides benefits to AES’ various stakeholders. Our membership in and payments to specific trade associations or other organizations does not mean that we endorse every position or issue that the organizations may support. AES maintains an active role in the trade associations in which we belong so that we may address any significant conflicts between our values and perspectives of the trade associations. This includes a review of these trade associations and engagement with them to ensure alignment with the goals of the Paris Agreement, to the extent this is not already the case.
If we identify any actual or potentially significant misalignment on these or other high-priority issues, we will advocate for our views within the trade association and, depending on the circumstances, we may publicly dissent from a trade association position. Before considering withdrawal from any trade association due to misalignment, we would consider the overall benefits from continued membership and the impact we can have from remaining in the association.
Public policy engagement
We regularly communicate with government policymakers, public officials and regulators at the federal, state and local levels in order to protect and advance the long-term goals and interests of all AES stakeholders. AES has established processes to align its public policy engagement activities with its business objectives. Our senior executives with responsibility for external and government affairs drive this process to ensure coordination and consistency across all AES businesses. These processes are also designed to ensure that all public policy engagement activities are consistent with our Values, the AES Ethics and Compliance Program, and applicable laws and regulations.
Lobbying
Our Lobbying Policy requires approval by the AES Chief Ethics and Compliance Officer prior to engaging any individuals and entities to provide lobbying services on behalf of AES. This system of governance also helps to ensure that our climate related lobbying activities and trade association memberships are aligned with the Paris Agreement.
In addition to our voluntary Political Contribution Reports, AES files quarterly lobbying activity reports with the Office of the Clerk of the U.S. House of Representatives and the Secretary of the U.S. Senate. These reports disclose:
- Lobbying expenses incurred in connection with lobbying activities including all payments to retained lobby firms, outside entities or through trade association membership.
- Lobbying contributions, including those made through The AES Political Action Committee.
Governance around political and charitable activities
AES maintains a Charitable Contribution and Political Donations Policy to ensure all charitable contributions and political donations made by AES, or any of its subsidiaries on behalf of AES, are consistent with our Values, the AES Ethics and Compliance Program, and the laws and regulations that govern such practices in the United States and internationally. Political contributions must be approved by the AES Ethics and Compliance Department under the direction of the Chief Ethics and Compliance Officer or the General Counsel’s Office under the direction of the General Counsel.
Annually, the Governance Committee of the Board of Directors reviews the charitable contributions and political donations made by AES and its subsidiaries. The Committee also periodically reviews the Company’s contributions to trade associations, including any amounts related to charitable, political and lobbying expenditures and related activities.

AES is a global power company with generation and distribution businesses. We apply our development, construction and operational expertise across our global business platform to provide affordable and sustainable power in 12 countries.
What does AES stand for?
The company was originally founded as Applied Energy Services, which was later contracted to AES. Today the company’s legal name is The AES Corporation.
How many employees does AES have?
As of December 31, 2024, the Company and its subsidiaries had approximately 9,100 full-time / permanent employees.
When did AES go public?
AES went public on June 25, 1991.
Where is AES common stock listed?
AES common stock is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: AES).

For AES people, how we accomplish our successes and deliver our products is as important as the services we provide.
It is our duty and responsibility to conduct business with the highest level of integrity in all situations. Consistent with our Values, our people are expected to uphold the highest ethical standards and must comply with all applicable laws and accurately record and track all business transactions.
Code of Conduct
Our Code of Conduct , the AES Values Guide, is structured around our company Values: Safety First, Highest Standards, and All Together. The Code helps our people translate our Values into action by describing the business practices and principles that AES people are expected to apply to the work we do and the business decisions we make on behalf of the Company. The Code summarizes our key compliance policies, including those covering anti-corruption, conflicts of interest, gifts and entertainment, sanctions and trade controls, charitable contributions and political donations, third party risk management, and whistleblower protection.
Training and Communications
We conduct regular training and communication campaigns on our Code of Conduct, company policies, and other compliance topics. We provide resources to managers to reinforce their ethical expectations and obligations as leaders. Through the AES Ethics Champions Program, we promote open discussion of actual AES business issues and problems so that participants can discuss how AES values and policies are applied in real-world situations.
Investigations & Corrective Action
At AES, we strive to create a work environment where AES employees feel free to raise questions or concerns. We welcome all questions, no matter how small, and provide a variety of outlets for seeking help. We reinforce our commitment to ethics and compliance with the expectation that all employees report suspected violations of laws and company policies.
Our confidential whistleblower hotline, the AES Helpline, is available for AES people and stakeholders to anonymously raise concerns or seek guidance. AES people are encouraged to use the helpline for guidance regarding legal or regulatory requirements, clarification of AES policies or procedures, and for counsel on appropriate behavior. AES strictly prohibits retaliation against employees who in good faith report ethics and compliance concerns.
Visit aeshelpline.com to ask a question, report a concern, or follow-up on a report. If you need to report an outage or have a question, please visit AES Indiana or AES Ohio.
Third Party Management
We hold our business partners and contractors to the same high ethical standards that are expected of all AES people. AES partners undergo a thorough due diligence process, and strict compliance language is included in contacts, where appropriate. Compliance language may address such issues as corruption, influence peddling, legal and regulatory compliance, and reporting requirements. Compliance Officers work closely with business partners, contractors and project development teams to identify potential ethical concerns and resolve issues before agreements or contracts are signed.
Risk Assessments & Program Audits
We conduct frequent audits and assessments of our Ethics and Compliance Program to ensure that the Program is effective and that emerging risks are identified and managed. Assessment tools include internal audits, site visits to operating businesses, surveys and focus groups.

Innovation at AES
AES Next
AES Next builds and invests in a portfolio of high-growth businesses and strategic partnerships that drive the global energy transition. By identifying key challenges and opportunities in the energy sector, we apply deep industry expertise to develop and scale breakthrough solutions—from pioneering energy storage to revolutionizing solar deployment—creating tangible value for our company, partners, and communities.
AES Next Portfolio
AES Next follows a proven approach to identity, select, develop and scale strategic business opportunities to create long-term value for our business, our partners and the industry.
Maximo: revolutionizing solar installation through robotics
To keep up with increased energy demand, AES developed Maximo, the first AI-enabled solar robot for the end-to-end installation of solar modules. Skilled construction crews rely on Maximo to improve speed and safety by automating the strenuous heavy lifting of solar panels to accelerate solar installation.
Safer
Maximo automates the heavy lifting of solar plans, reducing strenuous work and lifting by 90%.
Faster
Maximo makes solar deployment 2x faster than traditional methods and accelerates the delivery of more megawatts in less time, utilizing a mobile microgrid and AI-enabled computer vision for precise module placement.
Cost Effective
Maximo reduces time to power, enhances safety and accessibility, and enables EPCs and solar project developers to deliver more solar cost-effectively and efficiently than traditional installation methods.
AI Fund: Co-building AI-powered startups
Through our partnership with Andrew Ng's AI Fund, we’re co-building AI-powered startups that accelerate the energy transition. By incubating and investing in cutting-edge AI solutions, we’re not just adapting to the future of energy; we’re creating it.
Context & insight
The industry urgently needs solutions that optimize energy generation and storage management, enhance worksite safety, and expand access to clean energy finance. By combining AES’s energy expertise with AI Fund’s leadership in AI, we’re tackling some of the industry’s most pressing challenges faster and smarter than ever before.
Action
AES and AI Fund developed a fast-track incubation model to identify, fund, and launch AI-powered energy solutions. This agile, venture-driven approach ensures that the most impactful ideas get scaled quickly, delivering next-level results for customers and the energy sector.
Outcome
- 3 companies co-founded in the first year of partnership
- In just six months, our first startup progressed from concept to funding
Fluence: scaling energy storage
Building on our pioneering work in lithium-ion storage, AES joined forces with Siemens to launch Fluence, a global leader in grid-scale energy storage, supercharging the energy transition with award-winning technology and AI-optimized solutions.
Context & insight
Energy storage was viewed as risky, unknown technology when we first started pioneering the technology. We knew it had untapped potential that was key to unlocking a more sustainable and reliable energy future.
Action
AES was the first to go big into utility-scale batteries in 2007 and built one of the world’s largest battery fleets over the next decade. Siemens is one of the world’s biggest energy and grid technology providers. We joined together in 2017 to create the industry powerhouse Fluence, dedicated to innovating modern electric infrastructure.
Outcome
Today, Fluence is a global leader in energy storage and digital optimization. We are accelerating the energy transition with gigawatts of projects successfully contracted, deployed, and under management across nearly 50 markets worldwide.

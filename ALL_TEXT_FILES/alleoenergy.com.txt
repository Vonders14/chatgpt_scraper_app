Partner With Us
Alléo Energy Bay Minette Facility
In June 2020, Alleo acquired an underutilized biomass conversion plant in Bay Minette, near Mobile, AL—a region abundant in wood resources. Permits were swiftly secured, enabling the technical team to conduct vital R&D on its proprietary wood-to-hydrocarbon system. This yielded a finalized design, strong intellectual property, and an elite on-site team. The first reactor launched in Q1 2023, achieving proof-of-product and nameplate production. The second reactor followed in Q1 2025, delivering green hydrogen at nameplate yields with multi-day continuous operations. Alleo's technology is now scale-ready, proving its commercial viability.

Partner With Us
Simeon Chow
Chief Executive Officer
Mr. Chow has over 30 years of experience in mechanical engineering, logistics and transportation industry and is the owner of a logistics consulting firm, Global Link One, Inc. Mr. Chow also worked for the Boeing Corporation, United Parcel Service and Con-Way Freight before turning his attention to the commercialization of biomass conversion technology which he has been working on for about 15 years. A mechanical engineer by training, Mr. Chow received his Bachelor of Science from University of California, Irvine, and an MBA from University of Southern California.
Tim Adkins
Chief Financial Officer
Mr. Adkins has over 25 years of experience in structured finance, private equity and insurance. Prior to co-founding Alléo Energy, Mr. Adkins was a managing principal and founder at a real estate private equity firm in Austin, Texas (2017-2019), a registered principal at an investment banking firm focused on private placements in energy, life sciences and technology (2004-2007). He is currently a partner in a real estate development firm founded in 2018. Since 2010, Mr. Adkins has been a partner in a risk management consulting firm. Mr. Adkins has also served on the advisory board for a clinical stage private life sciences company since 2005, for which he has negotiated and raised over $15,000,000 of private equity through challenging market conditions. In his career, Mr. Adkins has structured, negotiated, and raised over $100,000,000 of equity for private placements, and significantly more than that in debt. He has served as a fiduciary and general partner for multiple investment partnerships in his career. Mr. Adkins has a bachelor’s degree from University of California, Irvine.
Matthew Wilde
Chief Operating Officer
Mr. Wilde’s career as a project manager and development consultant goes back 25 years, the last five of which have been devoted to the acquisition and build out of the Bay Minette conversion facility. He has been responsible for all phases of the real estate development cycle, from assembly, acquisition and design through project financing, construction management and ongoing operations. He has been directly involved in all phases of the project development process on real estate transactions totaling over $350M. Mr. Wilde received his bachelor’s degree in Construction Management and Real Estate Finance from Washington State University.
Tim Cayford
Chief Strategy Officer
Mr. Cayford has over 20 years’ experience in industrial strategy and development. After setting up his consultancy business in Europe, Mr. Cayford advised dozens of European and global energy companies on various renewable energy topics including carbon emission mitigation. He has provided board-level strategic advice to international energy associations in Brussels and has managed several strategic committees with multiple industry working groups for Boardroom initiatives, EU renewable energy legislation, and UN policy frameworks. He has published articles on innovation and given numerous expert presentations concerning technically and economically viable ways to achieve the the global energy transition. Mr. Cayford began focusing on the commercialization of green hydrogen from biomass conversion technology over 10 years ago. Mr. Cayford holds a B.A. from the University of Washington in Economics, and a MSc in International and Institutional Economics from La Sorbonne, Paris, France.
Ray Fletcher
Chief Technology Officer
Mr. Fletcher has over 35 years’ industrial experience in chemical engineering, refinery operations, and process optimization - including catalyst operation, design, and optimization (liquid/liquid, vapor/solid, homogeneous/heterogeneous). He is a specialist in focusing on new technology development and design. Mr. Fletcher draws upon a wide network of industrially recognized experts to fully meet the requirements of each specific project he has undertaken. Mr. Fletcher has been in approximately 2/3 of the world’s petroleum refineries (outside of China), holds 9 industry patents, and has authored 50 industry articles. Mr. Fletcher holds a Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering from the University of Washington.

Partner With Us
BAY MINETTE, Ala.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Alléo Energy, a pioneer in sustainable energy solutions, today announced a cellulose-to-hydrogen process yielding over one hundred kilograms of hydrogen per ton of cellulosic waste. Alléo’s proprietary process converts cellulosic waste into abundant carbon-negative green hydrogen, capable of achieving negative Carbon Intensity (CI) scores that surpass competing approaches. By transforming this waste into clean energy, Alléo is poised to contribute a significant step forward for the global hydrogen market, valued at $280 billion in 2025 and projected to grow at a CAGR of 9.3% through 2030.
The renewable energy requirement of the Alléo process is less than 63% of the benchmark established by water electrolysis. This efficiency enables production costs well below competing initiatives, making Alléo green H2 scalable, safe, and reliable for widespread adoption. Alléo has achieved this production in a commercial-scale reactor under multi-day operations. Alléo intends to sequester 6 kg CO2 for every kg of hydrogen produced: Alléo-produced H2 will result in a six-fold reduction in greenhouse gas emissions!
“In a few short months, we expect to begin selling H2 into the transportation market from our first commercial plant in Bay Minette. Alléo’s carbon-negative green hydrogen is sustainably abundant and doesn't require subsidies to be profitable; it’s a game-changer," said Simeon Chow, CEO of Alléo Energy. “We want to thank our investors and partners who have carried us to this point. We believe their patience will be rewarded as we execute our business plan to scale up production at multiple sites around the world.”
Alléo's Bay Minette facility in Alabama, operational since 2023, has proven its reforming technology on a commercial scale. The first reactor achieved nameplate production in Q1 2023 in liquid fuels focused operations and commissioned a second reactor in Q1 2025 demonstrating scalability, yield, safety, and reliability in multi-day continuous operations.
For more information, visit www.alleoenergy.com.
About Alléo Energy - Alléo Energy develops carbon-negative technologies to produce green hydrogen, renewable fuels (SAF), and biochar from cellulosic waste. Headquartered in Bay Minette, Alabama, the company is committed to a sustainable future through innovative, scalable solutions.

Amp Americas develops and operates on-farm dairy RNG projects, sources and markets RNG to supply fuel to some of the country’s largest fleet customers, and partners with dairy farmers across the U.S. to bring more ultra-low CI gas to market.
Amp Americas received the first dairy waste-to-vehicle fuel pathway certified by California's Air Resources Board (CARB) and was awarded a Carbon Intensity (CI) score of -254.94 gCO2e/MJ, then the lowest score issued by CARB.

Amp Americas Investors
2017
In August 2017, Amp Americas, announced a $47 million equity commitment from Houston-based EIV Capital.
2019
In July 2019, Amp Americas raised $75 million of new equity in a round led by EIV Capital.
2021
Amp Americas tripled its footprint of carbon-negative dairy RNG Operations with two transactions. First, Amp expanded its partnership with Generate Capital, consolidating Generate Capital’s flagship Indiana dairy RNG project assets into Amp Americas’ expanding dairy RNG portfolio, with Generate investing in the corporate equity of Amp Americas. Amp has managed the Indiana projects since developing them in 2012.
Amp Americas also acquired a group of RNG project assets in Idaho from another developer. The Idaho projects consist of three digesters at two gas processing plants and one electric power generation plant that capture methane from over 940,000 gallons of dairy waste per day generated by 36,000 cows across three dairies.
2023
Amp Americas secured a new $400M commitment from its capital sponsors EIV Capital and Generate Capital to continue investing in dairy RNG operations and grow its portfolio of methane abatement projects on American dairy farms.
Founded in 2009, EIV Capital, LLC is a Houston-based private equity firm specializing in providing growth equity to the North American energy industry. EIV focuses on infrastructure and related service businesses with an emphasis on businesses involved in the processing, transportation, storage, or conversion of oil, natural gas, renewable fuels and refined products. EIV responsibly invests with companies that endeavor to create long-term value for all stakeholders and has included investments that focus on emissions reductions and renewable fuels in its portfolio since inception. The firm’s management has extensive experience leading and investing in successful companies across the energy value chain.
Generate Capital, PBC is a leading sustainable infrastructure company driving the infrastructure revolution. Generate builds, owns, operates and finances solutions for clean energy, water, waste and transportation. Founded in 2014, Generate partners with over 40 technology and project developers and owns and operates more than 2,000 assets globally. Generate is the one-stop shop offering pioneers of the infrastructure revolution tailored funding and support needed to get projects built. Generate’s Infrastructure-as-a-Service model delivers affordable, reliable and sustainable resources to over 2,000 customers, companies, communities, school districts and universities.

Our Mission
Build, own, operate, and profit from a portfolio of assets producing 100% renewable, carbon-negative fuels and feedstocks from waste at farms, industrial facilities, and municipal waste facilities
We act with integrity in accordance with our core values:
Safety
Trusting Relationships
Excellence
Entrepreneurship
Personal Responsibility
The Amp Way
We succeed in our mission and achieve our vision by operating safely, reducing greenhouse gases, improving air quality, providing well-paying jobs in rural communities, and creating superior financial returns for our employees and our shareholders.
We conduct our work with a shared understanding of the Amp Way:
Safety
We work safely. Safety is our number one priority in everything we do. We will devote resources to train and equip our people to work safely and eliminate workplace hazards.
We expect zero injuries and zero harm to health, safety, and the environment. All injuries are preventable, health risks are controllable, and each of us is accountable to make this so.
Trusting Relationships
We treat everyone with respect unconditionally. We demand respect from others. We do everything in our power to earn and command respect by living our values.
We value action over words.
Yet, our words matter. We do what we say we will do.
We are here for the long-haul. We act urgently, but we are disciplined and patient. We do not trade short-term gains for long-term sustainability.
Excellence
We each give all of our professional creative energy to our mission.
We demand honesty of ourselves and our partners. If we say it, we have proof to back it up. If we are not sure, we explain our assumptions. If we are mistaken, we own the mistake without qualification.
We will know more than anyone else about our field because we work incessantly to stay ahead.
Entrepreneurship
We are the innovators. We cannot outsource innovation. Our job is to continually invent new ways to ensure we achieve our mission.
We are the underdog. Whether we’ve just succeeded or failed. Whether we’re ahead or behind. Someone is always trying to eat our lunch, and they will if we don’t jealously protect our “Day 1” mindset.
We are biased to action. Leadership has an obligation to consider dissent, but we will not be deterred or delayed by indecision. Once the decision is made, EVERYONE must be committed. We will move forward and fail fast. We will learn from our mistakes, and we will not make them twice.
Our team is our strongest weapon. The team depends on individuals striving toward a common goal. We create our own opportunities, and we support each other to achieve our shared mission. We are creators and not order-takers. Personal advancement and status are byproducts of team achievement not our primary goals.
Personal Responsibility
We are owners. Our job is to make our mission happen. We rely on partners and we choose them freely. If our partners do not deliver, it is our responsibility, our money, our legacy. We will choose partners that embrace and live our values.
Sustainability requires profitability. If we do not create economic value, our environmental impacts will not sustain.
If we disagree, we say so. Everyone in our organization, regardless of hierarchy, has an obligation to speak up when they disagree with a proposed course of action or anything material that impacts our company’s mission.
We value diversity and will act to increase the diversity of our team in gender, race, lifestyle, and viewpoint. We will prioritize diversity in our recruiting, hiring, and workforce development. We will protect an inclusive work environment by living our core values.
Not everyone can accept our mission and our culture. For those who embrace the Amp Way, we commit to changing the world together and creating an unparalleled experience.

Our Mission
Build, own, operate, and profit from a portfolio of assets producing 100% renewable, carbon-negative fuels and feedstocks from waste at farms, industrial facilities, and municipal waste facilities

The Amp Way
We succeed in our mission and achieve our vision by operating safely, reducing greenhouse gases, improving air quality, providing well-paying jobs in rural communities, and creating superior financial returns for our employees and our shareholders.
We conduct our work with a shared understanding of the Amp Way:
Safety
We work safely. Safety is our number one priority in everything we do. We will devote resources to train and equip our people to work safely and eliminate workplace hazards.
We expect zero injuries and zero harm to health, safety, and the environment. All injuries are preventable, health risks are controllable, and each of us is accountable to make this so.
Trusting Relationships
We treat everyone with respect unconditionally. We demand respect from others. We do everything in our power to earn and command respect by living our values.
We value action over words.
Yet, our words matter. We do what we say we will do.
We are here for the long-haul. We act urgently, but we are disciplined and patient. We do not trade short-term gains for long-term sustainability.
Excellence
We each give all of our professional creative energy to our mission.
We demand honesty of ourselves and our partners. If we say it, we have proof to back it up. If we are not sure, we explain our assumptions. If we are mistaken, we own the mistake without qualification.
We will know more than anyone else about our field because we work incessantly to stay ahead.
Entrepreneurship
We are the innovators. We cannot outsource innovation. Our job is to continually invent new ways to ensure we achieve our mission.
We are the underdog. Whether we’ve just succeeded or failed. Whether we’re ahead or behind. Someone is always trying to eat our lunch, and they will if we don’t jealously protect our “Day 1” mindset.
We are biased to action. Leadership has an obligation to consider dissent, but we will not be deterred or delayed by indecision. Once the decision is made, EVERYONE must be committed. We will move forward and fail fast. We will learn from our mistakes, and we will not make them twice.
Our team is our strongest weapon. The team depends on individuals striving toward a common goal. We create our own opportunities, and we support each other to achieve our shared mission. We are creators and not order-takers. Personal advancement and status are byproducts of team achievement not our primary goals.
Personal Responsibility
We are owners. Our job is to make our mission happen. We rely on partners and we choose them freely. If our partners do not deliver, it is our responsibility, our money, our legacy. We will choose partners that embrace and live our values.
Sustainability requires profitability. If we do not create economic value, our environmental impacts will not sustain.
If we disagree, we say so. Everyone in our organization, regardless of hierarchy, has an obligation to speak up when they disagree with a proposed course of action or anything material that impacts our company’s mission.
We value diversity and will act to increase the diversity of our team in gender, race, lifestyle, and viewpoint. We will prioritize diversity in our recruiting, hiring, and workforce development. We will protect an inclusive work environment by living our core values.
Not everyone can accept our mission and our culture. For those who embrace the Amp Way, we commit to changing the world together and creating an unparalleled experience.

Grant Zimmerman
CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER & DIRECTOR
Grant Zimmerman is CEO and Director of Amp Americas. He joined the company as Senior Vice President of Business Development in 2015 and assumed the role of CEO in 2016.
As CEO, Grant reimagined the company’s business model by divesting its network of CNG fueling stations and creating and executing an innovative dairy RNG development strategy. Under Grant’s leadership, Amp Americas has developed some of the largest and most productive dairy biogas-to-renewable gas facilities in the country, received the first dairy waste-to-vehicle fuel pathway certified by the US EPA and by California's Air Resources Board (CARB). During his tenure Amp has prevented 2,000,000 metric tons of greenhouse emissions, equivalent to removing 500,000 cars off the road for one year.
In 2019, Grant led the successful closing of Amp Americas’ second round of private equity funding and the recapitalization of Amp’s original investments delivering nearly three times return for Amp’s private equity investors in less than two years. He subsequently raised $75 million in private equity growth capital to bring the company’s unique expertise and capability in RNG development, operations, services and marketing to other biogas producers, fleets, and their capital partners. Amp Americas now operates facilities on 12 dairies, processing waste from nearly 100,000 cows.
Prior to joining Amp Americas, Grant was an Associate Principal at McKinsey & Company in the Global Energy and Materials and Corporate Finance practices where he served clients in strategy and operations. Before McKinsey, Grant served in commercial and finance roles with Enron and the Enron bankruptcy estate.
Grant earned his MBA from the University of Chicago’s Booth School of Business, graduating with high honors. He also holds a MPA and BBA, Honors from the University of Texas at Austin.

ABOUT US
Bombard Renewable Energy was founded in 2004 to meet Nevada’s growing demand for clean, consistent and reliable energy. A division of Bombard Electric LLC, Bombard Renewable has installed more than 800 MW of solar photovoltaic systems across Nevada.
Our team of professional designers and installers provides exceptional solutions for renewable projects of all sizes and scopes.
Bombard Electric has been serving Nevada and the surrounding region since 1982. It is the area’s premier provider of IBEW Local #357 electrical construction services.
WHAT WE DO
We build solar power systems that use solar photovoltaic (PV), wind energy and concentrated PV, also known as CSPV.
Our professional designers and installers are experienced in providing exceptional solutions for all sorts of challenges.
We believe in providing a safe work environment for our employees and the safest possible electrical systems for our customers.
We believe that trust is at the core of every relationship. We build every project with the goal of working with our customers again in the future.
We believe that the one constant in the world is change. We strive to improve efficiency through innovation, without opposing the principles our experience is built upon.
Bombard Renewable Energy is an Everus company. Everus Construction Group is an industry-leading construction services provider, safely Building America’s Future™ through innovation and expertise.
Everus operates across the United States, providing electrical and mechanical, and transmission and distribution services.
As part of a company with nationwide operations and a highly skilled workforce, Bombard Renewable Energy has deep expertise, a proven execution playbook and readily available additional resources. Our local, market-leading brand, backed by a national platform, ensures we provide the best possible outcomes for our customers.
OUR MISSION
We believe the construction industry has a responsibility to the communities it serves to provide the safest, most reliable buildings and infrastructure possible. Bombard succeeds by developing relationships and associations built on trust and common goals. We deliver the highest value to our customers by implementing innovative methods, while using our vast experience and the highest quality materials.

Utility Scale Solar solutions
Utility-scale solar projects in Nevada have been providing clean, consistent and reliable energy for decades.
We’ve designed and constructed utility-scale solar projects that:

- Provide reliable energy off the grid.

- Offer renewable energy options to utility company customers.
- Power water treatment facilities.
CASE STUDY 1
Valley Electric Association Community Solar
- AP Systems 2016 Project of the Year
- Solar Builder Magazine’s Editor’s Choice Award
Bombard Renewable Energy and project owner Valley Electric Association worked closely with U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service to protect the endangered Mojave Desert Tortoises discovered at the project site.
Unique design and construction elements were used to reduce the impact on the tortoises and vegetation.
- We did not grade or level the project site as we normally would before construction. This helped preserve vegetation the tortoises eat and the tortoises’ habitat.
- We used a versatile racking system that allowed the panels to be installed on uneven terrain.
- The ground-mounted solar panels were installed at a height of 48 inches to allow room for vegetation and tortoises. Typically, panels are installed at a height of 18 to 24 inches.
- One-foot-wide tortoise access points were created along the fence line to allow them to pass through the site.
- An expansive, temporary habitat was created to house the tortoises during construction. They were returned to the site after construction was complete.

INFLATION REDUCTION ACT OF 2022
how the INFLATION REDUCTION ACT BENEFITS COMMERCIAL SOLAR AND BATTERY STORAGE
The Inflation Reduction Act was signed into law in August 2022. The following summary highlights some of the major benefits regarding commercial solar and battery storage projects.
Bombard Renewable Energy qualifies as a union contractor for the prevailing wage and apprentice requirements in this act.
Significant changes and opportunities
- Non-profit organizations can sell Investment Tax Credits to other organizations with tax appetites.
- Renewable Energy Credits (RECs) are being sold and traded more than ever. They can fetch as much as $10 per credit (1 MWh=1 REC) in Nevada and even more nationally.
Investment Tax Credit
- Up-front credit given to an organization with a legitimate tax appetite to offset tax burden.
- Adjusted from 26% to 30%.
- Extended to 2032.
- Projects over 1MW AC must follow prevailing wage and apprentice standards to take advantage of full 30%.
- Cannot be combined with Production Tax Credit.
Production Tax Credit
- Recognized by IRS over a 10-year period.
- Adjusted every year to accommodate for inflation.
- Currently worth 2.6 cents (at 100% PTC) per kilowatt hour produced by solar.
- Must follow prevailing wage and apprentice standards to maximize this incentive.
- Cannot be combined with Investment Tax Credit.
Commercial battery storage
- 30% Investment Tax Credit is available for projects from 5 kWh to less than 1 MW AC output.
- Must follow prevailing wage and apprentice standards.
Adders*
- Domestic Content Bonus Credit. An additional 10% adder will be applied if the project uses steel/iron produced 100% in the USA. In addition, the remaining products used need to be at least 40% produced in the USA.
- Energy Community Bonus Credit. Brownfield sites. Refer to the EPA definition of brownfield site.
- Low-Income Communities. Projects in low-income communities or on American Indian land and qualified low-income residential building projects qualify for an additional 10% adder.

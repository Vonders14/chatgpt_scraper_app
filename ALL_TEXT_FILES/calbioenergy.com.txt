We Turn Waste into Fuel and Power
At CalBio, we partner with dairy families to capture methane-rich biogas in a dairy digester. We then harness this gas to produce energy – powering vehicles and generating electricity – and advance energy independence. Trucks and buses use our methane, and we generate electricity to power dairy farms and residential and commercial customers of the utility.
Powering the Future
At CalBio, we’re turning methane from dairy farms into a powerful, American energy source. We aim to double the gas we capture and energy we produce over the next three years.
In addition to capturing methane and producing energy, our digester projects also capture hydrogen sulfide. The result is improved local air quality benefiting the community. We then convert the hydrogen sulfide into elemental sulfur, which serves as a beneficial soil amendment for the dairy farm.
Together with family farmers we're producing energy for the future.

Your Ideal Development Partner
CalBio is the ideal partner for dairy farms looking to convert waste into energy while also generating a new revenue stream.

a cleaner future
Our Mission
At CalBio, we’re turning methane from dairy farms into an American energy source. By capturing the biogas at the dairy (which is roughly 65% methane), we prevent nearly 2 million metric tons of CO2e from being released each year. We aim to double that in the next three years and double the energy we produce.
Another key way we're making a difference is by utilizing the methane as vehicle fuel. This shift from diesel to natural gas greatly reduces NOx, improving local air quality.
Together with family farms we’re producing energy for the future.

TURN YOUR WASTE
INTO ENERGY
CalBio captures methane-rich biogas at a dairy farm and turns it into fuel and power.
We partner with dairy farmers and invest in long-term solutions.
Since 2006, CalBio has designed, developed, operated, and financed digester projects. We use our expertise to turn the methane-rich biogas generated from dairy waste into energy. We build successful projects and deliver long-term value for farmers and partners.
Mastering Key Project Components
With almost 20 years of experience, we have industry-leading expertise to handle every stage of the development process – financing, engineering, construction, operations, and revenue generation.
Biogas Production
We capture the biogas generated from dairy waste and use the methane content as vehicle fuel and to generate electricity.
Maximizing Value
We work closely with federal and state agencies, utilizing available programs to make a project economically secure.
Tailored Solutions for Dairy Farmers
Every family operation is unique, and we design systems to fit their specific needs based on our many years of experience. Our solutions streamline operations, save time and money, and position dairy farm partners for long-term success.
1
Waste Collection
Manure is flushed into collection areas, where solids are separated before entering the digester—integrating seamlessly into daily dairy operations.
2
Biogas Capture
Inside the digester, microorganisms break down organic matter, producing methane-rich biogas. Instead of escaping into the atmosphere, it's captured for energy production.
3
Renewable Natural Gas
Captured methane is converted into Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) to power trucks, buses, and other vehicles or to generate electricity. The electricity can be sold to a utility, used to power the dairy farm, or used to power data centers.
4
Effluent Use
Digested effluent water is recycled as flush water, keeping lanes cleaner, improving hygiene, and reducing slips. The process also converts over 60% of organic nitrogen into a more plant-available form, enhancing crop yields and lowering fertilizer costs.
Dairy Biogas,
High-impact Uses
Fueling Transportation
Biogas is upgraded to RNG and powers trucks and buses resulting in a locally produced fuel.
Grid-Ready Electricity
Biogas is used to generate electricity that can be sold or used onsite.
Reliable,
On-Demand Energy
Dairy farm biogas provides consistent, flexible energy, delivering steady power when needed. The biogas can be stored up to four days, allowing for energy to be dispatched during peak demand periods.
Endless Possibilities
CalBio continues to explore new end-use opportunities for our biogas, including potential applications in maritime fuel, sustainable aviation fuel, power for data centers, and more.

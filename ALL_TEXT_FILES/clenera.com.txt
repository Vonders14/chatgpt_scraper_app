Since 2013, Clēnera has delivered reliable, safe, clean energy to communities across America. By building better projects, we are improving local economies and empowering more and better investments where our projects operate. We are dedicated to build better so we can give more. URL: https://clenera.com/projects/apex

This project is Clēnera's first operational project in Montana. The clean power produced from this site is sold to Northwestern Energy. The ribbon cutting celebration was joined by Governor Greg Gianforte, local elected officials, and the project team.
This solar farm uses sheep and llamas to help with vegetation management. Clēnera has an agreement with a local rancher to bring in some sheep during the spring lambing season and they are guarded by llamas. The sheep get to raise the lambs within the farm's fences while improving the overall soil conditions.

Yes. Herd animals, likely sheep, will also be used for vegetation management. The vegetative mix will include native herbaceous hedgerows, grasses, clover, and California poppy to promote pollinator-friendly habitat.
Who is receiving the energy produced at this project?
Clēnera has entered into a power purchase agreement with the Sacramento Municipal Utility District (SMUD) to buy the clean energy produced at this solar farm.
Who owns the land for this project?
Clēnera is leasing the land for this project from private property owners and SMUD.

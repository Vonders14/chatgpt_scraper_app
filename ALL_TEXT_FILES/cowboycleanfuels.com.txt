Our Team & Mission
Our Story. Our Purpose. Our Team.
Commercializing a new, world-changing technology
Cowboy Clean Fuels’ roots trace back to the Center for Biogenic Natural Gas Research at the University of Wyoming, where Dr. Michael Urynowicz began his work to understand and enhance the unique geological and microbiological conditions in the Powder River Basin of Wyoming and Montana, which resulted in a vast biogenic coalbed methane resource. Over more than a decade, his and the Center’s work yielded multiple patents surrounding the novel approach to transform and leverage this unique resource into a large-scale Renewable Natural Gas and Carbon Removal and Sequestration opportunity. The patents and decade’s worth of learnings and know-how have formed the basis for Cowboy Clean Fuels, which was founded to commercialize the UW technology in 2020. CCF is now poised to bring the technology to market, consistent with the “Biomass with Carbon Removal and Storage, plus Renewable Natural Gas” or “BiCRS+RNG” Methodology.
While at the University of Wyoming, Dr. Michael Urynowicz, conducted extensive research at the Center for Biogenic Natural Gas Research and patented several technologies that form the foundation of the Cowboy process. This innovative process leverages natural systems and repurposes idle, existing natural gas infrastructure to permanently remove carbon from the atmosphere and produce carbon-negative natural gas.
Our goal is to play a leading role in the energy transition by producing carbon-negative renewable natural gas at scale.
Cowboy Clean Fuels upholds core values of innovation, sustainability, and leadership while maintaining and prioritizing a spirit of partnership in all stakeholder relationships.
Cowboy Clean Fuels is a reliable and trustworthy supplier of carbon-negative RNG and durable CDR credits to our customers.

Company Projects
Project 1 — The Triangle Unit
Innovative Renewable Energy and Carbon Sequestration Project:
Cowboy Clean Fuels (CCF) is developing the "Triangle Unit Renewable Energy and Carbon Capture and Storage Project" (TRECCS) in Wyoming’s Powder River Basin, turning coalbed methane (CBM) resources into low-carbon renewable natural gas (RNG) while sequestering carbon dioxide (CO2) in coal seams.
Economic and Job Benefits:
The project will support Wyoming's energy and agricultural sectors, creating 221 direct and indirect jobs in 2025, with $8.8 million in tax revenues and contributing over $36 million to Wyoming's GDP annually once fully operational.
Sugar Beet Residue Products as Feedstock:
CCF uses residue products from sugar beet refining, such as molasses, injecting them into deep coal formations where natural methanogenic organisms convert them into methane (CH4) and CO2, with the CO2 permanently sequestered in the coal.
Wyoming's Leadership in Clean Energy:
CCF's project aligns with Wyoming’s role in decarbonizing the West, leveraging the state's natural resources, infrastructure, and workforce to pioneer renewable energy and carbon capture solutions.
Carbon Sequestration and Renewable Gas Production:
Once fully operational, the Triangle Unit project is expected to produce 0.7 billion cubic feet of RNG annually and sequester around 180,000 metric tons of CO2 per year.
Partnership with the University of Wyoming:
The project is supported by research from the University of Wyoming (UW) and its School of Energy Resources, and CCF will pay several million dollars annually in technology license fees to UW, further boosting Wyoming's innovation ecosystem and its flagship educational institution.

URL: https://cowboycleanfuels.com/company-news/cowboy-clean-fuels-unveils-industry-first-bicrsrng-methodology-to-advance-carbon-removal-and-renewable-energy/

Denver, CO – Monday, April 28th, 2025 – Cowboy Clean Fuels (CCF), a leading innovator in renewable energy and carbon removal technologies, has announced the completion and release of its groundbreaking Biomass Carbon Removal and Storage, plus Renewable Natural Gas (BiCRS+RNG) methodology.
This industry-first framework establishes a rigorous approach for measuring, monitoring, reporting, and verifying a novel form of carbon removal, which combines biogenic methane production with permanent CO₂ sequestration in geologic formations utilizing legacy energy infrastructure.
A Breakthrough in Carbon Removal and Low-Carbon Energy Production
The BiCRS+RNG methodology introduces a meticulous, verifiable pathway to high-integrity carbon removal crediting, ensuring that carbon removal projects meet the highest industry standards. Built on CCF’s proprietary process, the methodology enables:
- Injection of biomass-derived feedstocks into geologic formations to support microbial conversion to CO₂ and biomethane.
- Production of biomethane at the surface to be delivered to end users as a clean, low-carbon renewable natural gas (RNG).
- Permanent sequestration of CO₂ within the formation through a highly durable adsorption process, creating long-term and scalable geologic carbon storage.
This innovative carbon removal approach unlocks new opportunities for climate-conscious industries to support decarbonization efforts while leveraging existing energy infrastructure for low-carbon fuel production.
Setting a New Standard for Carbon Accounting
EcoEngineers (Eco), a clean energy consulting, auditing, and advisory firm recently acquired by leading global assurance partner LRQA, collaborated with CCF in the development of this methodology.
Eco helped CCF ensure that the BiCRS+RNG methodology adheres to the highest standards of scientific rigor, transparency and sustainability, ensuring projects developed using this approach will be in compliance with international standard ISO 14064-2:2019.
This framework provides RNG buyers, carbon dioxide removal (CDR) credit purchasers, investors, and project developers with a proven and market-ready solution for addressing climate goals while ensuring:
- Robust measurement, reporting and verification (MRV) compliance
- Full alignment with U.S. Environmental Protection Agency and Wyoming Department of Environmental Quality Underground Injection Control (UIC) permit requirements
- Advanced gas composition tracking and reservoir integrity monitoring for long-term environmental safety
- Consistency with standards for high-quality carbon dioxide removal, including durability, leakage and co-benefits
Ryan Waddington, Chairman and CEO of Cowboy Clean Fuels, said: “The completion of the BiCRS+RNG methodology represents a major milestone in the evolution of engineered carbon removal and renewable energy production. This methodology enables shovel-ready projects to move forward with confidence, allowing our capital partners to invest with certainty in the Powder River Basin and beyond.”
Driving Market Transformation
By combining carbon removal with RNG production, the BiCRS+RNG methodology revolutionizes how legacy energy assets are repurposed for sustainable use. Key benefits include:
- Unlocking new economic opportunities by transforming uneconomic, end-of-life fossil energy infrastructure into productive, revenue-generating clean energy assets
- Providing a scalable and verifiable carbon removal framework that meets stringent environmental and regulatory requirements
- Delivering high-integrity carbon credits backed by independent verification and industry-leading MRV compliance
- Empowering companies with a trusted pathway to net-zero goals, allowing for direct participation in carbon markets and decarbonization initiatives
David LaGreca, Managing Director of Carbon Markets at EcoEngineers, added: “Eco is helping bring yet another innovative engineered carbon removal solution to market with this novel methodology. Project developers, like Cowboy Clean Fuels, who deploy projects compliant with BiCRS+RNG can significantly and simultaneously address the need for rapid scaling of carbon removal activities while also supporting the increased production of renewable energy.”
About Cowboy Clean Fuels
Cowboy Clean Fuels is an advanced climate tech and energy transition company dedicated to permanent CO₂ sequestration and carbon-negative renewable natural gas (RNG) production. By leveraging depleted CBM reservoirs, CCF has pioneered a scalable, verifiable carbon removal process, transforming biomass-derived feedstocks into low-carbon energy solutions. Visit www.cowboycleanfuels.com.
About EcoEngineers
EcoEngineers, an LRQA company, is a consulting, auditing, and advisory firm exclusively focused on the energy transition and decarbonization. Its team of engineers, scientists, auditors, consultants, and researchers operates at the intersection of low-carbon fuel policy, innovative technologies, and the carbon marketplace. URL: https://cowboycleanfuels.com/company-news/cowboy-clean-fuels-and-mercuria-announce-landmark-offtake-agreement-for-carbon-negative-renewable-natural-gas/

Gillette, WY — Cowboy Clean Fuels (CCF), a Wyoming-based climate tech and energy transition company, today announced a groundbreaking offtake agreement with Mercuria, one of the world’s leading independent energy and commodity trading groups. This partnership marks the first commercial agreement for the sale of Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) generated under CCF’s pioneering BiCRS+RNG Methodology at the company’s Triangle Unit Project near Gillette, Wyoming.
Under the agreement, Mercuria will purchase the Environmental Attributes (EAs) associated with Cowboy’s carbon-negative RNG and distribute them through its global network of energy transition customers. The agreement covers an initial term of 10 years and represents the offtake of 5 million MMBTU equivalent EAs.
“Cowboy is excited to partner with Mercuria, who we consider the global leader in energy and environmental commodities,” said Ryan Waddington, CEO of Cowboy Clean Fuels. “This landmark agreement supporting offtake from our first commercial project is a game-changer for our company, and we look forward to working with Mercuria for years and years to come.”
Founded in 2020, Cowboy Clean Fuels converts readily available agricultural byproducts into carbon dioxide and renewable methane using a natural biogenic process in deep geologic coal formations, leveraging existing Coal Bed Methane (CBM) infrastructure in the Powder River Basin. The result is a carbon-negative fuel with a verified zero-to-negative Carbon Intensity—offering a scalable solution for hard-to-decarbonize sectors such as manufacturing, clean transportation, and AI-powered data centers.
Mercuria, headquartered in Geneva and operating globally since 2004, is known for its leadership in advancing the energy transition. With a dedicated team of environmental products specialists, Mercuria provides tailored decarbonization solutions to help companies achieve their net zero goals.
“Mercuria has been looking for the next generation of RNG projects and technology to support the next phase of growth in the global RNG market,” said Harrison Clay, Head of Biogas, Mercuria. “When we found Cowboy, we immediately recognized they had the technology and growth potential necessary to carve out a leadership position in this market. We are thrilled to be their go-to-market partner.”
With additional RNG projects in development across Wyoming’s Powder River Basin and beyond, Cowboy Clean Fuels is positioned to become a leading supplier of carbon-neutral and carbon-negative fuels at scale.
About Cowboy Clean Fuels
Cowboy Clean Fuels is an advanced climate tech company leveraging coal bed methane infrastructure to produce carbon-negative Renewable Natural Gas and permanently sequester atmospheric CO₂. Founded in 2020, the company is headquartered in Wyoming’s Powder River Basin.
About Mercuria
Mercuria is one of the world’s largest integrated independent energy and commodity trading companies. With a commitment to the energy transition, Mercuria provides comprehensive solutions across environmental commodities, including RNG, biofuels, and carbon credits.

Denver, CO / Gillette, WY — Cowboy Clean Fuels (CCF), a leader in carbon-negative renewable natural gas (RNG), and Mangrove Systems, a pioneer in digital measurement, reporting, and verification (dMRV) solutions , have announced a strategic collaboration to deploy Digital Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV) technology across Cowboy’s commercial carbon removal and RNG operations in Wyoming’s Powder River Basin.
This partnership marks a pivotal step toward scaling high-integrity, verifiable carbon credits at a time when buyers and investors are demanding greater transparency and accountability in the voluntary carbon market.
Cowboy Clean Fuels, building on its recently published BiCRS+RNG methodology and completed 10-year RNG offtake agreement with Mercuria, continues to scale operations to commercial deployment. By integrating and implementing Mangrove’s dMRV platform at Cowboy’s first project, CCF will now be able to automate operational and environmental data collection, accelerate RNG and CDR credit issuance, and enhance the credibility of its carbon removal operations.
“This partnership sets a new bar for integrity in the carbon market,” said Ryan Waddington, CEO – Cowboy Clean Fuels. “By embedding digital MRV into our operations, we can ensure that every molecule of RNG and every ton of CO₂ we remove is backed by real-time, verifiable data.”
Mangrove’s dMRV platform provides continuous, audit-ready tracking of environmental performance aligned with standards such as ISO 14064-2. Its key benefits include:
- Accuracy & Credibility – Automated data streams reduce subjectivity and error
- Accelerated Credit Issuance – Verification timelines are shortened, accelerating credit availability.
- Scalability – Seamlessly supports multi-site, high-volume project portfolios.
- Data Transparency – Real-time audit trails build confidence with buyers, investors, and regulators.
- Future-Readiness – Aligns with evolving digital registries and compliance frameworks.
“We’re thrilled to support Cowboy’s mission to scale carbon-negative RNG with the transparency of today’s market demands,” said Evan Little, COO – Mangrove Systems . “ Digital MRV turns raw operational data into verifiable, registry-ready credits — so there’s confidence the climate impact is real, permanent, and transparent. ”
As carbon markets mature, CCF’s integration of dMRV will deliver meaningful advantages to credit purchasers and RNG offtakers with faster credit issuance cycles , verified, real-time carbon removal data , enhanced trust and reduced transaction risk, in addition to long-term alignment with compliance-grade standards.
With this collaboration, Cowboy Clean Fuels is uniquely positioned to offer high-quality, digitally verified carbon credits and low-carbon fuels at scale, backed by the tools and infrastructure that define the future of carbon markets.

At Cowboy Clean Fuels, we are pioneering a revolutionary approach to durable carbon removal and geologic sequestration.

Revolutionizing Renewable Gas Production
What sets our Renewable Natural Gas apart
At Cowboy Clean Fuels, we are pioneering a revolutionary approach to producing Renewable Natural Gas (RNG).
Our unique process starts with the injection of organic biomass into coal bed methane (CBM) wells where existing microorganisms convert it into sustainable energy and carbon dioxide. The coal seam then selectively captures and permanently stores the carbon dioxide, ensuring it will never be released to the atmosphere.

Our innovative Biomass Carbon Removal and Storage Plus Renewable Natural Gas (BiCRS+RNG) methodology leverages coal bed methane (CBM) reservoirs as natural bioreactors for the conversion of biomass into renewable natural gas (RNG) and the permanent sequestration of carbon dioxide (CO2).
Utilizing indigenous microorganisms, the injected biomass undergoes anaerobic digestion within the coal reservoirs, generating RNG that can be produced along with CO2 that is permanently sequestered and absorbed onto the coal surfaces.
This process ensures anet reduction of greenhouse gases (GHGs) while creating a viable source of renewable energy.
The methodology is rigorously designed to meet the highest standards of carbon accounting, as defined by ISO 14064-2:2019. It provides clear guidelines for project development on site selection, feedstock characterization, and the quantification of net carbon dioxide removal (CDR) and emissions reductions.
Our approach emphasizes the importance of permanence and additionality, ensuring that carbon is stored safely for over 100 years and that projects yield genuine environmental benefits beyond business-as-usual scenarios.
Detailed monitoring, reporting, and verification (MRV) protocols are included to guarantee transparency and accuracy throughout the project lifecycle.
This methodology offers a unique opportunity for partners to engage in cutting-edge carbon removal projects that not only contribute to net-zero goals but also produce valuable renewable natural gas.

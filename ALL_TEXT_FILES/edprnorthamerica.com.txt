North America
EDP Renewables North America
Powering America’s energy future
EDPR NA is a leading developer, owner, and operator of renewable energy projects across North America. Our team is dedicated to delivering homegrown energy solutions that create lasting value for the communities we serve. EDPR NA is a division of EDP Renewables and is headquartered in Houston, Texas.
Our technologies
Wind energy
61 wind farms
Solar energy
29 solar parks
Energy storage
Robust storage and hybridization pipeline
Distributed generation
540+ active sites
Who we are
EDPR NA serves communities and businesses with more than 12,100 megawatts of reliable, clean energy across the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Our wind, solar, and energy storage facilities power the equivalent of more than 3.3 million homes each year.
Content card grid
About us
We develop, own, and operate renewable energy projects that generate reliable and affordable energy to American communities.
Our impact
Our renewable energy projects strengthen North America's diverse economic landscapes.
Projects
EDPR NA's project portfolio is bolstering the power grid, strengthening local economies, and meeting the energy demand.
Our community-driven approach
From the early development phases, through construction, and throughout a project’s operational life, we are committed to engaging with local residents and being an active community member.
Who we serve
Utilities
We are committed to working with public and private utilities across North America to drive a resilient energy future.
Landowners
Building meaningful relationships with landowners and project communities is at the core of our business.
Renewable energy buyers
We work with a variety of customers to deliver innovative solutions that meet their unique energy needs.
Become a pioneer in the new energy era
With more than 1,000 employees across the continent, our success as a leading renewable energy provider comes from our company's greatest asset: its people.
Stay updated with our latest news

URL: https://icarusrt.com/2024/08/21/icarus-rt-partners-with-epri-and-con-edison-to-drive-solar-innovation/

Icarus RT is excited to announce its collaboration with the Electric Power Research Institute (EPRI) and Con Edison to enhance the future of hybrid solar plus storage systems. As solar technology continues to advance, this partnership aims to optimize solar energy efficiency, reduce CO2 emissions, and tackle energy storage challenges. By leveraging Icarus RT’s cutting-edge hybrid photovoltaic/thermal (PV/T) technology, this partnership promises to deliver more sustainable and cost-effective energy solutions for commercial and industrial sectors.
EPRI’s commitment to cleaner, reliable power aligns with Icarus RT’s mission to revolutionize solar energy through their unique technology, which increases energy output by 12-18% and captures thermal energy for storage. The collaboration focuses on deploying innovative solutions to meet energy demand and address grid reliability challenges. Con Edison’s active role in this partnership ensures that the new technology is tested in real-world conditions, setting the stage for wide-scale adoption.
This project builds on the California Duck Curve challenge, where solar energy production often misaligns with peak demand. Icarus’ hybrid solution mitigates this issue by storing solar energy for use during high-demand periods, thus lowering utility costs and increasing energy resilience. The joint efforts will pave the way for cleaner, more efficient power across multiple sectors.
For more on this exciting collaboration, read EPRI’s official press release here.

With Quartet, you can now maximize your energy efficiency and realize:
Introducing
Quartet by Icarus RT
The Quartet System enhances photovoltaic (PV) panels’ efficiency and integrates thermal energy storage for superior performance.
Industries
The Icarus Quartet serves several key markets:
Performance
Optimize Your Solar Energy.
More Energy per Panel
Quartet doubles the energy output of each solar panel, maximizing the efficiency and impact of your solar investment.
Enhanced Solar Performance
A significant boost in solar power generation with a 12-18% increase in photovoltaic output, thanks to our advanced panel cooling technology.
Faster Average ROI
A faster return on investment, which delivers a payback period of just 3.2 years, compared to the typical 6-7 years for standard solar plus storage solutions.
Intellectual Property
Proprietary Heat Extractor
Our advanced heat extractors attach seamlessly to existing PV panels, reducing surface temperatures by up to 18°C, significantly boosting power output by 12-18%. This technology not only enhances electrical efficiency but also captures waste heat, which is then stored for later use, providing a dual benefit of energy generation and thermal storage. Click here to read the patent.
Hybrid PV/T Conversion
Our technology converts standard solar PV panels into hybrid PV/thermal systems, maximizing energy capture and efficiency.
Enhanced Power & Hot Water Generation
Increase your solar panel power by 12-18% while simultaneously generating hot water, all from the same system.
Smart Control System that manages it all.
Icarus RT’s proprietary control software optimizes energy use by automatically managing when to store, sell, or use the captured thermal energy. This intelligent system maximizes your energy savings by monitoring utility rates, solar output, and demand in real-time. By reducing peak demand charges and ensuring optimal energy use, customers experience a significantly faster ROI—as low as 3 years compared to traditional systems. This intelligent energy management delivers better performance and greater financial benefits, all while simplifying your solar energy operations.
Product Specifications
Discover how Icarus RT’s hybrid PV-Thermal system delivers more energy per square foot, faster ROI, and dual-output performance. Download the spec sheet to explore system details, performance data, and integration options for your next project.
Frequently Asked Questions
Can the Icarus system be retrofitted to existing PV installations?
Yes, the Icarus Quartet System is designed to be easily retrofitted to existing PV installations, enhancing their efficiency without the need for a complete system overhaul.
What kind of maintenance does the Icarus system require?
The Icarus system requires minimal maintenance. Regular inspections and occasional servicing of the heat extractor and thermal storage components will ensure optimal performance.
Is the Icarus system compatible with all PV panels?
Our heat extractors are designed to fit most off-the-shelf 300W PV panels. We strive for compatibility with all major brands, but specific assessments can be conducted if needed.
What is the expected lifetime of the Icarus Quartet System?
The Icarus system is built to last, with an expected operational lifetime of 20 years.
How does the Icarus system help with peak energy demand?
The system stores thermal energy during the day and can provide power or hot water on demand, reducing peak energy costs and easing the load on the grid during high-demand periods.
How does the Icarus Quartet System work?
The Icarus Quartet System cools PV panels using a proprietary heat extractor, increasing their power output by 12-18%. The system also stores the extracted thermal energy, which can be used for hot water or converted into additional electricity on demand.

EPRI’s Incubatenergy Labs (IEL) program has announced that 20 clean energy startup companies have been selected to participate next month in its pitch day, presenting pilot project concepts to energy industry stakeholders. The startups were chosen from more than 200 applicants to demonstrate at EPRI’s Electrification 2024 International Conference & Exposition in Savannah, Ga.
In its sixth year, IEL is built for startups to engage EPRI and energy companies in demonstration projects. The program connects companies leading the advancement of electrification, decarbonization, and grid modernization with energy stakeholders to demonstrate and scale those innovations.
IEL’s 2024 Pitch Day is scheduled March 12-13 in the Innovation Theater at the Electrification 2024 conference. Conference and registration information is available here: Home (electrification2024.com)
“The companies invited to Pitch Days at EPRI Electrification have a unique opportunity to pitch their demonstration concept directly to innovation scouts from an international group of energy providers,” said Dan Killoren, program manager of EPRI’s Global Innovation Hub. “These innovation leaders work with EPRI experts to select demonstration projects that address industry needs and can be executed in a rapid, proof-of-concept manner.”

Icarus RT was proud to be named a winner of the “Ready!” Stage of the Department of Energy’s prestigious American-Made Solar Prize — earning a $50,000 award and advancing to the “Set!” Stage of the competition.
What This Means:
Icarus RT’s innovation has been recognized at the national level for its potential to transform solar energy performance and economics.As a “Set!” Stage competitor, we are now in the running for additional cash awards up to $100,000 and $90,000 in National Lab vouchers to accelerate our product validation and commercial scale-up.Our submitted 5-minute pitch video and letters of support demonstrate growing traction from industry stakeholders and partners.
Why It Matters:
Traditional solar panels waste more than half of their potential energy as heat. Icarus RT’s heat extraction technology converts that lost heat into value — enhancing electricity output, reducing CO₂ emissions, and extending panel lifespan. Our selection in this competitive DOE program affirms the market readiness and technical viability of our solution.This milestone strengthens our visibility and opens new collaboration pathways with national labs, investors, and clean energy leaders.

URL: https://icarusrt.com/2024/08/13/rice-alliance-names-15-startups-to-its-cleantech-accelerator/

clean energy program has announced its third cohort and named the 15 startups that were accepted into to the accelerator.
The Rice Alliance’s Clean Energy Accelerator revealed its 2023 cohort that will be in the 10-week program that kicks of July 25. CEA, a hybrid program based out of the Ion, will wrap up with a Demo Day alongside the 20th Annual Rice Alliance Energy Tech Venture Forum on September 21.
The accelerator, led by Kerri Smith and Matt Peña, provides the cohort with programming, networking, and mentorship from six executives in residence — Nathan Ball, Fatimah Bello, Michael Egan, Michael Evans, Stephen Sims, and Deanna Zhang.
Since the Clean Energy Accelerator launched in 2021, the program has supported 29 ventures that have gone on to raise over $75 million in funding, identified and launched pilots, and created jobs, According to Rice, many of these companies relocated to Houston.
Class 3, which has already raised $23.3 million in funding, hails from four countries and seven states and are addressing a range of energy solutions — from advanced materials, carbon management/capture, energy storage, hydrogen, solar energy, wind energy, and more. They were selected by a screening committee consisting of more than 50 industry experts, investors, energy leaders, and entrepreneurs.
The third class, as announced by Rice Alliance, is as follows:
Mexico City-basedTycho Solutionssupports clean energy project developers by saving time and money during the critical project-siting process.
Ayrton Energy, based in Alberta, Canada,provides hydrogen storage technology that improves hydrogen transport logistics for distributed energy applications.
Headquartered in Massachusetts,Carbix transforms atmospheric carbon dioxide emissions into building materials using proprietary reactor technology.
Houston-based CryoDesalination lowers the carbon footprint and cost of removing salts and heavy metals from water and industrial effluents.
Digital Carbon Bank,based in Alberta, Canada, provides a carbon solution tailored for the energy industry.
Chandler, Arizona-basedEarthEn provides compressed carbon dioxide-based energy storage and artificial intelligence solutions allowing grid owners/operators to be completely renewable.
H Quest Vanguard, from Pittsburgh, provides green hydrogen at a five to 10 times lower cost to users of natural gas to decarbonize industrial heat.
Calgary, Alberta-based Highwood Emissions Management’sSaaS platform allows oil and gas companies to understand their emissions and develop robust plans to reduce them.
Icarus RT,from San Diego, California, improves photovoltaic efficiency while enabling useful heat energy storage.
Los Altos, California-based Khepra has developed a chemical manufacturing platform for the low-cost, sustainable production of agrochemicals.
Binghamton, New York-based Natrion’s electrolyte is a drop-in solid-state battery component that can be rapidly implemented into existing batteries.
Oceanways, based in London, provides low-cost, flexible and scalable zero-emission underwater “virtual pipelines” to energy producers.
Relyion Energy, from Santa Clara, California, is developing battery usage and intelligence solutions with deeper data and insights for retired electric vehicle batteries.
Massachusetts-based Triton Anchor provides a more cost-effective anchoring solution for offshore clean energy with minimal environmental impact.
TROES, from Markham, Ontario, provides a 4-in-1 microgrid solution with integrated hardware and software for a streamlined energy storage experience.

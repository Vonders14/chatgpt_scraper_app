SERVICE & MAINTENANCE
We Represent Your Brand with Integrity & Precision
Central California's Most Reliable Solar Service Subcontractor
If you need a solar service subcontractor for maintenance or warranty calls, KS Electric is here to help. Our team will handle service calls on jobs installed by any contractor, including servicing both residential and commercial solar sites.
WHAT WE DO
Our service team handles:
- Solar panel inspections & diagnostics
- Troubleshooting inverter or wiring issues
- Battery system diagnostics (Tesla, Enphase, Generac, etc.)
- Site assessments for system upgrades
- De-install/Re-installs
- Abandoned projects
- Site transfers
- EV installation
- Small residential electrical hook ups
Benefits of Our Services
Our solar panel cleaning and system monitoring services are designed to protect the ROI of both residential and commercial investments. For commercial sites, this ensures high-output efficiency across larger arrays where dirt accumulation can significantly impact bottom-line energy production.
Enhanced Efficiency
Dirt and debris can greatly reduce your solar panel efficiency. Regular cleaning ensures that your solar panels generate power at their fullest potential.
Early Issue Detection
Proactive monitoring identifies performance drops and hardware faults before they lead to costly downtime or lost energy production.
Prolong System Life Span
Regular maintenance can significantly reduce wear and tear on your solar setup, prolonging its lifespan.
Long-Term Value
Periodic cleaning is key to extending the life of your solar panels, saving you money on repairs and replacements down the line.
Partner-Focused Service Model
Need additional partners for solar service calls? We support solar companies with a $250 truck roll
fee per call, offering white-label or branded technician support.
- Specialized knowledge in solar + battery systems
- Scalable workforce without overhead
- Service coverage across Central California

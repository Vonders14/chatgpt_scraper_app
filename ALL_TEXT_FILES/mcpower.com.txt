How Do Commercial Solar Systems Work?
Why Solar Energy Is Beneficial
Solar energy is clean, renewable, and sustainable, making it a powerful alternative to fossil fuels. It reduces carbon emissions, helps combat climate change, and promotes energy independence by lowering reliance on the grid. Businesses also benefit from significantly lower electricity bills and minimal maintenance needs, as solar panels are highly durable and backed by long warranties.
How Solar Panels Work
Solar panels, made of photovoltaic (PV) cells, capture sunlight and convert it into electricity. Here's a quick breakdown:
Sunlight Collection: PV cells absorb sunlight and excite electrons, generating direct current (DC) electricity.
Inverter Conversion: An inverter then converts DC electricity into alternating current (AC) for business use.
Usage or Storage: Solar energy can be used immediately or stored in batteries for backup power.

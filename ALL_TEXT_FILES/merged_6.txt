URL: https://ugs.solutions/Landfill_Gas_Upgrading

SOLUTIONS
Landfill gas (LFG) presents unique challenges for gas treatment technologies due to its high levels of impurities. Efficient pre-treatment systems are essential to remove VOCs, siloxanes, and other contaminants effectively. At UGS, we combine expertise and experience to design reliable, cost-efficient pre-treatment solutions tailored to the specific needs of each landfill.
Our comprehensive solutions include regenerative TSA systems, adsorption filters, and biological scrubbing systems, ensuring optimal performance and compliance with environmental standards.
One of the key challenges with LFG is its typically high nitrogen (N₂) concentration, which reduces the heating value of biomethane when using conventional technologies. To address this, UGS has developed an innovative two-stage hybrid system that combines membrane and our unique NRU (Nitrogen rejection unit). This advanced design effectively removes CO₂ and N₂ from LFG, delivering a high heating value and achieving industry-leading CH₄ recovery rates.

TECHNOLOGIES
At UGS, we are proud to offer advanced membrane technology for gas separation, designed to meet the evolving needs of the industry. Our membranes exhibit high selectivity and permeability, leveraging the latest advances in materials science to ensure optimal performance.
A state-of-the-art membrane system is used to remove CO2 from the biogas stream. The separation process takes place when biogas flows through the hollow fibers of the membrane modules. Components such as CO2, which permeate quickly, move to the shell side of the membrane module. Meanwhile, most of the slow-permeating CH4 remains in the hollow fiber at high pressure. At the end of the membrane module, the highly concentrated CH4 stream is collected and made available for further use at approximately 12 barg pressure.
Combined with our expertise in process design and system manufacturing, we deliver high-performance solutions in applications such as hydrogen recovery, nitrogen production, and carbon dioxide removal. Our scalable and customizable membrane systems provide cost-effective and energy-efficient solutions across various industries.

URL: https://ugs.solutions/PSA-NRU

TECHNOLOGIES
In the pressure swing adsorption system designed by UGS, pressurized gas flows through the adsorbent vessel. Highly adsorbing components are adsorbed in an adsorption media under pressure, while less adsorbing components are concentrated at the outlet of the adsorption vessel.
Applied to biogas upgrading, CO2 is adsorbed in the media, while CH4 is rejected and concentrated at the outlet of the vessel, producing Biomethane. After saturation of the media, CO2 is desorbed again by reducing the pressure in the vessel down to below 100 mbara, regenerating the media for a new adsorption cycle.
Combined with our expertise in process design and system manufacturing, we deliver high-performance solutions in applications such as hydrogen recovery, nitrogen production, and carbon dioxide removal. Our scalable and customizable membrane systems provide cost-effective and energy-efficient solutions across various industries.

TECHNOLOGIES
Temperature Swing Adsorption (TSA) is a proven technology for removing impurities such as siloxanes, VOCs, CO₂, and moisture from biogas or landfill gas, ensuring high-quality biomethane.
During the adsorption phase, the gas passes through a column filled with adsorbent material (e.g., activated carbon or zeolites) at ambient temperature. Contaminants bind to the adsorbent, allowing purified methane to exit the system. Once the adsorbent reaches saturation, the desorption phase begins. The adsorbent is heated to release the captured impurities, which are safely vented or collected.
After desorption, the adsorbent is cooled and ready for reuse. TSA’s ability to regenerate adsorbents makes it highly cost-effective and efficient. The system operates cyclically, ensuring continuous gas cleaning.
TSA is ideal for biogas upgrading and landfill gas applications, achieving strict purity requirements for grid injection, LNG production, or energy recovery. With its reliable performance and scalability, TSA provides a sustainable solution for transforming raw gas into clean, valuable biomethane.

WHAT WE DO
MPower Direct is the sales arm for Mpower Energy and one of the fastest-growing direct sales firms on the east coast. We build teams of sales consultants to sell 100% renewable energy products to home owners & businesses and help save the planet one step at a time!
Our specialists are trained to help homeowners & businesses understand the benefits of renewable energy and do their part in bettering the environment.
Where we work
Our experienced teams and managers create a supportive environment that rewards determination, reliability and teamwork. Our team philosophy is that a rising tide lifts all boats, and our teams have the cooperation and leadership to help reach your goals.

How we work
MPower is a face-to-face sales and marketing company. Our teams are literally the faces of our company. We speak to people every day, building relationships, closing sales and leveraging technology to facilitate our sales force.

CAREER PATH
MPower gives you the tools to succeed and the environment to take your career to the next level.
For example: In a month you can move from Field Rep to Training Manager, in half a year be a Team Leader and move up to Assistant Manager within a year. Manage your own office shortly after! The opportunity is there for YOU.
Income Glossary:
> Salary: Once hired, every MPower team member receives a base salary.
> Commissions: Earn a set commission on every qualified sale.
> Overrides: Experienced team members train incoming members and earn overrides on their sales.
> Bonuses: Double your minimum quota and earn double your base.
And you don’t have to do it on your own.
Once hired, you’ll receive comprehensive paid training to prepare you for a highly rewarding career. After that, you’ll start with a base Salary and earn Commissions on top of that for every qualified sale. As you move up the ladder and build your own team, you train incoming members and earn overrides on their sales. Additionally, you can earn performance-based bonuses, such as doubling your base salary, and earning weekend bonuses. Are you ready to start earning?

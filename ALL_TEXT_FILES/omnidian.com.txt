WHO AM I REALLY?
I’m a mom to two teenage girls, a labradoodle named Connor, and a revolving cast of pets that keep life interesting. I love to cook (and feed people), clear my head outside, and believe that almost anything can be solved with a long walk and good music.
Professionally, I’m a lifelong field service leader with a passion for building high-performing teams and solving complex operational challenges. I’ve spent my career in the trenches of retail, construction, real estate, and home services—where speed, quality, and a good sense of humor go a long way. WHAT MATTERS MOST TO ME
What matters most to me is leaving things better than I found them—whether that’s a process, a team, a relationship, or a Saturday night dinner. I care deeply about doing meaningful work with good people, and creating an environment where others can thrive and grow.
I believe in showing up with integrity, leading with clarity, and listening more than I speak (though I’ve learned that one over time). At work, I’m passionate about delivering results, but just as passionate about how we get there—collaboratively, with accountability, and a sense of humor intact.
At home, what matters most is my family. My daughters keep me grounded and inspired, and remind me daily of the kind of example I want to set—strong, curious, kind, and a little fearless.

Built specifically to monitor and maintain your system. Omnidian’s unique collaboration between solar experts, software experts, and data scientists is an industry first. The resulting state-of-the-art advances in asset monitoring, diagnostics, and remediation drive unprecedented insights and unparalleled operational efficiencies for our clients.
Multi-phase analytics and proprietary machine learning models that can detect, diagnose, and prioritize the important issues while cutting through all the noise. Our technology can detect hardware, communication, and environmental issues, such as soiling, shading, and snow, including the individual impact of multiple issues.
Our technology platform monitors your system(s) 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. The platform processes every data point as it arrives, executing our entire suite of analytics within seconds. Finally, it has a fault-tolerant design that detects network and machine hiccups (common in the IoT space) and then automatically and gracefully recovers. All of these together means a system you can rely on.
We care about your privacy and will never share or sell your data to third parties. Our software and internal processes are designed to keep your data private and secure—all developed under an internationally recognized information security and cybersecurity program.
With over 380,000 years of asset data, our technology covers hundreds of thousands of assets across the country and gives us unprecedented visibility into the factors that impact long-term system performance (including module manufacturer and micro-climates). We understand and can quantify system and fleet performance, with energy waterfalls and comparisons between actual energy, weather-adjusted energy, and pro-forma budget models.
For system owners, our asset — or portfolio-level — energy guarantee reimburses you for covered energy shortfalls at year-end. Downtime? Bad weather? No problem, we cover your system all year long.

Let’s cut to the chase. Your morning routine involves logging into multiple systems — DAS provider portals, inverter OEM dashboards, battery management systems, and your CMMS — just to get a complete picture of asset performance. Each platform offers a piece of the puzzle, but none deliver the unified visibility you need to make informed operational decisions.
Then battery storage entered the chat.
The value proposition is solid: demand charge management, frequency regulation revenue, and grid services that actually move the needle on IRR. But operationally? You’ve just inherited a whole new category of complexity.
Now you’re coordinating between your solar O&M provider and that BESS specialist you finally found (after three months of searching), managing two sets of OEM warranty protocols that seem designed by committee, and navigating safety requirements that make OSHA look relaxed.
When performance drops, the diagnostic fun really begins. Is it inverter clipping? Battery degradation? A BMS firmware bug? Good luck getting a definitive answer when you’re coordinating across multiple vendors with competing priorities.
One Provider, Actual Answers
This operational fragmentation is exactly what we’re solving with Omnidian’s Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Preventive Maintenance plan and QA/QC service. With these solutions, we’re consolidating your solar + storage operation under one unified provider.
For commercial developers, IPPs, and asset owners managing portfolios where every basis point of availability matters, this means real operational simplification:
Unified Service Standards
Our BESS PM plan delivers annual preventive maintenance that satisfies OEM warranty requirements while maintaining consistent safety protocols across your entire portfolio. We deploy our vetted field service technician armed with standardized procedures and documentation. Translation: every service report follows the same format, covers the same critical systems, and delivers the actionable data you actually need for asset management decisions.
Warranty Peace-of-Mind
One missed PM cycle and you’re looking at a voided warranty faster than you can say “thermal runaway.” Our team submits initial warranty claim information if needed, but ultimately we help you mitigate these claims by ensuring regular PM is scheduled and completed on time. Your team stays focused on portfolio optimization instead of worrying about losing your warranty.
Deep Technical Expertise
The learning curve on commercial BESS operations is steep, and training budgets are finite. Instead of building internal expertise from scratch, leverage our dedicated solar and storage engineering team. We’re already neck-deep in thermal management protocols, battery analytics, and grid interconnection requirements, so your operations team doesn’t have to be.
QA/QC Support
EPRI reports 72% of all BESS failures happen during construction, commissioning, or within the first two years of operation. Our QA/QC service can help identify potential issues as early as possible before or after handoff, and provide guidance on remediation. Ultimately, we aim to keep your BESS system in the 28% of safe, operational assets so you realize your energy and financial benefits as soon as possible.
The Bottom Line
Time to retire the vendor juggling act. We’re eliminating the operational overhead of managing fragmented solar and storage systems. With Omnidian as your partner, you get unified reporting visibility, standardized maintenance protocols, and a single point of accountability for your renewable energy infrastructure.
Our BESS PM plan is available immediately for both integrated solar + storage projects and standalone battery installations.

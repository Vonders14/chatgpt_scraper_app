Palmetto’s unmatched Energy Intelligence software simulates the impact of any home upgrade on a customer’s energy bill. First, we leverage our massive proprietary dataset to build a digital twin of the home, exposing more than 60 detailed attributes such as details regarding the building’s roof, windows, insulation, HVAC systems, major appliances, and more. Next, we calculate how much energy and money a household could save through the home upgrades you offer. Together, this understanding of the current state of the home and its efficient potential provides a full picture of who is most likely to convert, and sizes potential revenue for each lead.

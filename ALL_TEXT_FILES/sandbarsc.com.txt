Your bay area solar contractors
Sandbar Solar & Electric
Trusted solar and electric contractors
Sandbar Solar & Electric helps homeowners, businesses, and organizations save money and the environment through the efficient installation of solar power systems.
Founded in Santa Cruz in 2004, our large, experienced team of trusted solar experts provides high-quality design, integration, and installation services for both residential and commercial projects.
Founded in 2004
Sandbar Solar and Electric was founded in 2004 by Scott Laskey. Scott set out to create a team of industry professionals made up of seasoned commercial electricians, project managers, and construction experts.
As a result, the team of experts anchoring the Sandbar team has become one of the industry’s most highly regarded solar companies in California. Sandbar provides comprehensive construction and Operation and Maintenance services to the solar and electrical contracting and solar industries.
Santa Cruz Solar & Electric
Why Choose Sandbar?
Save With Local Solar Experts
We’re local and proud of it. From our Santa Cruz base, Sandbar Solar & Electric sends out crews every day to install solar energy systems in Silicon Valley, Scotts Valley, Capitola, Soquel, Aptos, Watsonville, Salinas, Monterey, and of course, here in beautiful Santa Cruz.
With Sandbar Solar & Electric, you know that everyone helping you go solar — project managers, engineers, electricians, and installers — are Santa Cruz locals themselves. Benefit from our experience on thousands of commercial and residential solar projects: Harness your local solar power and save thousands of dollars on electricity bills.
Benefit from a Large, Experienced Crew
Over the past 20 years, Sandbar has experienced exponential growth, rapidly becoming one of the largest solar installers in Santa Cruz County. We’re creating new jobs every year and stimulating the local economy. With more than 60 full-time employees, we provide top-notch service at an outstanding value. Our pricing is especially competitive since we use high-quality solar products.
As a larger-scale operation, we’ll able to keep promises of quick, efficient installation. Our team holds expertise in every conceivable solar energy area, so we don’t need to bring in extra assistance — and projects rarely get delayed.
Go Solar for Residential or Commercial
With customer satisfaction as our primary goal, we’ve helped thousands of homeowners and businesses go solar. Each installation is a customized solution designed for a particular customer. And we don’t shy away from the difficult jobs that others won’t try.
For homeowners, we deliver an efficient, cost-effective process that causes little interference to your family life. We first make sure your situation is right for solar, and help you find the best financing options. For commercial, nonprofit, and government properties, we have an unrivaled reputation for installing the largest, most advanced systems in the Santa Cruz area, including NHS Skateboard, Plantronics, the Wrigley building, Santa Cruz High, and more.
Enjoy a Smooth Process from Start to Finish
The first time you speak with the friendly, knowledgeable Sandbar staff, you will notice the difference. No hard sell, no confusing language. Since we know solar options inside and out, we won’t force one type of system or one set of materials on you. We explain choices — including financing options — and provide an easy-to-understand proposal.
A project manager will explain exactly what we will do and when we will do it. We make every effort to lessen the impact on your home or business. Our efficient team can often get in and out in days. And once the system is installed, we show you how to monitor your solar savings — and we provide ongoing maintenance and support.
Local Community Events Powered by the Sun
At Sandbar Solar & Electric, we’re active sponsors and participants in community activities and causes throughout Santa Cruz County and beyond. The Sandbar Solar Trailer powers local festivals, concerts, races, street fairs, sports tournaments, beach clean-ups, and more. And we make donations — time and resources — to local charities, schools, and sports teams, and we installed a free solar system for the Santa Cruz Surfing Museum’s lighthouse.

RESIDENTIAL Energy Storage Systems
Enhance your home’s energy resilience with Sandbar Solar & Electric’s advanced energy storage solutions. Our expertly designed solar battery backup systems store excess energy from your solar panels, ensuring reliable power during outages and reducing reliance on the utility grid. Discover how we can help you achieve greater energy independence and savings.
Comprehensive Energy Needs Assessment
Assessing Your Energy Needs
At Sandbar Solar & Electric, our journey begins with a thorough assessment of your home’s energy consumption and backup requirements. We analyze your energy usage patterns, identify critical circuits for backup, and explore how solar batteries can enhance your energy efficiency. This foundational step allows us to tailor a storage solution that perfectly aligns with your needs and maximizes your savings.
Energy Analysis: Detailed evaluation of your energy usage and backup needs.
Critical Circuit Identification: Pinpointing essential circuits for uninterrupted power.
Custom Recommendations: Tailored solutions to optimize energy storage and savings.
Custom Design for Efficient Energy Storage
Designing Your Solar Battery System
Our expert team creates a bespoke design for your solar battery system, ensuring seamless integration with your existing solar panels. We focus on designing dedicated “Critical Loads Subpanels” to keep essential circuits powered during outages and selecting the most suitable battery technology for your home. Our design phase emphasizes efficiency and reliability, providing you with a robust energy storage solution.
Tailored Design: Custom configuration to match your home’s energy requirements.
Critical Loads Subpanels: Specialized panels for essential circuit continuity.
Technology Selection: Choosing top-tier batteries for optimal performance.
Expert Installation and System Integration
Seamless Installation and Integration
Our skilled installation team ensures that your solar battery system is seamlessly integrated with your solar panels and home’s electrical system. We handle everything from setting up the battery units to configuring the inverters and connecting the “Critical Loads Subpanels.” Our professional approach minimizes disruption and guarantees a clean, efficient installation that enhances your home’s energy resilience.
Professional Setup: Precise installation of battery units and system components.
Inverter Configuration: Efficient integration of inverters for seamless energy conversion.
Minimal Disruption: Installation process designed to be quick and unobtrusive.
Activation and Continuous Support for Your Energy Storage System
Activation and Ongoing Support
After installation, we activate your energy storage system and provide comprehensive guidance on monitoring and managing your solar batteries. Sandbar Solar & Electric Headquarters Building
In 2019, Sandbar Solar & Electric inaugurated its new 11,500 square-foot headquarters, purposefully designed to be a model of energy efficiency and innovation. Faced with potential delays from PG&E in establishing new electrical service, we opted to design, engineer, and install a robust stand-alone microgrid ourselves.
Microgrid Components:
Solar Capacity: 57 kW DC
Energy Storage System (ESS) Capacity: 154 kWh
Generator: 35 kW
Installation Details:
Installation Type: Mechanically attached, flush-mounted racking on a pitched metal roof
Inverters: Ideal Power 30 kW, DC-coupled inverters
Batteries:
Avalon 30 kWh Batteries
Blue Planet 64 kWh Battery
96.5% of our building’s energy requirements
Our solar array and battery system meet 96.5% of our building’s energy requirements annually, while the generator is used for only about 300 hours each year, or 3.5% of the time. This setup ensures that the Sandbar HQ remains fully operational even during extensive grid power outages, showcasing our capability in commercial-grade, 480V, 3-phase power systems.

Solar Panel Cleaning: How Often Should You Really Do It?
Key Takeaways
Regular solar panel cleaning helps maintain energy output by removing dirt, salt residue, and debris that block sunlight and reduce efficiency.
Most homeowners should clean solar panels once or twice per year, with coastal and high-dust areas often needing more frequent service.
Professional solar panel cleaning protects your investment by safely removing buildup without damaging surfaces, wiring, or warranties.
Solar panels are designed to be low maintenance, but that does not mean they are maintenance-free. Over time, dirt, dust, pollen, bird droppings, and coastal salt can reduce how much sunlight reaches your panels. When that happens, your system produces less energy, and your savings take a hit.
Here on California’s Central Coast, solar panel cleaning plays a bigger role than many homeowners realize. This guide explains how often solar panels should really be cleaned, what affects cleaning frequency, and how proper maintenance protects your investment.
Why Solar Panel Cleaning Matters
Solar panels work by absorbing sunlight. When debris builds up on the surface, it blocks light and reduces efficiency. Even a thin layer of dirt can cause noticeable energy loss over time.
In coastal areas like Santa Cruz and Monterey Counties, salt air, fog residue, and airborne particles can accumulate faster than in inland regions. Add seasonal pollen, wildfire ash, and bird activity, and panels can lose efficiency without obvious signs from the ground.
Keeping your panels clean helps:
Maintain consistent energy production
Protect long-term system performance
Maximize your return on investment
Extend the life of your solar panels
How Solar Panels Lose Efficiency Over Time
Solar performance loss often happens slowly, making it easy to overlook. Several environmental and seasonal factors contribute to declining output:
Coastal salt and fog residue: Leaves a thin film that reduces light transmission
Pollen and dust: Builds up seasonally, especially in spring and summer
Wildfire ash: Fine particles can cling to panels and block sunlight
Bird droppings and tree debris: Cause localized shading that disproportionately reduces output
Because these issues develop gradually, homeowners may not notice reduced production until the losses become significant.
What Happens If You Don’t Clean Your Solar Panels?
Neglecting solar panel cleaning can lead to more than just short-term efficiency loss. Over time, the consequences may include:
Reduced energy production and lower monthly savings
Decreased return on investment across the system’s lifespan
Potential long-term surface or seal damage from baked-on residue
Warranty complications if maintenance recommendations are ignored
Routine cleaning is a simple step that helps avoid unnecessary performance and financial losses.
How Often Should You Clean Solar Panels in California?
For most homeowners, solar panel cleaning is recommended once or twice per year. However, your ideal schedule depends on your environment.
Coastal homes (Santa Cruz, Monterey, Santa Clara, Alameda, San Benito, San Mateo): Every 6 months due to salt spray and fog residue
Inland or suburban homes: Once per year is usually sufficient
High-dust or wildfire-prone areas: More frequent cleaning may be needed
Homes near trees or birds: Spot cleaning between full services
If your energy production has dropped and weather conditions have remained consistent, dirty panels may be the cause.
Can Rain Clean Solar Panels?
While rain can rinse away loose dust, it does not fully clean solar panels. Rainwater often leaves behind mineral residue and does not remove sticky contaminants like bird droppings, pollen, or salt buildup.
In dry climates or during long periods without rain, grime accumulates quickly. Relying on rain alone can lead to gradual efficiency loss that adds up over time.
Signs Your Solar Panels Need Cleaning
Homeowners often assume their system is working fine because panels still look mostly clean from the ground. However, performance data usually tells a different story.
Common signs that solar panel cleaning is needed include:
A noticeable drop in energy production
Visible dirt, bird droppings, or residue on panels
Long dry periods without rain
Panels located near trees, roads, or the ocean
Monitoring your system’s output is one of the easiest ways to tell when cleaning is overdue.

While it may be tempting to clean solar panels on your own, DIY cleaning can be risky. Walking on roofs increases the chance of injury, and using the wrong tools or cleaning solutions can damage panel surfaces or void warranties.

Solar Power for Residential Homes: Is Your House a Good Fit?
Key Takeaways
Most homes can support solar with the right design. Roof orientation, sun exposure, and layout can usually be optimized so solar power for residential homes performs efficiently across Santa Cruz and Monterey.
Higher energy use often means stronger long-term value. Homes with larger electricity bills typically benefit most from residential solar, not only through savings but through predictable, stable energy costs.

Solar power for residential homes has become one of the most practical ways for homeowners to lower energy bills, gain control over long-term electricity costs, and invest in their property’s future. With utility rates continuing to rise across California, many homeowners ask the same question: Is my house good for solar panels?
The answer depends on several key factors, including your roof, sun exposure, energy use, and electrical setup—which we’ll walk through below. Understanding these details helps determine whether residential solar is the right fit for your Santa Cruz or Monterey home.
What Makes a Home a Good Candidate for Solar Power?
Every home is different, and solar performance depends on a combination of physical and electrical factors. Fortunately, modern solar technology is flexible and can be adapted to many property types.
Roof Orientation & Sun Exposure
Homes that receive consistent sunlight throughout the day are ideal for solar power. South-facing roofs generally produce the most energy, but east- and west-facing roofs can also deliver strong performance with proper system design. Even homes without perfect orientation can still benefit from solar when panels are strategically placed.
Shading Considerations
Trees, chimneys, nearby structures, and roof features can create shading that reduces output. However, professional design evaluates shading patterns throughout the year and places panels where they will generate the most energy overall. In many cases, shading challenges can be successfully worked around.
Roof Condition & Materials
Because solar panels are built to last decades, your roof should be in good condition before installation. Newer or well-maintained roofs are excellent candidates. Common roofing materials in Santa Cruz and Monterey—such as asphalt shingles, tile, and metal—are all compatible with residential solar systems.
If a roof replacement or repair is needed, addressing it before installing solar helps ensure long-term system reliability and avoids unnecessary disruption later.
How Solar Panels Work
Understanding how solar power for residential homes works can make the decision process easier. The system itself is straightforward and highly reliable.
Solar panels are made up of photovoltaic (PV) cells that capture sunlight. When sunlight hits these cells, it creates an electrical current. This electricity is produced as direct current (DC), which your home cannot use on its own.
That’s where the inverter comes in. The inverter converts DC electricity into alternating current (AC), which is the type of power used by your home’s appliances, lighting, and electrical outlets.
Most residential systems in Santa Cruz and Monterey are grid-tied, meaning they stay connected to the utility grid. This allows your home to use solar energy during the day while drawing from the grid at night or during periods of lower production. Any excess energy your system produces is typically sent back to the grid, helping offset future electricity use through utility credits.
How Your Energy Usage Impacts Solar Savings
One of the strongest indicators that solar power for residential homes makes sense is your household’s electricity usage. Homes with higher monthly energy consumption often see the greatest financial impact from solar.
Residential solar systems are custom-sized based on how much electricity your home actually uses. Rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all approach, system design focuses on offsetting a portion—or in some cases, nearly all—of your household’s energy needs.
In addition to savings, solar provides long-term bill stability. As utility rates continue to rise, producing your own electricity helps protect your household from unpredictable cost increases.

Santa Cruz County is an excellent location for residential solar. Strong year-round sunlight combined with high electricity rates creates an environment where homeowners can see meaningful long-term benefits.
Santa Cruz homeowners also benefit from California’s Net Energy Metering (NEM) policies and the federal Solar Investment Tax Credit (ITC), which can significantly improve system return on investment. Professional installers handle the incentive paperwork, ensuring homeowners receive the benefits they qualify for.
Beyond savings, solar supports local values. Investing in residential solar aligns with Santa Cruz’s sustainability goals and can provide added resilience during Public Safety Power Shutoff (PSPS) eents that affect parts of the region.

Your home’s electrical system plays an important role in solar readiness. In some cases, an electrical panel upgrade may be recommended to safely support a new solar installation. Rather than being a setback, this upgrade improves overall electrical safety and reliability.

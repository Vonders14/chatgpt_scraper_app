AND
MINING THE SUN
WHO WE ARE
Standard Sun, Inc. is a vertically integrated, multinational solar energy corporation. We specialize in the development, mechanical installation, and Operations and Maintenance of ground mounted systems. In 2012, we became Standard Sun; the name emphasizes our focus on delivering the highest standards in quality, safety and service to our partners and clients.
Our unique and proven techniques ensure both quality and production are optimized. As of 2018, we’ve built more than 700MW of solar photovoltaic ground mounted systems worldwide ranging from 400Kw to 120MW. These projects ranged from brownfields, former forests, military installations, superfund sites, subgrade farm land and decommissioned airports and were performed through some of the most challenging conditions the industry has seen to date: sub-arctic temperatures, 3-4 feet of snow, 6-8 feet of frost, spring thaws, flooded sites and desert temperatures.
We appreciate a challenge and have the experience and resources to value engineer reliable, cost effective solutions. We are proud installers of most racking systems: AET, Array Technologies, Clenergy, Cosma, GameChange, Gestamp Solar, Habdank, HST, KB Racking, Mounting Systems, Schletter, Session Solar, S:Flex, Solar FlexRack, Sunlink, Unirac, and more.
We strive to set new industry standards, and we look forward to helping you realize your solar project. Let Standard Sun help you mind the planet, and mine the sun.

Volkswagen
Near Chattanooga, Tennessee is a site formerly known as the Volunteer Army Ammunitions Plant. This plant manufactured upwards of 30,000,000 pounds of trinitrotoluene--commonly known as TNT--per month. Standard Sun with it's partners built a power plant that feeds up to 14% of Volkswagen's Leed platinum certified assembly plant...with no munitions found!

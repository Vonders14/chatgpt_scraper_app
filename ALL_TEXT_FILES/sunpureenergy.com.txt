Asset Management Services
Solar PV systems typically require little ongoing maintenance. Solar is an no maintenance solution however, natural wear and tear over the years can result in poorer operational efficiencies. Undertaking an annual preventative maintenance servicing can ensure that your system delivers optimum performance.
PV systems are exposed to the weather. Within their service life span of 25 years, various faults can develop. The method that Sunpure undertakes for its servicing depends on the type of PV system. Sunpure will primarily review the potential for faults. Circuit diagrams and a technical description of the system are very helpful in this respect, however, Sunpure are often called to work on systems with little data or specification available.
Before Sunpure undertakes any measurements, a visual check of the PV system is carried out, in particular the PV generator. Here we will look for mechanical damage and soiling. The wiring and electrical connections will be checked by our engineers for the following faults which can occur and do not trip any fuse and hence lead to dangerous touch voltages or to arcing:
- Poor or loose wire connections
- Earth leakage through insulation faults
- Short-circuit through insulation faults
Attention will be paid to mechanical and thermal damage and to wires and insulation chewed by animals (such as rodents). The insulation can also be damaged by UV rays, ageing or over-voltages. All insulation ages in the course of time. An insulation fault can lead to arcing and ultimately cause a fire. In Sunpure’s experience the following causes of system faults or failures can occur, listed according to their frequency:
Fault at the inverter
- Loose wiring connection
- Defective string fuses
- Defect with a module and hence partial or total failure of a string (bypass diodes or contacts of individual cells in a module)
- Defective surge protector
- Insulation fault
Before remedying the fault Sunpure will check whether fixing the fault is covered by your installation warranty and whether equipment manufacturers’ warranties can be called on (product liability). If this is not the case, a cost estimate for remedying the fault will be made. An inspection log will be produced for the re-commissioning.
Please get in contact with us to book in servicing for your system.

Residential Solar Plants: A Smarter, Cleaner Energy Choice with Sunpure Energy
In a world racing toward sustainability, renewable energy isn’t just
In today’s fast-paced business environment, sustainability is no longer just an option—it’s a necessity. Companies across industries are adopting renewable energy solutions to cut costs, reduce carbon footprints, and enhance brand reputation. Among these, commercial solar plants have emerged as a powerful tool to achieve both environmental and financial goals.
But installing a solar system is only the first step. The real challenge lies in maintaining its performance, reliability, and efficiency over time. This is where Sunpure Energy’s Asset Management Services come into play—ensuring that businesses maximize returns from their solar investments while staying worry-free about operations.
Imagine setting up a large solar plant on your factory rooftop or open land. The panels start generating electricity, the bills reduce, and you’re satisfied. But over the years, without proper monitoring and maintenance, dust accumulates on panels, wiring loosens, inverters lose efficiency, and unexpected downtimes creep in.
The result? Decreased energy generation, increased repair costs, and delayed return on investment (ROI).
That’s why asset management is the backbone of a successful solar project. It ensures:
Continuous performance tracking to identify underperformance early.
Preventive maintenance to avoid breakdowns.
Optimized energy yield to extract maximum value from every panel.
Financial transparency with reports and analytics for stakeholders.
In short, solar asset management is about making sure your solar plant doesn’t just work—but works at its best, every day.
Sunpure Energy has built its reputation as a trusted name in renewable energy solutions. With a strong focus on commercial and industrial solar plants, the company goes beyond installation, offering a comprehensive suite of asset management services tailored to maximize efficiency.
Here’s how Sunpure ensures your solar assets keep performing:
Sunpure uses advanced monitoring platforms to track solar plant output in real time. Every kilowatt generated is measured against expected benchmarks. If there’s a drop in performance, the system sends alerts instantly.
For example, if an inverter malfunctions or a panel string underperforms, Sunpure’s team detects it immediately. This proactive approach means issues are fixed before they cause significant energy or revenue loss.
Instead of waiting for something to break down, Sunpure relies on predictive analytics to foresee potential problems. Using data insights, the team schedules preventive maintenance, such as cleaning panels, tightening connections, or replacing weak components, before they fail.
This strategy reduces costly downtime and keeps the plant running at maximum capacity.
Commercial businesses thrive on data—and Sunpure delivers just that. Their detailed monthly and annual reports provide insights into:
Total energy generated
Plant efficiency trends
Savings achieved on electricity bills
Environmental impact (CO₂ reduction, equivalent trees saved)
These reports not only guide operational decisions but also serve as valuable sustainability records for audits, investors, and CSR reporting.
Sunpure’s technical support team offers a hybrid model—remote troubleshooting backed by on-site service teams. Whether it’s a small glitch that can be resolved online or a larger issue requiring physical inspection, Sunpure ensures businesses never face long downtimes.
From day one until the end of the plant’s lifecycle, Sunpure helps businesses extract the highest ROI possible. This includes inverter replacement planning, panel degradation management, and financial modeling for upgrades or expansions.
While several companies provide solar services, Sunpure Energy differentiates itself through its end-to-end ownership of performance. For Sunpure, asset management is not a side service—it’s a core commitment.
Key benefits of choosing Sunpure include:
Expertise in commercial solar plants tailored for industries, warehouses, and corporate offices.
Technology integration with AI-driven monitoring tools.
Pan-India service network, ensuring quick support wherever your plant is located.
Customized service plans depending on plant size, capacity, and business needs.
Let’s bring this into perspective with a real-world scenario.
A manufacturing company installs a 1 MW solar plant on its facility. Without asset management, due to dust, shading, and unnoticed inverter issues, the plant’s efficiency drops by 10% annually. That’s a loss of nearly 100,000 units of electricity every year—translating to lakhs of rupees in savings lost.
Now, with Sunpure’s Asset Management Services, the same plant maintains its efficiency, ensuring peak performance throughout its lifespan. Over 20–25 years, this means millions saved, plus the goodwill of being a sustainable brand.
Forward-thinking businesses are treating solar energy not just as an infrastructure investment but as a strategic asset. When managed well, it enhances competitiveness in multiple ways:
Lower operating costs free up capital for growth.
Stable electricity bills protect against rising grid tariffs.
Sustainability credentials improve brand perception and attract eco-conscious customers and investors.
Sunpure’s asset management aligns perfectly with this vision—helping organizations turn solar into a long-term business advantage.
With advancements in AI, IoT, and smart sensors, the future of solar asset management is becoming even more powerful. Sunpure is already integrating next-generation technologies such as:
AI-driven performance analytics for accurate fault prediction.
Drone-based inspections for faster panel and wiring checks.

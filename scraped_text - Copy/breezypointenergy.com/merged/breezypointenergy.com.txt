Indigenous Mission
Bringing Integrity into Being for Mother Earth
To bring integrity into being, Breezy Point believes that we must honor our past as we build our future. We accomplish this by working closely with indigenous communities in various capacities.
We aim is to develop an unyielding bridge with Indigenous Nations by:
Creating lifelong relationships by recognizing and respecting the people
Gaining a comprehension of their traditional practices and philosophies
Creating a constructive relationship for a healthier engagement
Absorbing and understanding local community etiquettes
Warranting mutual interactions with Indigenous societies
Respecting and valuing the land
Acquiring knowledge and empathy for the history of Indigenous Nations
We will accomplish these objectives while informing & promoting the importance of renewable energy with local Indigenous Nation Communities.
Working closely with the Indigenous people supports the practices of:
Recycling
Reducing greenhouse gas emissions
Establishing zero carbon footprint
Decreasing fossil fuel use
All designed to ensure respect, admiration and permanence for Mother Earth.
Educational Programs
Empowering youth to be part of restoring our planet is how we assure a better future.
Helping our planet to thrive is a worthy cause requiring collaboration, innovation, and perseverance. Problem solving this ominous task requires a multitude of resources with the most valuable resource being “people.”
Breezy Point Energy acknowledges the need for change by investing in the education of our youth and young adults. Our educational initiatives are committed to supporting individual students, educational systems, and educators to provide learning platforms that result in every student achieving the productive skills to navigate the 21st Century. This mission contributes to the growth and care of our fellow neighbors of all races, ages, genders, religions, and sexual orientations.
Our company is proud to offer students cutting-edge internships & apprenticeships designed to integrate experiences of:
Self-directed learning
Meaningful adaptive skills
Demonstrated proficiency in both academic and skill-based applications
All focused to promote student self-efficacy in the workplace as well as advancing educational opportunities. Breezy Point Energy’s educational program believes that learning is a process that extends beyond the sharing of knowledge and embraces the wholistic growth of each person’s heart, body, and mind.

CleanPowerInnovation™ is centered around developing on-grid and off-grid large scale solar power projects dedicated to emerging markets. Our value proposition is to enable the deployment of large solar power plants (>10MW) with a significant contribution of local contents. We are targeting countries with large oil and gas natural resources and high Solar Direct Normal Irradiance (DNI) that use highly subsidized non-renewable energy sources to generate local, subsidized electricity. The mission of CleanPowerInnovation™ group is three-fold:
75MW turn key solar cell line in Sunnyvale, CA
2MW solar park in Kénitra, Morocco

Targeted customers will be countries with oil and gas natural resources and high DNI that are financially motivated to move away from using their natural resources subsidized electricity. For instance, Saudi Arabia is generating its own electricity using oil that is >90% subsidized and is expected to become an oil importer by 2030 if no other cost effective alternatives for electricity production come into play. Saudi Arabia plans to generate 41GW of solar electricity by 2032. CleanPowerInnovation™ has already presented such projects in Algeria, Turkey and Morocco and received strong interest.
Other potential candidates will be countries such as Saudi Arabia, Libya, UAE, and Qatar and other countries with high DNI, which allows for the maximum harvest of production (kWh) per installed base (kWpeak). We expect that the cost of kWh produced in these high DNI countries will be at or below the local subsidized grid parity. Also, the company will be building off-grid solar plants isolated from grid infrastructure, for instance along the Algerian borders which will make possible new communities to settle. Market in those regions is expected to reach 100GW within the next 10 years based on current market forecast.
CPI is present in the following countries:
U.S.A, France, Germany, Italy, Morocco, Algeria, Qatar and Kazachstan.

Beyond merely developing and building solar PV generating facilities, CleanPowerInnovation™ is proposing a fully integrated concept, whereby local manufacturing capacity (wafer/cell/module/racking/inverters, etc.) will be developed in parallel. The company targets this local content to increase over time to reach 80% within 10 years.
CleanPowerInnovation™ will ultimately guarantee the kWh output and cost. Thus, the company will be responsible to develop, engineer, procure and construct the solar generating facilities on time, within budget and will minimize the environmental impacts. The company, directly and through its strategic partners will create a local solar infrastructure that will be competitive with market prices and will guarantee bankable quality products at lowest cost of goods sold. Further, the company will help develop solar products that are compatible with the arid and harsh climate of most of these countries.
The guaranteed local content will be competitive with the best products available in the market to guarantee output and cost at the kWh level. For that purpose, the company will oversee the entire manufacturing operation for each supply chain components delivered to the solar plants.
For instance, the company will guarantee: kW outputs, manufacturing yields, product quality, cost of goods sold and conformance along with continual improvement of work practices. The company would oversee the local manufacturing of wafer/cell/module as well as inverters and storage.
The company is bringing years of experience and expertise in technology development, fast production ramp up and high quality volume manufacturing. To ensure continual products innovation and manufacturing improvements such as throughput, efficiency, cycle time, class yield, the company will set up procedures and work practices in conformity to ISO 9001:2008 standards. In addition, due to the long term business agreements, the company will be able to implement new disruptive technology paths into the local content supply chain to enable further kWh cost reduction.

CPI proposes development of residential as well large industrial solar heater projects in emerging countries with three large scale ongoing projects.

CPI is developing local content in the area of solar heaters in emerging countries by supporting local production, and believes that clean power using economical solutions will drive renewable energies in those countries.

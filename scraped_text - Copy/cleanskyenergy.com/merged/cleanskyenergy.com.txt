What if you have a power outage?
In the event of an emergency power outage in your home, call your local utility company. They are responsible for fixing power outages when they occur. Your utility’s contact information is found on your monthly energy invoice.
Do I need any special equipment for a green energy plan?
No additional equipment is necessary to receive electricity from CleanSky Energy. Your home or business will use the same energy-supply lines as always.
Is CleanSky Energy affiliated with the local utilities?
No. We are in no way associated with utility companies. CleanSky Energy is licensed by the appropriate Public Utility Commissions to supply electricity in all of the states we service. The utility companies are regulated to transport electricity to the population. CleanSky Energy purchases green energy commodities from the open energy market in order to provide the most affordable energy rates possible to our customers.
How long will it take for my electricity to switch over?
The exact times for switching is determined by your local utility. It depends on when you enroll but the change will most likely occur on the date of the next scheduled meter-read by the utility. After enrolling with us, you will receive a welcome letter with the details of your energy agreement. It may take one to two billing cycles for CleanSky Energy to appear on your invoice from the utility company.
Is it necessary to call my current supplier?
No. We’ll handle notifying the right people for you. CleanSky will ensure it’s completely seamless and with no interruption in energy service.
How much does it cost to switch to CleanSky?
There is no enrollment fee with CleanSky Energy. However, your local utility may charge a nominal, one-time service charge that will appear on the following invoice from the utility.

Residential Electricity FAQs
What are residential electricity plans?
Residential electricity plans allow homeowners to choose their electricity supplier, plan length, and
pricing structure while continuing to receive power through their local utility.
How do I switch to CleanSky Energy?
Enrolling is fast and simple! Explore our energy plans and agreements, compare rates, and select
the plan that aligns with your needs. You can sign up online or call us at 1-888-733-5557. You can
also reach out via email at CustomerCare@CleanSkyEnergy.com. We’re available Monday-Friday
from 8:00am-7:00pm ET and Saturday from 10:00am-2:00pm ET.
How much does it cost to switch to CleanSky Energy?
There is no enrollment fee with CleanSky Energy; however, your local utility may charge a nominal,
one-time service charge that will appear on the following invoice from the utility.
Are CleanSky’s residential energy plans renewable?
Yes! All CleanSky residential energy plans are powered by 100% renewable wind and solar
energy, verified through Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs).
What are Renewable Energy Certificates?
Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) represent the environmental benefits of electricity generated
from renewable sources like wind and solar. One REC is generated for every megawatt-hour of
renewable electricity produced and added to the power grid.
What are the advantages of renewable energy?
CleanSky offers 100% renewable energy plans backed by Renewable Energy Credits (RECs). This
ensures that every unit of energy used is tracked and accounted for via sustainable sources such as
wind, solar, and hydro.

How do clean energy plans work in Texas deregulated markets?
Customers choose their retail electric provider, such as CleanSky Energy, and electricity plan rather than being assigned a utility with a set rate. Utilities still own and maintain the power lines and deliver electricity, but REPs buy power from the wholesale market and sell it to customers through a variety of plans. These plans can vary by price structure (fixed vs. variable), contract length, time-of-use rates, and features like renewable energy content or bill credits.
Clean energy plans are an option within deregulated markets. The actual electrons powering your home come from the same Texas grid regardless of plan, but REPs purchase renewable energy certificates (RECs) on your behalf to match the amount of electricity you use.

Campus-scale energy, where generation meets demand.
Copia Power is a U.S. developer of power and digital assets, focusing on gigawatt-scale campuses that include solar, energy storage, thermal and digital grid-connected projects.
Since our founding in 2021, we have focused on enhancing grid reliability and unlocking infrastructure investment opportunities through the convergence of generation, capacity and compute demand resources. For each campus, we partner with utilities, municipalities, cooperatives, and corporations to deliver tailored solutions. Through close collaboration, we unlock best-in-class transmission, interconnection, and load solutions, which are optimized for performance and reliability.
What We Build
At Copia, we build more than projects — we build campuses. Each campus integrates renewable generation, storage, and dispatchable resources with the ability to host digital infrastructure. We also design and construct the critical transmission and substation infrastructure that ties it all together — ensuring reliable delivery and grid connectivity. The result is a platform that delivers power where it’s needed most, with the speed, certainty, and scale today’s grid and customers’ demand.

About Copia
Copia Power is a Carlyle-backed energy and digital infrastructure platform committed to developing, constructing, and owning large-scale assets across the U.S. to accelerate the electrification and growth of the digital economy. Copia Power has over 2,600 MW of energy generation and storage assets in operation or under construction and is actively developing over 15 GW of grid-connected data centers supported by Copia’s portfolio of gigawatt-scale energy campuses comprising more than 25 GW of solar and storage and 7 GW of natural gas generation assets.
Core Strengths
What sets Copia apart is not just what we build, but how we build it. Our model is designed to overcome the barriers that slow traditional developers, combining strategic siting, disciplined execution, and proven leadership. Equally important, we build for the long term – with our engineering teams delivering durable assets, and operating them with discipline that ensures performance for generations. Backed by Carlyle, Copia delivers the reliability, resilience, and enduring value that utilities, digital customers, and communities demand.
Copia Model
Regional Campus Model
Instead of scattered 100 MW projects, Copia develops phased 1–3 GW campuses that compound in value over time.
Multi-Asset Layering
We integrate solar, storage, thermal, and digital infrastructure on a single site — creating “infrastructure inertia” that attracts utilities, hyperscalers, and industrial customers. These critical infrastructure points give our partners the runway and time to invest long-term knowing they’ll have the power, water, and networking infrastructure to support their growth ambitions regardless of scale.
Execution Certainty
A lean, high-conviction team with proven playbooks, financial discipline, and institutional capital ensures speed to market and durable returns.
What We Build
At Copia, we build more than projects — we build campuses. Each campus brings together renewable generation, storage, and dispatchable resources, along with the transmission and substation infrastructure needed to deliver that power reliably, with the ability to host digital infrastructure on-site. By integrating multiple technologies and grid assets in one location, Copia creates flexible, resilient platforms that adapt to customer needs, strengthen the grid, and support long-term growth.
Copia is a leading developer of utility-scale solar projects. Our in-house team delivers reliable, cost-competitive power solutions that meet the low-carbon needs of our customers.
Solar
Copia currently leverages our deep expertise in battery storage to develop utility-scale projects that enhance grid reliability and provide customers with the flexibility to access power when it’s needed most.
Storage
Copia’s strategically sited thermal generation provides dispatchable capacity that complements renewables and supports grid stability. Offering around-the-clock power, these projects deliver reliable energy during peak demand and system stress, ensuring customers have access to the power they need with certainty and scale.
Thermal
Copia’s digital infrastructure sites pair power with load, creating purpose-built sites designed for energy data-intensive industries. By integrating energy, fiber, and land within a single development, we deliver scalable solutions that provide customers with the speed, certainty, and reliability needed to grow their digital footprint.
Digital
Our Team
Copia’s leadership team brings decades of experience across renewable power, thermal generation, and digital infrastructure. Together, they have a proven track record of developing and operating large-scale assets, raising billions in financing, and executing with institutional discipline. Guided by a clear vision and shared history, our leaders not only drive Copia’s growth but also empower our people to build meaningful careers as we deliver the next generation of campus-scale infrastructure.

Copia Power, a Carlyle Portfolio Company, Upsizes Corporate Debt Facility to $575mm to Fuel Increased Growth Opportunities Across Its Scaled Digital and Power Infrastructure Business
Dana Point, CA and Washington, DC, December 4, 2025 – Copia Power (Copia) is pleased to announce it has upsized its existing corporate credit facility, originally closed in March 2024, to $575 million to support the continued growth and execution of its fully scaled digital and power infrastructure businesses.
Alongside the upsize, Copia has also expanded the availability of the facility to be used to fund the procurement and development for its near-term 8+ GW of thermal generation and to accelerate data center commercialization at several of its giga-scale campuses. This facility is designed to grow organically alongside the business, providing an increasing but right-sized borrowing base as Copia continues to deliver market-moving solutions and growth.
According to Brian Callaway, Copia’s CFO, “We are grateful to our lenders for their support as we continue to execute on our $30b+ giga-scale, integrated power-plus-digital strategy that has been a half-decade in the making.”
Stoel Rives LLP served as Borrower’s counsel on the deal. Milbank served as CLA’s counsel.

Copia Power, a Carlyle Portfolio Company, Closes $595 Million Tax Equity Commitment for Centennial Flats
Dana Point, CA and Washington, DC, December 17, 2025 – Copia Power (Copia) today announced it has closed a $595 million tax equity commitment with J.P. Morgan, as the sole tax equity investor, for its Centennial Flats Project.
The Centennial Flats project is located in La Paz County, AZ, about 75 miles west of Phoenix, and consists of 500 MW of solar generating capacity and 1,069 MWh of battery energy storage capacity. The project is being constructed in three phases with expected completion dates throughout 2026.
“I'm deeply grateful for the dedication and commitment shown by all parties to advance Centennial Flats through the project’s first mechanical completion milestone and tax equity closing,” said Tony Archibald, EVP and Chief Operating Officer. “These achievements reflect the daily work happening across the site as the teams work through the final stages of construction toward operations. Copia Power, a Carlyle Portfolio Company, Closes an $890 million Tax Equity Commitment for Maricopa Energy Center
Dana Point, CA and Washington, DC, December 18, 2025 – Copia Power (Copia) today announced it has executed an $890 million tax equity commitment with Wells Fargo for its Maricopa Energy Center projects. Wells Fargo provided separate commitments across each phase of the Maricopa facility, which combined represents one of the largest commitments for a solar and/or storage facility in the US.
Maricopa Energy Center is located in Maricopa County, Arizona and consists of 550 MW of solar generating capacity and 2,200 MWh of battery energy storage capacity. This is the third portion of our 30,000-acre Harquahala digital and power infrastructure campus to be commercialized. The project is being constructed in two phases which have each executed a Power Purchase Agreement with APS and are scheduled to reach commercial operation in 2026-2027. Once operational, Maricopa will be the single largest solar-plus-storage project in Arizona.
“Copia continues to demonstrate exceptional execution as it builds large scale, high-impact power infrastructure projects across the U.S. and this tax equity commitment underscores the strength of our platform,” said Dan Murphy, Copia’s Managing Director and Head of Capital Markets. “Maricopa Energy Center is a defining project for Arizona as one of the largest power-infrastructure projects in the state and we are proud to close this transformative transaction with Wells Fargo.”
“Closing tax equity for Maricopa underscores the strength of our teams across planning, procurement, and construction as well as our commitment to deliver complex, infrastructure solutions at unprecedented scale to our partners,” said Nate Lapierre, Managing Director and Head of Project Management and Procurement.

Our History
Our history is built on proven platforms, strategic partnerships, and disciplined execution — united by our mission to deliver reliable, scalable infrastructure where it’s needed most. Together, these foundations have shaped Copia into a leading builder of integrated power and digital infrastructure.
Foundations of Copia
-
Birch Infrastructure
Founded in 2019, Birch Infrastructure was established to meet the rising power needs of data centers and other large industrial consumers. Built on the premise that the internet’s growth would eventually outpace available energy supply, Birch focused on developing renewable solutions to serve this demand. In 2022 Copia Power and Birch complete a strategic merger, combining platforms to accelerate growth.
-
Carlyle
In 2021, Carlyle invested in Birch Infrastructure and launched Copia Power. Together, the two created a platform capable of delivering multi-gigawatt energy and digital infrastructure projects across the U.S.
-
Copia Power
Copia Power was founded by Carlyle in 2021. Built on a leadership team with decades of experience in energy, the company develops, finances, and operates gigawatt-scale campuses that integrate generation and load. Today, Copia is assembling one of the largest energy and digital portfolios in the U.S., anchored in discipline, reliability, and long-term value creation.

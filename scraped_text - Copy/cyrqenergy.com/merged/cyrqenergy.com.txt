URL: https://cyrqenergy.com/about-us/

Cyrq Energy is responding to rapidly growing electricity demand from data centers, electric vehicles and other needs by providing clean baseload energy around the clock. As a leader in geothermal energy operations and development, Cyrq delivers 100% renewable power to its customers throughout the western United States, day or night, rain or shine, and the wind doesn’t blow. Cyrq is proud to play an important role in sustainability and carbon reduction in the western U.S. generation resource stack.
We harness the heat of the earth to create clean energy while pursuing the highest standards of health, safety, and sustainability.
We believe in a future fueled by renewable energy and are driving the transition towards that vision.
We put the well-being of our teams, partners, and communities first. Every project, procedure, and decision is grounded in a culture of safety that ensures people return home exactly as they arrived.
We treat everyone we work with—employees, partners, landowners, and communities—with honesty and respect. We listen first, communicate clearly, and build long-term relationships rooted in trust.
We take ownership of our work and our impact. Every individual at Cyrq is responsible for upholding our standards, honoring commitments, and delivering on what we promise.
Energy transformation happens through teamwork. We collaborate across disciplines and with our partners to solve complex problems, share knowledge, and drive geothermal innovation forward.

URL: https://cyrqenergy.com/leadership/

Mark Sudbey is the Chief Executive Officer of Cyrq Energy, a leading provider of clean, reliable, and renewable geothermal power. Headquartered in Salt Lake City, Cyrq Energy operates five geothermal power facilities across Nevada, California, and Utah, with additional projects under development throughout the western United States. Under Mark’s leadership, the company continues to advance sustainable power generation and drive a cleaner energy future.
With more than 30 years of experience in the power and energy sector, Mark is a mission-driven and forward-thinking executive known for his strategic growth leadership. He combines deep industry knowledge with a broad network of stakeholders, plant operators, and energy partners, focusing on operational excellence, long-term value creation, and the development of high-performing, purpose-driven teams. Before joining Cyrq Energy, Mark served as CEO of AlphaGen, a newly established energy platform overseeing affiliate operations including Eastern Generation and Generation Bridge. In May 2024, he transitioned to a senior advisor role and continues to serve on the AlphaGen Board of Directors.
Mark has been recognized for his contributions to the energy industry, appearing on the City & State Energy & Environment Power 100 list in 2021, 2022, and 2023 for his leadership in advancing New York’s energy transition and environmental progress.
Mark holds a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering Technology from the University of Massachusetts, Lowell, and completed Executive Education in Applied Business Analytics at the MIT Sloan School of Management.

Three geothermal production wells and Five injection wells

SALT LAKE CITY–(BUSINESS WIRE)–Cyrq Energy, LLC (Cyrq) is pleased to announce an agreement to sell its Blue Mountain geothermal power facility in Nevada for $88 million to a subsidiary of Ormat Technologies, Inc. (NYSE:ORA) as part of its five-year strategic plan.
With headquarters in Salt Lake City, Cyrq has been a leader in developing and producing geothermal power since 2007. Currently, Cyrq owns several power generating facilities, including the Soda Lake and Patua geothermal power plants in north central Nevada, the Thermo geothermal power plant in south central Utah, and the Hudson Ranch geothermal power plant in southern California. The sale of the Blue Mountain plant will enable Cyrq to direct more of its business activities toward project development.
“The sale of Blue Mountain is an important component in Cyrq’s commitment to pursue project development,” said CEO Mark Sudbey. “By generating greater financial flexibility with proceeds from the sale, it allows us to pursue opportunities at various sites where we already have significant asset and resource positions. Now, more than ever, Cyrq’s experience developing renewable, reliable, baseload geothermal electricity is needed to build and ensure a sustainable future. We are proud of what Cyrq has achieved with the Blue Mountain project, and it is now time to focus our attention on our next stage of growth.”
The Blue Mountain geothermal power facility provides approximately 20MW of net generating capacity and is located near Winnemucca, Nevada in Humboldt County. Blue Mountain sells electricity to Nevada Power Company, a subsidiary of NV Energy, under a long-term power purchase agreement.

Cyrq Energy is pleased to announce that Mark Sudbey has joined Cyrq Energy as our new CEO. With over 30 years of experience in the power and energy sector, Mark is a forward-thinking and mission-driven leader. He was instrumental in building and growing one of the largest privately owned power-generating portfolios in the U.S. during his tenure at US Power Generating Company (USPG), where he led the company through three private equity transactions. He has deep operations experience in thermal energy, overseeing 22 plants across the US in his most recent role at AlphaGen.

Our Activities
At DORIS we are committed to bringing the highest value to our clients’ projects by providing robust engineering during all the phases of a development up to decommissioning. Our multidisciplinary teams draw on our 60 years of experience and our expert capability to deliver engineering services that enable faster and safer developments with reduced cost and environmental impact.
With our independent expertise, we are ideally positioned to resolve the technical and business challenges experienced by governments, operators, project and technology developers. Our consulting team, supported by our technical experts, comprehensively address risks and opportunities to ensure the ultimate success of our clients’ developments or businesses.
At DORIS we manage projects of any size, both local and international. We provide a complete offering of project management services and can allocate resources on an adaptable basis, be it integrating into our clients’ technical team or supervising and managing projects. Our project management teams focus on planning and de-risking projects and ensuring that costs are appropriately monitored and controlled.
Across our 60 years of experience, we have developed a broad range of technical expertise that we actively reapply to our clients’ projects. We can support clients from the earliest phases of their project’s development, through execution, construction, installation, and up to field operations.
ODE Asset Management, a subsidary 100% owned by DORIS, is an established asset management and operations support provider. Across our operations, we integrate ourselves seamlessly into clients’ teams. We draw on our deep understanding of the cost and complexity challenges seen in the UKCS energy sector and have a unique service-offering tailored to our clients’ needs.
Data management and valorization allow organizations to be more efficient in their decision-making process and in their project execution and operation. For these reasons, DORIS has developed adapted tools to support our clients with their operational challenges and their continuous process of collecting, structuring and valorizing data.

URL: https://www.dorisgroup.com/doris-rosen-and-spiecapag-announce-the-pipeline-transition-alliance-providing-full-services-capability-to-re-purpose-natural-gas-infrastructure-to-hydrogen-service/

Members of the Pipeline Transition Alliance after signing the strategic MOU
(From left to right) Hernando Caceres (ROSEN, VP – Asia Pacific), Jason Paterson (SPIECAPAG, Business Strategy & Growth Manager), Daniel Schneke (ROSEN, Director – Business Execution), Jordan Yoxall (ROSEN, ADIA Regional Manager), Antony Loane (DORIS, Business Development Manager – APAC)
DORIS, ROSEN and SPIECAPAG announce the formation of the Pipeline Transition Alliance, a partnership dedicated to re-purposing Natural Gas Infrastructure to Hydrogen Service.
Broad adoption of hydrogen as a new energy source depends on large scale, cost effective transmission, and owners of pipeline infrastructure will play a critical role in meeting this challenge by re-purposing existing assets to hydrogen service. Associated benefits of re-purposing existing infrastructure may include mitigated de-commissioning burdens, and security of future energy supply contracts. However introducing hydrogen into existing pipelines represents significant complexity and risk given the differentiated properties of hydrogen over natural gas.
The Pipeline Transition Alliance provides dependable, world class support to pipeline infrastructure owners across all project phases. The value we bring derives from the skill sets and expertise of each global partner that, together, allows us to cover the entire scope:
- ROSEN is responsible for pipeline inspection, assessment and integrity management services. ROSEN’s cutting edge inspection and testing technologies allow for rapid, dependable early stage assessment and estimation.
- DORIS undertakes engineering of the pipeline and associated hydrogen production and conditioning systems. With 60 years of experience in the energy industry, DORIS’ renowned gas processing engineering capabilities support the design of highly efficient and safe facilities.
- SPIECAPAG provides Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) services for delivery of the re-purposed pipeline infrastructure along with new facilities as required to meet the client’s specifications and future demands.
Antony Loane, DORIS’ Business Development Manager for Asia-Pacific, commented, “We’re able to provide our clients with confidence and clarity in understanding the risks and costs associated with introducing hydrogen into their assets. But the principal benefit comes from access to our cutting edge technology and on-going experience from hydrogen transition projects we’re currently undertaking across the globe –.”
Hernando Caceres, the Vice President for Business Execution, ROSEN Group in Asia Pacific, emphasised the significance of this partnership, stating, “In forging this strategic alliance between Doris, ROSEN, and Spiecepag, we are positioning to deliver an integrated solution for hydrogen pipelines. Leveraging our combined expertise, we guide customers in repurposing existing pipelines while efficiently designing, building, and operating new ones. I believe this alliance is a critical step towards accelerating our customers’ ambitious energy transition plans, reinforcing our focus on sustainable solutions.”
Jason Paterson, Spiecapag’s Business Strategy & Growth Manager added, “This alliance has been formed to provide asset owners with an ‘end to end’ pipeline transition service to assist with the re-purposing of existing pipeline infrastructure from natural gas to Hydrogen, covering all stages of the project lifecycle. This combined partnership of world leaders in our respective fields now provides our customers with access to a formidable team of subject matter experts and technologies to assist with this transition”.
About DORIS
With six decades of expertise across the globe, DORIS is a leader in providing engineering, advisory, and project management services to the energy sector. Committed to innovation, DORIS is dedicated to making conventional energies cleaner and advancing the development of renewable and new energies through the delivery of reliable, environmentally integral, and innovative projects.
DORIS is renowned for enhancing the oil, gas, and LNG industries by minimizing carbon footprints, reducing costs, optimizing performance, and improving safety. Its history is marked by more than 100 industry firsts, highlighting its role as a catalyst for change within the energy sector.
Utilizing its comprehensive experience from working on conventional and renewable energy projects, DORIS supports the advancement of new energy industries, including hydrogen, carbon capture, and biogas. The firm is focused on developing a low-cost hydrogen industry, de-risking the CCUS value chain, and meeting the international demand for biogas.
About ROSEN
ROSEN Australia Pty Ltd was established in 1998, with offices in Perth and Melbourne. With more than 25 years of experience in Australia, ROSEN has built a solid foundation and developed a network of local people and technology experts in asset integrity management. Since its establishment, ROSEN Australia has successfully completed inspection projects on over 120,000 kilometers of pipelines in most countries within the regions. ROSEN provides comprehensive operational support, data processing and evaluation, and maintenance facilities in Perth and Melbourne, enabling them to provide tailored solutions.

URL: https://www.dorisgroup.com/projects/block-29-conceptual-engineering/

DORIS delivered Conceptual Engineering for the Subsea Production System (SPS) & Subsea Umbilical, Risers and Flowlines for Block 29.
Project outline
Development of the Conceptual Engineering for the Subsea Production System (SPS) and Subsea Umbilical, Risers, and Flowlines (URF) and Gas Export for Block 29. Block 29 is located offshore in the south of the Gulf of Mexico about 88 km from shore with a water depth ranging from 500 to 600 m. - Develop the technical definition of the gas export concept
- Address local logistics for the supply and installation of equipment
- Perform CAPEX, OPEX, and ABEX cost estimates
- Develop a total project execution schedule
- Perform risk assessments

URL: https://www.dorisgroup.com/projects/seaquester-offshore-ccs-system/

DORIS delivered conceptual process design for the carrier vessel in this offshore CCS system.
Project outline
SeaQuester has defined a new system for the disposal of carbon dioxide in offshore reservoirs. The Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS) system allows for the loading, conditioning and compressing of gaseous carbon dioxide (CO2) into high pressure storage facilities onboard a bespoke carrier vessel. The vessel sails to location, hooks up to a modified subsea Christmas tree called the Pivotree™, that provides the station keeping (mooring) and riser, and injects the carbon dioxide into the reservoir.
Services delivered by DORIS
DORIS provided a conceptual process design for the vessel topsides including:
- Identification of unit operations.
- Process Simulation.
- Definition of boundary conditions.
- Estimation of key utility requirements.
- Process flow diagrams.

URL: https://www.dorisgroup.com/projects/gray-whale-3-floating-offshore-wind-farm/

DORIS is delivering two FEED contracts across this floating offshore wind project for BadaEnergy.
Project outline
The Gray Whale 3 floating offshore wind farm is set to have a capacity of 504 MW. Situated approximately 60-70 km from the port of Ulsan, the project is part of the three-phase Gray Whale development which is expected to reach a total capacity of 1.5 GW and become one of the largest floating offshore wind farms in the world.
BadaEnergy is a joint venture between Corio Generation, TotalEnergies and SK ecoplant.
Services delivered by DORIS
DORIS has been selected to deliver two FEED contracts on the Gray Whale 3 offshore wind farm. The first FEED, awarded in consortium with HHI, covers the floating units of the project including the design and the procurement and installation of the floaters and their station keeping systems.
The second FEED contract DORIS is executing includes the project’s full electrical and communications system to the grid connection, array and export cabling, and all aspects of the fixed offshore substation foundation and topsides structure.
DORIS is drawing on its extensive track record in South Korea – which currently exceeds over 3 GW – and its expert capability in floating offshore wind to deliver these contracts.

URL: https://www.dorisgroup.com/projects/trion-field/

DORIS delivered SURF and Export Pipeline Pre-FEED and SURF FEED to this project.
Project outline
The Trion field encompasses an area of 1,285 sq km (798 sq mi) and is located in the Perdido Fold Belt in the northern portion of the Mexican sector of the Gulf of Mexico at a water depth of approximately 2500 meters. Woodside is the operator holding a 60% interest in the development and PEMEX Exploration and Production is the non-operating partner with a 40% interest. The development includes Production Wells and Water Injection Wells tied back to a floating host facility. At the host facility the produced fluids will be processed, the oil will be offloaded, and an export pipeline will transport the gas back to shore. The produced water will be injected back into the reservoir.
This project would be in the deepest water depth ever developed in Mexico.
Services delivered by DORIS
- SURF and Export Pipeline Facilities Pre-FEED. The objective of the Pre-FEED was to evaluate, select, and determine the optimal subsea facilities configuration.
- SURF FEED. The objective of the FEED was to develop technical documentation at a sufficient level of detail to prepare for an EPCI Execution Phase.
- During these studies, subsea layouts, gas export route comparison, technical definition of main equipment / system, and project cost estimate and execution schedule were completed to support the project development.

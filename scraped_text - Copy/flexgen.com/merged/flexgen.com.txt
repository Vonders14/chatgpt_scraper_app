URL: https://flexgen.com/resources/blog/derisk-energy-storage-projects-global-supply-chain-partners

As the year winds down, battery storage developers, IPPs, and utility companies are racing to get energy storage projects moving, and for good reason. Geopolitical tensions, supply chain instability, and evolving trade policies are reshaping how and where critical components can be sourced. At the same time, the battery technology space is quickly evolving, adding more pressure on teams to make strategic decisions for the future of their energy storage projects.
In this environment, hardware availability and pricing can change overnight, making year-end energy storage project milestones especially challenging to meet. Relying on a single OEM’s factory queue or locking in on an inflexible configuration can stall progress and limit flexibility just when agility matters most. That’s where a hardware-agnostic energy storage partner, along with a global procurement network and rigorous qualification process, creates real competitive advantage. This blog explores how to move energy storage projects forward with confidence amid regulatory, supply chain, and market uncertainty.
Watch the latest webinar to hear from experts on how to safe harbor projects in 2025.
Global procurement that moves projects forward
Partnering with a global procurement team that manages vendors around the world can streamline projects and cut lead times. They provide optionality to developers while constantly doing the due diligence of comparing vendors across key criteria: quality, cost, safety, performance, availability, service, and bankability. Hardware flexibility backed by established relationships between supply chain experts and manufacturers provides developers with not just flexibility, but proven configurations that have been pre-integrated and tested. This also streamlines contracting with flexible scheduling andm with financial support from a bankable partner, accelerates project launches.
Working with a hardware-agnostic energy storage partner makes all the difference when managing project timelines amidst market uncertainties. A flexible, policy-aware procurement process enables developers to remain compliant and adaptable. As supply chain experts actively track FEOC (Foreign Entity of Concern) and non-FEOC supply paths, they can pivot quickly if a vendor’s eligibility status changes. Rather than redesigning the system late in development, projects can swap in compliant components and keep moving forward without missing incentive windows or interconnection prioritization.
Instead of managing the time-intensive vendor qualification process or juggling multiple OEM contracts, developers can rely on a single partner to coordinate it all. A truly end-to-end partner manages the complex network of suppliers, giving customers one point of contact and one contract to oversee. With in-country team members on the ground, factory audits are more comprehensive, communication is streamlined, and quality audits are more frequent. This streamlines not just BESS procurement, but also reduces risk throughout the entire lifetime of the project with:
- Simplified contracting, ensuring zero legal gaps and a single point of accountability to reduce procurement and project risk
- Faster speed to contract and build, leveraging prequalified OEMs who have multi-gigawatt capacity and price-locking for projects targeting COD by year-end
- A single system performance guarantee, consisting of power, energy, and round-trip efficiency that covers you from the site level down to each component
- Strong bankability and liquidity position, covering comprehensive project risk management
With the right partner, developers gain both supply chain flexibility and project confidence, without the complexity of managing it all themselves.
Energy storage solutions built with you, not for you
Choosing a hardware-agnostic energy storage partner means designing your system around your needs, not around a vendor’s catalog. Hardware flexibility enables a more tailored and collaborative design process built around your market, duty cycle, and KPIs, not a cookie-cutter template. A turnkey solution means working with supply chain, system engineering, and commercial experts to:
- Select the best chemistry at the best price based on what’s available
- Design systems that best fit specific project and market requirements
- Accelerate project timelines by leveraging multiple suppliers that have been prequalified and preconfigured
- Enable policy-proof flexibility and the ability to shift vendors with real-time supply insights
- Future-proof your battery storage system so the same flexibility applies when it’s time for augmentation or replacement down the road
Energy storage owners and developers don’t need to be experts in every aspect of BESS design or procurement to build reliable systems. The key is partnering with a team that is. A hardware-agnostic partner brings the technical depth, vendor relationships, and validation processes needed to evaluate and integrate multiple technologies with confidence; ensuring the project performs as intended today and well into the future.
Quality control through integration, commissioning and beyond
Developers don’t need to manage multiple vendor warranties or validate every energy storage component themselves. An end-to-end software and services provider can oversee the full quality control process, ensuring that every piece of the system, from battery modules to power conversion equipment, performs reliably before it reaches the site.
This includes factory audits and witness testing to catch issues early, as well as hardware-in-the-loop simulations that verify how components work together under real-world conditions. By managing these steps internally with our Innovation Lab, FlexGen reduces risk, speeds commissioning, and gives energy storage developers and financers confidence that the system will operate as expected from day one.
As an authorized service provider (ASP) for multiple battery and PCS manufacturers, FlexGen enables faster on-site response and resolution. And, those same supply chain relationships that support design and procurement play a role in operational success and a closed feedback loop. With a single partner handling testing, validation, and service, projects move forward smoothly, with fewer surprises and long-term performance you can trust.
Building momentum into 2026
Locking in the right energy storage partnerships, validating configurations, and ensuring policy alignment now can accelerate progress to secure existing tax benefits and favorable positions in the supply queue before year-end.
Take advantage of a hardware agnostic partner with global procurement agility to move faster, manage risk more effectively, and position your projects for success well into 2026 and beyond.
FlexGen’s team of global procurement specialists, system engineers, and integration experts can help you design, source, and deploy a hardware-agnostic storage solution that meets your project’s unique goals. Contact us to get started.

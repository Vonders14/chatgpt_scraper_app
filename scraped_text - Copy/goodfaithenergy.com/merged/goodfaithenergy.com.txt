About Good Faith Energy
Good Faith Energy History
Good Faith Energy made its entry into the solar industry in 2014. Founder Mohammed Abdalla found inspiration in his transformative experiences abroad. After being let go from a corporate role in the oil and gas industry, Mohammed embarked on a year-long journey. He travelled across South America, Europe, Africa, and Asia. Carrying only two backpacks, he spent much of his time reflecting on his life and the environmental impact of his former career.
While traveling by train through Germany, Mohammed encountered a sight that would change the course of his life: vast numbers of rooftops adorned with solar panels, harnessing the sun’s energy. Mohammed was startled by the discovery, especially after learning that about 40% of Germany’s power was solar-generated, despite receiving as little sunlight as Alaska. This revelation ignited a passion in him to bring similar sustainable energy solutions to Texas, leading to the birth of Good Faith Energy. Our company has made a commitment to reduce environmental footprints and promote solar energy, inspired by the vision Mohammed gained on his travels!
Why We Believe in Solar Energy
Driven by a vision of a cleaner, sustainable future, Mohammed committed to transitioning from traditional energy sources to a solar-electric economy. He could envision a future where every household device—from your television and computer to your HVAC system—could be powered safely and sustainably by solar energy.
Returning to the States, Mohammed faced challenges while trying to install solar panels on his mother’s house in Plano, TX. He encountered unresponsive companies and received impersonal service, which lacked the educational support he valued. This experience underscored the need for a more customer-focused approach in the solar industry.
Motivated to fill this gap, Mohammed engaged with the North Texas Renewable Energy Group and Plano Solar Advocates. These are two nonprofits committed to promoting renewable energy education and advocacy. He dedicated two years to volunteering with these organizations. He was eager to absorb knowledge and experience to realize his renewable energy project development dreams.
A decade later, Good Faith Energy stands as a nationally recognized leader in distributed solar and storage, as well as roofing. We are more than just an electrical and roofing contractor; we are a movement, a lifestyle, and a community-focused family.
Our Award-Winning Excellence
At Good Faith Energy, we’re proud to be recognized for excellence and leadership in the renewable energy industry. In 2025, we were honored as REC Americas Installer of the Year – Central US, a testament to the hard work, technical expertise, and customer-first approach that define our team. We were also named a 2024 Solar Power World Top Contractor, recognizing our consistent performance, growth, and impact within the solar industry. Our commitment to innovation and community impact has also been celebrated through individual recognition: Mo Abdalla was named a D CEO Award Finalist in both 2024 and 2025, highlighting exceptional leadership and contributions to the industry. These awards reflect our ongoing dedication to quality, integrity, and sustainable energy solutions that make a lasting difference.

How Prepaid Solar PPAs Work
Prepaid solar Power Purchase Agreements provide a way for homeowners to go solar in 2026 while still benefiting from federal incentive value through a third-party structure.
- A solar partner installs the system on your home and maintains ownership during the initial term.
- You make a large upfront payment, usually covering about 70% of the total system cost, either in cash or through financing.
- The third-party owner claims the available federal solar tax incentive and factors that value directly into your system pricing.
- Your solar system produces energy at a predictable rate while monitoring, performance, and maintenance are handled for you.
- After the federal tax credit recapture period, typically six years, the system can often be transferred into your ownership.
- Once ownership transfers, you receive the full benefit of the system without ongoing payments.
This allows homeowners to reduce upfront solar costs and access incentive value even though direct homeowner tax credits are no longer available in 2026.

Prevailing Wage Compliance Analyst
Summary
As a Prevailing Wage Compliance Analyst in Greenskies’ Accounting team, you will report to the Assistant Controller to help ensure adherence to federal, state, and local labor standards, including prevailing wage, certified payroll, and apprenticeship requirements. This contract role plays a critical part in maintaining eligibility for tax incentives, protecting the company from compliance risk, and supporting smooth project execution across multiple job sites.
Location(s): Hybrid - North Haven, CT/Remote
Responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:
- Monitor and interpret prevailing wage laws including Davis Bacon Act, state wage determinations, and Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) labor requirements relevant to solar projects.
- Review certified payroll reports (CPRs) from subcontractors and internal teams for accuracy, completeness, and compliance.
- Verify worker classifications to ensure labor categories align with wage determinations and project scopes.
- Audit payroll documentation, including timesheets, pay stubs, fringe benefit records, and apprenticeship participation.
- Track apprenticeship utilization to ensure compliance with IRA apprenticeship requirements and state mandates.
- Communicate with subcontractors to resolve discrepancies, request missing documentation, and provide guidance on compliance expectations.
- Maintain organized compliance records for internal audits, external audits, and incentive program verification.
- Prepare compliance reports for project managers, legal teams, and executive leadership.
- Support project onboarding by educating subcontractors on prevailing wage requirements and documentation standards.
Qualifications:
- Experience with prevailing wage compliance, labor compliance, payroll auditing, or related regulatory roles.
- Familiarity with IRA prevailing wage & apprenticeship requirements.
- Strong understanding of construction labor classifications, especially within renewable energy or solar projects.
- Proficiency with certified payroll systems (e.g., LCPtracker, SURGE).
Contract Details:
- Contract position (full time or part time depending on project needs).
- Remote or hybrid
- Duration aligned with project timelines, typically 6–12 months with potential extension.
Forward resume and cover letter with salary requirements to: jobs_assetmanagement@greenskies.com

Solar Project Developer
Summary
The Solar Project Developer will be responsible for all aspects of both internal and external management of developing distributed generation solar projects. This position will report to the Director of Development and work with a team of dedicated solar developers and engineers. The role will involve cross departmental communication with finance, engineering, and construction teams. Location(s): North Haven, CT

- Ensure that assigned projects secure all need interconnection agreements, use permits, environmental permits, land entitlement, and revenue contracts.

Closed New Haven, CT Landfill to be Converted to Solar Panel Field
A former New Haven, Conn., landfill is being converted to a hub for a new solar project.
Waste360 | Staff
September 4, 2025
A former New Haven, Conn., landfill is being converted to a hub for a new solar project.
The new project was announced this week and will see the installation of nearly 2,000 solar panels on top of the old, closed landfill. The solar field will be able to generate more than one megawatt of clean electricity.
New Haven has partnered with Greenskies Clean Energy to develop the project, and when it's complete, the company will lease the site, sell the electricity back into the grid, and send monthly payments back to the city.
“The city will get a check for $6,000 a month for the next 20 years. So that will help with budget pressures,” said Steven Winter, executive director of the city’s Office of Climate & Sustainability.

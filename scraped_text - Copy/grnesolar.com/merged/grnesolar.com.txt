Nelnet Renewable Energy Has Joined
MARS Energy Group
Nelnet Renewable Energy has been acquired by MARS Energy Group, a national leader in commercial and utility-scale solar solutions, engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) services, and long-term energy infrastructure.
The acquisition by MARS will expand the services, reach, and support for our customers—offering enhanced national coverage, deeper technical and financial capabilities, and a unified team committed to reliability, transparency, and long-term performance.

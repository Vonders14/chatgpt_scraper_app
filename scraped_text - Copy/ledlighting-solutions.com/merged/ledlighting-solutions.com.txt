Frequently Asked Questions
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q. What are LEDs?
A light-emitting diode (LED) is an electronic light source. LEDs are based on the semiconductor diode. When the diode is forward biased (switched on), electrons are able to recombine with holes and energy is released in the form of light. This effect is called electroluminescence and the color of the light is determined by the energy gap of the semiconductor. The LED is usually small in area (less than 1 mm2) with integrated optical components to shape its radiation pattern and assist in reflection.
In other words LEDs are the revolutionary way to directly convert electrical energy to visible light. This is unlike conventional light sources (light bulbs), which use extreme heat (wasted energy) to generate the light.
Q. What is Color Temperature?
An LED’s color temperature is measured in kelvins (K). Color temperatures greater than 5500K are "cool white" with a blue/green color. Color temperatures of 3000-5500K are “neutral white” while color temperatures of 2700–3000 K are "warm white" with a yellow/red color.
A common complaint regarding LED lighting is that it appears too ‘blue’. Some manufacturers of LED lighting solutions utilize the higher temperature LEDs (blue color) due to their high lumen per watt output.
Standard LED lights are produced in the ‘Neutral White’ color. This color temperature closely resembles the spectrum of natural daylight.
Q. What is Luminance, Illuminance and Foot Candles?
Luminance is the intensity of light per unit area of its source. Luminance does not change with distance from the light.
Illuminance, or Light Level, is the total luminous flux incident on a surface/work plane, per unit area. The work plane is where the most important tasks in the room or space are performed (ie. The desk surface in an office). Illuminance does change with distance from the light. When discussing light levels required in offices, classrooms, etc… at task level illuminance is the relevant term.
Illuminance is measured in foot candles (ftcd, fc, fcd) (or lux in the metric SI system). A foot candle is actually one lumen of light density per square foot, one lux is one lumen per square meter.
Q. What is a Conversion or Retrofit?
Conversion kits are a quick and simple way to convert and upgrade your existing lighting fixtures to extremely energy efficient LED lighting. Maintaining the existing fixture minimizes time, cost and waste.
Q.What are the real benefits of LEDs?
Energy Savings – LED lights products save from 50% to 80% over existing technologies such as fluorescent, metal halide, incandescent and HID (high intensity discharge) light bulbs.
No Mercury or Lead – Disposal of LEDs does not contaminate the environment or poison our wildlife.
Long Life – Eliminates replacement and maintenance costs throughout its life of 50,000+ hours.
Minimal Heat Generation – Reduces air conditioning costs.
Directional Illuminance – Can eliminate light pollution by aiming the fixture and LED in the desired direction– Can eliminate light pollution by aiming the fixture and LED in the desired direction
Life Span Not Affected by “On/Off” – The life of conventional light sources is diminished each time they are cycled on and off, but this is not the case with LEDs, which see no reduction in life due to cycling.
Instant On – LEDs turn on instantly and do not require a ‘start up’ time. HID light bulbs require a start up time of up to several minutes. This is a significant benefit for industrial applications where delayed ‘on’ times can cause major disruption.
Q. What is Mercury?
Mercury is a highly toxic metal that is harmful to both humans and wildlife. Exposure to even small amounts of mercury, over a long period of time, may cause negative health effects including damage to the brain, kidneys and lungs.
Unborn and young children are especially susceptible to mercury exposure because their nervous systems are still developing. Whether it is absorbed through the skin, inhaled or ingested, exposure to mercury should be taken very seriously.
Mercury is classified as a toxic substance.
Q. What types of light sources contain mercury?
Mercury is present in several types of conventional lighting including:
- Fluorescent and compact fluorescent bulbs.
- High intensity discharge bulbs (HID). The following are HID bulbs:
- Mercury Vapor
- Metal Halide
- High Pressure Sodium

Traffic Signs
Traffic Signs: Essential Components for Road Safety and Traffic Management
Traffic signs are crucial elements in our transportation infrastructure that provide vital information to drivers, pedestrians, and all road users. At LED Lighting Solutions, we offer a comprehensive range of high-quality traffic signs designed to enhance road safety, guide traffic flow, and ensure clear communication on roadways of all types. Our collection includes regulatory, warning, guide, and informational signs manufactured to meet the highest industry standards and specifications.
Understanding Traffic Sign Categories
Traffic signs are categorized based on their function and purpose. Each category serves a specific role in maintaining order and safety on our roads. Our extensive inventory includes all major categories to meet your traffic management needs:
Regulatory Signs
Regulatory signs inform road users of traffic laws and regulations that must be followed. These signs are typically rectangular or circular with specific color schemes that indicate their level of authority.
- Stop Signs
- Our octagonal red stop signs with white lettering are manufactured with high-intensity reflective materials for maximum visibility in all conditions.
- Yield Signs
- Triangular yield signs feature retroreflective surfaces that maintain visibility during nighttime and adverse weather conditions.
- Speed Limit Signs
- Available in various speed designations, our speed limit signs are constructed with durable materials that resist fading and weathering.
- No Parking Signs
- Clear and authoritative no parking signs help manage traffic flow and ensure emergency access where needed.
Warning Signs
Warning signs alert drivers to potential hazards or changing road conditions ahead. These diamond-shaped signs typically feature a yellow background with black symbols or text.
- Curve and Turn Signs
- Pedestrian Crossing Signs
- School Zone Signs
- Railroad Crossing Signs
- Deer Crossing and Wildlife Warning Signs
- Slippery When Wet Signs
Guide Signs
Guide signs provide directional information and help motorists navigate to destinations. These signs are essential for efficient traffic flow in both urban and rural settings.
- Directional Arrows
- Street Name Signs
- Mile Markers
- Exit Signs
- Destination Distance Signs
Informational Signs
Informational signs communicate various services, facilities, and points of interest to travelers.
- Rest Area Signs
- Hospital and Emergency Services Signs
- Gas, Food, and Lodging Signs
- Recreational Area Signs
- Historic Site Markers
Traffic Sign Materials and Construction
The effectiveness of traffic signs depends greatly on their construction quality and materials used. Our signs are manufactured using industry-leading materials and techniques:
Reflective Materials
Visibility is paramount for traffic signs, especially during nighttime and adverse weather conditions. Our signs utilize various grades of reflective sheeting:
- Engineer Grade - Economical option with 7-year warranty, suitable for less critical applications
- High Intensity Prismatic (HIP) - Superior brightness with 10-year warranty, ideal for most roadway applications
- Diamond Grade - Premium reflectivity with up to 12-year warranty, perfect for high-speed roadways and critical safety applications
Substrate Options
Our traffic signs are available on various substrates to suit different applications and budgets:
- Aluminum - Durable, weather-resistant material ideal for permanent installations
- Aluminum Composite Material (ACM) - Lightweight yet rigid material with excellent durability
- Fiberglass - Impact-resistant and non-conductive, perfect for areas with extreme weather conditions
- Corrugated Plastic - Economical option for temporary signage needs
Enhanced Visibility Options
To further improve the effectiveness of traffic signs, especially in challenging visibility conditions, we offer several enhancement options:
LED-Enhanced Traffic Signs
Our LED-enhanced traffic signs combine traditional reflective surfaces with strategic LED lighting to dramatically increase visibility and driver compliance. These signs are particularly effective at hazardous intersections, pedestrian crossings, and school zones.
Solar-Powered Options
For locations without easy access to power infrastructure, our solar-powered traffic signs provide reliable illumination without the need for electrical connections. These environmentally friendly options include:
- Solar-powered flashing beacons
- Self-contained LED-illuminated signs
- Radar speed feedback signs with solar charging
Customization and Compliance
All our traffic signs are manufactured to meet or exceed the specifications outlined in the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD). We understand that different jurisdictions may have specific requirements, which is why we offer customization options:
Size Options
Traffic signs are available in various standard sizes based on the roadway type and speed limit:
- Residential streets: 24" or 30" signs
- Conventional roads: 36" signs
- Expressways: 48" signs
- Freeways: 60" or larger signs
Custom Graphics and Text
In addition to standard traffic signs, we can produce custom regulatory, warning, or informational signs to address specific needs in your community or facility. Our design team can assist with creating compliant custom signs that effectively communicate your message.
Applications for Traffic Signs
Our traffic signs serve a wide range of applications across various sectors:
- Municipal Traffic Departments - Comprehensive signage solutions for city streets and local roads
- Highway Authorities - High-visibility signs for interstate highways and major thoroughfares
- School Zones - Enhanced safety signs for areas with high pedestrian traffic and young children
- Construction Zones - Temporary and permanent signs for work zones and detours
- Private Facilities - Custom traffic control for parking lots, corporate campuses, and private roads
- Parks and Recreation Areas - Directional and informational signs for visitors
Why Choose Our Traffic Signs
When you select traffic signs from LED Lighting Solutions, you're investing in products that offer:
- MUTCD compliance and industry-standard adherence
- Superior visibility in all weather and lighting conditions
- Durable construction that withstands environmental challenges
- Customization options to meet specific requirements
- Energy-efficient LED enhancement options
- Expert consultation to ensure you select the right signs for your needs
Get Started with Your Traffic Sign Solution
Effective traffic management begins with high-quality, properly installed traffic signs. SPEED RADAR SIGNS
What Are Speed Radar Signs?
Speed radar signs, also known as driver feedback signs or radar speed displays, are traffic-calming devices that use radar technology to detect and display the speed of approaching vehicles. These signs are designed to alert drivers when they are exceeding the posted speed limit and encourage safer driving behavior without the need for police enforcement.
Unlike speed cameras, radar speed signs do not issue tickets. Their purpose is to promote awareness and voluntary compliance through visual feedback.
Benefits of Using Radar Speed Signs
Reduce Speeding in High-Risk Areas
Speed radar signs are especially effective in school zones, residential streets, work zones, and high-traffic pedestrian areas. By providing instant feedback, they create a psychological trigger that prompts drivers to slow down.
Increase Public Safety
Numerous studies show that radar signs reduce vehicle speeds by 5 to 10 mph on average. Slower speeds contribute to fewer collisions and increased pedestrian safety.
Cost-Effective Traffic Calming
Compared to physical measures like speed bumps or additional enforcement personnel, radar signs are a low-maintenance, one-time investment that delivers long-term impact.
Data Collection Capabilities
Many radar speed signs can log vehicle speed data, traffic volume, and time-of-day activity. This data can help municipalities and property managers identify peak traffic times or areas with recurring speed violations.
Features to Consider When Choosing a Radar Speed Sign
- Radar Detection Range: Choose a sign that fits your road type and speed limit.
- Display Size & Brightness: Ensure visibility in all conditions, including bright sunlight and nighttime.
- Power Source: Options include solar-powered, battery-powered, or hardwired units.
- Mounting Type: Pole-mounted and trailer-mounted designs are available for fixed or mobile deployment.
- Data Logging: Useful for traffic studies or performance reporting.
- Compliance Options: Some signs include flashing beacons or strobe lights when a vehicle exceeds a certain speed threshold.
Common Applications
- School zones
- City streets
- Construction zones
- Industrial complexes
- Parking lots
- HOA communities
- Hospital and university campuses
Speed Radar Signs vs. Speed Cameras
Radar signs are not enforcement tools—they do not issue citations or take photos. Their purpose is preventive, not punitive. Speed cameras, on the other hand, capture license plates and are often used in ticketing programs.
FAQ: Radar Speed Signs Explained
- Can radar speed signs issue tickets?
- No. These signs do not have the legal authority or equipment to issue speeding tickets.
- Do radar speed signs have cameras?
- Most do not. Their function is to detect and display speed—not to monitor or record driver information.
- How do radar speed signs work?
- They use Doppler radar to measure vehicle speed and display the reading in real time on a digital screen.
- Are radar speed signs effective?
- Yes. Numerous studies show that drivers reduce their speed when confronted with real-time feedback.
- How much do radar speed signs cost?
- Prices vary based on features, but basic models typically start around $2,000 and can go up depending on data logging and power configurations.
- Can police use radar speed signs?
- Yes, but typically for speed awareness campaigns rather than enforcement. Law enforcement may also use them for traffic pattern studies.
- Do radar signs collect data?
- Many modern radar signs include traffic data recording capabilities, which can be useful for municipal planning.

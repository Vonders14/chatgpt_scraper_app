OUR VISION
Our vision is to develop, build and operate private utility-scale solar energy farms that provide sustainable, renewable, and cost-effective energy to global communities.
We believe that by harnessing the power of the sun, we can create a more sustainable future for our planet and for generations to come. We are committed to leading the charge toward a more sustainable tomorrow.

DEVELOPMENT OF TURNKEY RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECTS

Our expertise and experience make us the go-to choice for companies and organizations that want to reduce their carbon footprint while maximizing their energy efficiency.
ADVISORY AND CONSULTING SERVICES
MIRE Solar not only builds and maintains private utility scale solar energy farms, but we also provide advisory and consulting services to help our clients make the most out of their investment. With our expertise and experience, we can assist you every step of the way in your solar project.

SERVICES
Solar Solutions for a Sustainable Future
Our services help businesses and communities reduce their carbon footprint while saving money on energy costs. We strive to provide exceptional service and support to ensure that our clients receive the best possible experience. Choose MIRE Solar for your private utility scale solar energy needs and let us help you make the switch to sustainable energy.
DEVELOPMENT
Our turnkey renewable energy projects offer a comprehensive solution to your energy needs. We specialize in designing and developing private utility scale solar energy farms that are not only sustainable but also cost-effective. Our team of experienced professionals is committed to providing you with reliable and efficient solutions that can help you achieve your energy goals and reduce your carbon footprint.
ADVISORY AND CONSULTING SERVICES
At our Advisory and Consulting Services, we provide comprehensive advice and consultancy to clients during the development, construction, and operation phase of private utility scale solar energy farms. Our experienced team is here to help you navigate the challenges of the industry, providing guidance on everything from technology selection to regulatory compliance. Partner with us for success.
ENERGY SALES
Our Energy Sales services match sustainable energy producers with buyers who are looking to purchase renewable energy. We provide support throughout the entire process, from searching for bidders and buyers to negotiating and advising on the Power Purchase Agreement (PPA). Join us in our commitment to preserving the planet for future generations.

Through the use of photovoltaic solar energy, wind energy, and energy storage, we can maximize the efficiency and sustainability of our farms. Our cutting-edge technologies allow us to produce clean energy at a scale that is both impactful and cost-effective for our clients.

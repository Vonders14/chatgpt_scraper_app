Nokomis Energy operates in the Upper Midwest and provides a variety of clean energy development and consulting services.
Nokomis Energy is a Minnesota based energy developer whose mission is to accelerate clean energy deployment through equitable partnerships.
Through a combination of development and consulting services we use distributed energy to accelerate the deployment of clean, local energy.
Our approach is predicated on creating value for all stakeholders involved. Our commitment to you is as a partner from day one.
Nokomis Energy operates in the Upper Midwest and provides a variety of clean energy development and consulting services.
Each community has a choice on how they wish to participate in energy generation and purchasing. We pride ourselves on developing energy that fits your community.
Every partner at Nokomis brings a different element to the table. From financing to project development, technology innovation and long-term operation, the full scope is accounted for.
With 25+ years of national experience in clean energy, Nokomis Energy brings knowledge to help at every layer of development.
We take an open book approach in everything we do, making sure our services and costs are communicated clearly giving you full visibility into each project we do together.
We believe that as a Minnesota company with experience in the field of renewable energy, Nokomis Energy will help our school district meet our energy needs in a way that's sustainable and environmentally responsible and we're excited that our current agreement projects to a yearly savings of $60,000 that we can put toward educating our students.
“Nokomis Energy’s fixed savings rate made all the difference. We looked at Community Solar in the past but could not get comfortable with the long-term risks until now. We can now plan for savings, lowering our operating budget and enhancing our city services thanks to solar.”

Director, Government Affairs
Matt has more than a decade of experience in journalism, public policy, and government affairs roles in the public, private, and non-profit sectors. Why do you work in clean energy?
Minnesota has been a leader on clean energy for decades. We were one of the first states to pass a renewable energy standard, we have the nation’s leading community solar program, and many of the most successful clean energy businesses on the entire continent are based in Minnesota. But to meet our climate goals we know we have to so much more. As other segments of the economy get electrified, it will only drive even more demand for solar and other clean energy. I want to be part of making those ambitious clean energy goals and climate demands a reality.
What is the coolest part of your job?
So many people on the Nokomis team are the absolute best at what they do. Being able to learn from them day in and day out is easily one of the coolest parts of this job.

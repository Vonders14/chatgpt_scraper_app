Heating and Air Conditioning
NOVA Green Energy maintains, services, and installs all brands of mini splitst, heat pumps, gas furnaces, and air conditioning systems. We offer a full product line from American Standard, Carrier, Mitsubishi. All major brands come with a 10 year limited manufactures warranty. We have a crew with over thirty years of experience in this field. Contact us for maintenance, service and installation. If you wish to replace your existing system with a more energy efficiency model we will find the right one for your home.
We also offer a HERS energy efficiency assessment of your home to correct heating and cooling issues you may have as one of only a few licensed contractors in the DMV certified to do so.

Gas Fireplaces
NOVA Green Energy services and installs all brands of vented, vent free, and direct vent natural gas and propane fireplaces. We install nearly all brands, including RH Peterson, Archgard, Brigantia, Valor, Rasmussen, and Heat-N-Glo. To assess the technical feasibility and suitability of installing a gas fireplace, we come to your home to perform a site survey. The cost of this site survey is $150 and if installing a gas fireplace is technically feasible and safe, the site survey will result in a proposal.
We are certified as a gas fireplace specialist by the National Fireplace Institute. Contact us for help in choosing the right fireplace to add warmth, ambiance, and value to your home.

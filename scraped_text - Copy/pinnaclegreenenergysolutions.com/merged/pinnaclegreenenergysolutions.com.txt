PORTABLE TOWER SOLUTIONS
Mission-ready mobile towers. Deployed in minutes. Trusted in any condition.
Pinnacle Green Energy Solutions’ mobile towers are built to satisfy specific application requirements of height, rigidity, portability, rapid deployment, ease of use, and safe operations. These towers are built to support > 2,000 lbs. payload and are ready for every mission. These self-supporting, non-ground penetrating mobile towers can erect 80’ in only 5 minutes and are extremely stable and automatically lowers based on wind speed.
-
Our mobile/portable towers range from 20’ to 120’
-
Rapid deployment, set-up and breakdown
-
Non-ground penetrating guy wires (guy wires mount directly to the tower outriggers)
-
Wind sensors provide automatic tower lowering
-
Cable management system
-
Our towers are de-iced at ground level – no climbing required
-
Power/BMS systems include: diesel, propane or hydrogen fuel cells
TOWERS IN ACTION
Surviving the Storm: How Our Tower Withstood An Ice Storm
When an urgent need arose for an additional communications tower on a North Dakota oil field, we delivered a fully operational tower in just 48 hours. Soon after, a severe ice storm blanketed the site with nearly nine inches of ice, causing all surrounding towers to fail - except ours.
Our self-erecting tower stood strong. Engineered with lightweight aluminum construction and patented gear technology, it withstood an estimated 5,000 pounds of ice and continues to operate reliably today. Read our case study below to learn more.
Rapid-Deploy Tower Technology
Interested in seeing our towers in action? The PTM-130 Mobile Tactical Tower is a rugged, transportable tower system designed for fast setup, stability, and heavy-duty performance. Built to elevate communications, lighting, or surveillance gear up to 80 ft in minutes, the PTM-130 combines portability with payload capacity in a C-130-transportable package. We are committed to delivering dependable mobile tower technology for demanding environments and mission-critical applications.

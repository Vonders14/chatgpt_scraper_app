World-class solar panel warranty with premium coverage
REC solar panels are widely renowned for their premium quality, supported by our very low warranty claims rate. With that, REC confidently offers warranties that empower consumers: 20-year product warranty + 25-year power warranty. Plus, all REC panels are eligible for REC ProTrust*; a comprehensive package that offers a 25-year warranty for each of product, power, and labor.
-
Product warranty
REC panels come standard with a 20 year product warranty. This covers panel defects and promises that the panels will maintain their superior quality for at least 20 years. All panels are eligible for a +5 year product warranty extension, as per the REC ProTrust Warranty* -
Power warranty
REC’s power warranty protects you from power loss and ensures that REC panels perform exactly as expected to – every year for 25 years and enables greater ROI predictability. For example, REC Alpha panels promise 92% of nameplate power in year 25 – an industry best. -
Labor waranty
The labor warranty is unique to the REC ProTrust Warranty* and gives end customers added protection in the unlikely event that an REC panel needs to be serviced. For approved claims, REC will pay a fixed labor fee to an REC Certified Solar Professional installer.
The REC ProTrust Warranty covers product, power, and labor – and is exclusively offered by REC Certified Solar Professional installers.
Consumers with installations up to 25 kW enjoy maximum comfort and peace of mind knowing that they can have a 25 year warranty for each of product, performance, and labor. Systems between 25-500 kW can have the same 25 year product and performance warranties, plus 10 year labor warranty. And if the original commissioning installer goes out of business, the REC ProTrust Warranty still applies; REC has you covered!

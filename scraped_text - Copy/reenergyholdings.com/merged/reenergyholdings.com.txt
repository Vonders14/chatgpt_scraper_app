About Us
A company dedicated to environmental sustainability.
ReEnergy Holdings LLC
ReEnergy Holdings LLC is a company dedicated to environmental sustainability that currently provides management services and strategic guidance to, and has an ownership interest in, ReSource Waste Services LLC, a leading company in the waste and recycling industry in eastern New England. ReSource Waste’s mission is to optimize the recovery of materials from waste streams while prioritizing workplace health and safety.
Company Background
ReEnergy Holdings was formed in 2008 by a senior management team comprised of four experienced industry professionals along with an energy-sector focused private equity fund. Prior to that time, the co-founders had decades of experience working together in growing and selling companies in the solid waste and waste-to-energy industries.
During ReEnergy’s 10+ year partnership with this private equity fund the company acquired, owned and/or operated biomass-to-energy facilities in New England, New York and North Carolina. ReEnergy also acquired, owned and operated facilities in eastern New England that process and recycle construction and demolition debris, with an eye toward recovery of wood that would be used as fuel in several of ReEnergy’s biomass-to-energy facilities.
In 2019, ReEnergy’s co-founders completed a management buyout of the private equity fund’s ownership interest in ReEnergy and at that point intentionally segregated its biomass-to-energy and waste & recycling businesses to set the stage for growing these businesses on separate and distinct paths. From that point forward ReEnergy and its co-founders provided strategic direction and corporate support services to both of its biomass and waste & recycling divisions.
In early 2021, ReEnergy established a partnership with another private equity/infrastructure fund in its biomass-to-energy company (renamed ReGenerate Energy Holdings) to pursue growth opportunities, the first of which was the acquisition of a large biomass-to-energy facility in Albany, Georgia in June 2021. ReEnergy sold its ownership interests in ReGenerate and two biomass-to-energy facilities in Maine to that fund in November 2023.
Current Activities
In late 2021, ReEnergy established a partnership with an affiliate of GCM Grosvenor in which GCM acquired a majority ownership interest in ReEnergy’s waste & recycling division (ReSource Waste Services), bringing industry experience and equity capital that is supporting the next chapter of ReSource Waste’s growth. The facilities owned and operated by ReSource Waste Services were acquired by ReEnergy over time: the Epping, NH and Salem, NH facilities in 2009; the Roxbury, MA facility in 2012; the Lewiston, ME facility in 2013; the Ware, MA facility in 2019; and then ReSource Waste Services acquired the Northeast Recycling (Avon, MA) collection company in 2024. Additional acquisitions and organic growth opportunities create an exciting future for ReSource Waste Services.

Gregory Leahey
Co-Founder
Greg Leahey is responsible for planning and executing growth opportunities for ReSource Waste Services. Most recently he served as President & Chief Operating Officer for ReSource Waste Services. He formerly served as Vice President of Recycling and Waste Services at EAC Operations, Inc. In that role, Greg had profit and loss responsibility for all of EAC’s waste procurement, waste collection, waste transfer, landfill and recycling activities. Greg also was responsible for the acquisition of a number of waste collection and transportation companies and negotiation of strategic waste and disposal contracts. Greg earned a bachelor’s degree in economics from Union College.

William Ralston
Co-Founder
William Ralston is a founder of ReEnergy Holdings. He was formerly General Counsel for EAC Operations, Inc. At EAC, he was involved in numerous acquisitions, project development initiatives, project restructurings and financings. Prior to joining EAC, he was in private practice with law firms in Albany, NY, with focus on the development and financing of independent power projects. Bill has a bachelor’s degree in economics and finance from McGill University, a juris doctor degree from Albany Law School and is a member of the New York State Bar.

Thomas Beck
Co-Founder
Thomas Beck is a founder of ReEnergy Holdings. He was a consultant to the solid waste and renewable energy industries for three years before the formation of ReEnergy Holdings. Before that, Tom was a Vice President of American Ref-Fuel Company and President of TransRiver Marketing Company. American Ref- Fuel was an owner and operator of waste-to- energy facilities and associated waste operations with annual revenues in excess of $500 million. Tom has a bachelor’s degree in economics from Union College and a master’s degree in business administration from Bentley University.

Larry Richardson
Co-Founder
Larry Richardson has significant experience as an executive in the renewable energy and waste-to-energy sectors. He is a founder of ReEnergy Holdings LLC and serves also as that company’s Chief Executive Officer. He formerly was President and Chief Operating Officer of EAC Operations, Inc., a company that owned and operated waste-to-energy facilities and related businesses for the collection, processing and transportation of solid waste and recyclable materials. Before working at EAC, he held management, project development and technical positions at companies including ABB/Combustion Engineering, Halliburton/Brown & Root and HDR Engineering. Larry is a licensed professional engineer. He has a bachelor’s degree in civil engineering from University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

Creating Sustainable Income for our Landowner Partners
When you sign a lease agreement with Scout, you are participating in an opportunity to harvest natural resources and earn a secondary long-term, reliable income. Since renewable energy projects typically operate for up to four decades, these annual payments can last well into retirement and future generations. Scout has extensive experience developing and operating projects that have involved dozens of communities and thousands of property owners including family farms, ranches, business owners, veterans, and retirees.

Bernie joined the wind industry in the mid 80’s working for Enertech Wind Systems, one of the few U.S. wind turbine manufacturers, erecting and operating roughly 500 machines. From there his career has followed several paths, like green field micro siting to maximize AEP, application engineering to improve budgetary numbers, performed construction project management in various countries, operated diverse wind turbine technologies, developed SCADA platforms to improve project performance, refurbished several hundred two-stage planetary gearboxes to improve service life, assisted with several structural component retrofit programs, performed forensic analysis of failed components and authored many operating procedures.

At Scout he has raised over $4.5B of capital across M&A, corporate, project level debt and tax equity and played an integral part in managing the company sale to Brookfield in 2022 – establishing Scout as one of the premier renewable IPPs. URL: https://www.scoutcleanenergy.com/team/michael-rucker-2

Michael Rucker is the Founder and CEO of Scout Clean Energy, a renewable energy owner-operator with 1,200 MW of operating projects and a 19 GW development pipeline of wind, solar and storage projects spanning 24 US states.
Michael was co-founder and formerly President of Harvest Energy Services, Inc. (sold to Takkion) which specialized in O&M and asset management services. Michael previously served as CEO of juwi Wind North America, a German wind developer, and as VP of Business Development at Clipper Windpower Development. Prior to that, he was a Commercial Director at GE Energy for west region wind, hydro, and natural gas turbine sales. Other positions included Director at APX for renewable energy certificate trading, power scheduling, and settlements. He also supported the UN Climate Change negotiations at the International Energy Agency in Paris as manager of the Climate Technology Initiative program. Michael was also a Manager at the Edison Electric Institute, the trade association for the US investor-owned utilities.

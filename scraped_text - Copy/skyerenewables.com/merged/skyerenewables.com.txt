One of Skye Renewables’ most significant partners is LCC, an operator of malls and supermarkets in the province of Albay, Philippines. In this video, their President, Ronaldo Tan, discusses their decision to deploy on-site solar across their buildings.
Honda Philippines Selects
Skye Renewables as Solar Partner
April 2024
Skye Renewables has completed a rooftop solar system at the Honda Philippines manufacturing site in Tanaua. The eco-friendly initiative is designed to harness the abundant sunlight that the Philippines enjoys year-round, providing a sustainable source of electricity. This aligns with Honda’s sustainability ambitions and will significantly reduce the carbon intensity of their operations. The team at Skye Philippines, headed up by Gary Espino, poured their hearts and minds into this project. This is the beginning of an exciting move forward in the future of renewable energy in the Philippines.
Decathlon Deploys Solar as
Part of Sustainability Push
May 2024
Skye Renewables was selected by Decathlon Philippines to develop the rooftop solar system at their flagship store in Manila. As one of the world’s leading retailers, Decathlon strives to be as sustainable as possible across all aspects of their operations. Skye’s rooftop solar, developed under a long-term PPA contract, forms a key part of their energy strategy.

URL: https://www.skyerenewables.com/british-international-investment-plc-invests-in-skye-renewables/

British International Investment (BII), the UK’s development finance institution (DFI) , and Idemitsu Kosan (IKC), one of Japan’s leading energy companies, today announced a joint investment into Skye Renewables Energy Pte Ltd. (Skye), a South-East Asia-focused renewable energy platform, forming a strong partnership to support the development of greenfield solar projects and decarbonisation of the region.
Drawing from BII and IKC’s extensive track record in the renewable energy sector globally, the investment aims to support the region’s sustainability goals by transforming Skye into a scaled- up regional renewable energy platform with a solar capacity of over 300MW in the coming years.

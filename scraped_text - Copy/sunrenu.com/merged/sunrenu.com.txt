SunRenu Solar has become one of the more prominent commercial solar installers in Arizona. Starting the business out of a kitchen in 2008, SunRenu has grown to over 20 full and part time staff and growing. We are big enough to handle the large scale solar projects, but small enough to give the proper attention and act quickly. Each project we care for puts the clients needs first and foremost, and we provide the best value in the industry. By 2013 our growth provides multi megawatts of solar design, installation, and finance experience. Our culture and attitude are bringing back the lost art of “doing what we say we are going to do”. Despite the shrinking incentive markets, Sunrenu has been consistently growing and hiring quality individuals to join the team.
Having much experience and a highly successful track record of providing a turnkey financing solution for our renewable energy projects. Our next venture is to raise capital from small businesses to provide tax credits and fixed income streams on an affordable basis. SunRenu’s sister company, RE3 Capital provides the contracts, legal, and fund management to a defined renewable energy investment.
As a practical visionary, let our family of companies help add value to your business or property. We will get it done and won’t fumble at the goal line. Our teams experience and integrity speaks for itself and we’ll work hard for your business and partnership.

SunRenu Solar has become one of the more prominent commercial solar installers in Arizona. Starting the business out of a kitchen in 2008, SunRenu has grown to over 20 full and part time staff and growing. We are big enough to handle the large scale solar projects, but small enough to give the proper attention and act quickly. Each project we care for puts the clients needs first and foremost, and we provide the best value in the industry. By 2013 our growth provides multi megawatts of solar design, installation, and finance experience. Our culture and attitude are bringing back the lost art of “doing what we say we are going to do”. Despite the shrinking incentive markets, Sunrenu has been consistently growing and hiring quality individuals to join the team.
Having much experience and a highly successful track record of providing a turnkey financing solution for our renewable energy projects. Our next venture is to raise capital from small businesses to provide tax credits and fixed income streams on an affordable basis. SunRenu’s sister company, RE3 Capital provides the contracts, legal, and fund management to a defined renewable energy investment.
As a practical visionary, let our family of companies help add value to your business or property. We will get it done and won’t fumble at the goal line. Our teams experience and integrity speaks for itself and we’ll work hard for your business and partnership.

OUR PROCESS
Going solar is easy
Meet with a Solar Expert
During your in-home visit, we verify that your roof qualifies for solar, calculate how much you could save on energy costs, and discuss incentives.
Leave the design and permits to us
We custom-design your solar system for maximum energy production and savings. We work with state and local officials, your utility and HOA to pull the permits you need.
Expert installation
Certified, highly trained pros install your system in 1 - 3 days. Your Project Concierge works with your utility to connect your system to the grid, keeping you informed at every step.
Start saving
Once your system is up and running, you’ll watch your electricity costs drop. Over the years, these savings really add up.
Individual savings will vary based on factors such as energy consumption, incentives eligibility and weather. You will receive separate solar and utility bills. Estimates are based on utility rate at time of purchase and example rate changes (based on historical data and national averages). Changes in utility rates could affect overall estimated savings. Trinity does not guarantee savings.
Going solar starts with a free consultation
During this 60-min visit, a Trinity Solar Expert will:
- Give you a customized quote and design proposal
- Assess your roof to confirm it qualifies for solar
- Analyze past utility bills to see how much electricity you need
- Calculate how much you could save with solar
- Present payment and financing options
- Let you know which incentives may be available to you
It costs nothing to meet with us, and this brief conversation could help you save big on energy expenses.

ABOUT TRUE POWER SOLAR
"Being a Partner You Can Trust"
At True Power Solar we aim to be our clients’ long-term partners by providing efficient customized energy solutions for their residential properties. Exceeding their expectations all while increasing environmental sustainability through California.
OUR STORY
Since 2011 - True Power Solar has been a leading Residential Solar Contractor headquartered in Southern California. Our company was founded in the city of Brea, CA and with our team's strong skill and dedication, we began to grow. Our team's continuous hard work allowed us to relocate our headquarters in 2016 with our very own warehouse facility in Pomona, CA. Our projects are completed with diligence. Our team strives on providing our customers with the best energy solution for their homes. With over 12 years of experience in the industry, we have helped well over 4,000 residential customers go solar.
OUR MISSION
True Power Solar’s mission is to deliver quality energy solutions tailored to our customer’s unique needs. We are committed to promoting environmental sustainability and providing exceptional customer service. Through innovation, expertise, and a dedication to excellence, we strive to make energy solutions accessible and beneficial for homeowners, communities, and businesses.
OUR VISION
True Power Solar aims to lead the transition to sustainable energy by becoming the most trusted provider of customized energy solutions, empowering homeowners, communities and businesses to harness the power of the sun for a cleaner, greener & better future.

HERE FOR YOUR SOLAR NEEDS
True Power Solar is an experienced & local contractor you can trust. We are dedicated to helping our clients find the best energy solution for their home or commercial needs. True Power Solar prides itself in the ability to provide experienced, efficient, and sleek designs, and equipment. You can rest assured that we will make sure your needs and expectations are surpassed.
TRUST THE PROCESS. TRUST IN US.
At True Power Solar our team of Solar Consultants is dedicated to educating consumers of the products available to them and helping you discover if solar is the right energy solution for you. We take our clients step by step through their solar journey. We want you to know what will be installed on your roof and we value transparency between both parties. This is not a transaction, it's a lifelong partnership.
ENERGY ANALYSIS AND DESIGN
After discussing your needs and energy usage, our skilled technicians will go assess your roof. They will compile any useful information about your roof that could be helpful for us to know. Our skilled technicians take the actual measurements of your roof space and the amount of solar radiation that can be harvested from it. Roof measurements, solar access, azimuth, and tilt are then given to our engineering department to create the perfect custom Solar PV Design for your property.
SYSTEM INSTALLATION
Our team of highly skilled technicians with experience & years of dedication will go to your property to install your new energy system. They have experience installing various different kinds of solar solutions on different roof types. Our team ensures that our projects are left clean & intact. We treat your home like our own.
MONITORING SET UP
During the finalization of your project we help you set up a monitoring system that will allow you to track your system's production.

Simulating Real-world Conditions to Prepare Your Power Systems
L2 to L5 commissioning validates that power and cooling systems are properly installed, integrated, and stress-tested, ensuring your data center is ready to go live with confidence.
Commissioning Services
L2
Component Verification
Individual power components like switchgear, UPS, and generators are inspected and tested to confirm proper installation and operation.
L3
System Verification
Subsystems are tested together to ensure proper wiring, sequence of operations, and readiness for startup.
L4
Functional System Testing
Integrated systems are tested under simulated conditions—like load changes and power loss—to confirm full system functionality and failover performance.
L5
Full-Scale Black Start Test
The entire electrical infrastructure is tested during a simulated total power outage to validate end-to-end performance, recovery, and resilience.
You gain confidence that your data center is ready to go live — without overspending or waiting on utility delays. Our commissioning process proves your power and cooling systems can deliver grid quality performance under real-world stress, so you can launch on schedule and without compromise.

Case Study
VoltaGrid’s Rapid Deployment of Microgrid Solutions for a Data Center in the Southwest United States
A leading data center in the Southwest United States faced a critical situation when its planned utility power fell through due to supply chain issues and unforeseen conditions.
Behind the Meter (BTM) is a configuration that allows data centers to get power directly from a power facility on the same site. This enables the data center to be independent of the utility grid for most of its power needs, providing several benefits for these energy-intensive operations. BTM can help data centers avoid grid connection queues, offer operational resilience during grid disruptions, and protect against fluctuations in energy prices.
BTM can be established through a Power Delivery Agreement (PDA), where an energy provider installs generation equipment on the site, and the business pays a predetermined rate for power over time.

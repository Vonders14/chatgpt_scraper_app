If you think all energy providers are alike, think again. At Clearview Energy, not only do we work hard to power your home, we’re working hard to help create a sustainable world—a world we’ll be proud to hand down to future generations.
To know why we're here, you have to know where we come from. Read about our history, how we came to champion the sustainable lifestyle, and what we stand for.
There are plenty of electricity suppliers out there, so what makes Clearview Energy so special? Here you'll learn what sets us apart and what we're doing to make this a better world.
We can't create a sustainable future on our own. It takes the work of a number of organization who share our same values. Organizations like the Shaw Institute, working to protect our beaches in the Northeast.

The Shaw Institute
Founded in 1990 by ecotoxicologist Dr. Susan Shaw, the Shaw Institute's research on plastics, ocean pollution, marine mammal health, toxic chemicals, and climate change has informed public opinion and fueled policy, impacting millions of people in the U.S. and worldwide.

URL: https://www.clearviewenergy.com/who-we-are/

Like other energy providers, Clearview Energy offers electric and natural gas plans to residential and business customers. And we also try to offer the most competitive plans with the best customer service.
But it goes beyond that.
We understand that as an energy provider, our actions—both as a company and as individuals—can have a serious affect on our environment. That’s why we’ve made it our mission to go well beyond many of our competitors and work towards creating a sustainable future.
How? By supporting organizations who work to protect (or repair) our environment. Organizations like the Shaw Institute, who work to connect environmental health with human health through innovative science, collaboration and community outreach.
And we offer the tools you need to help make smarter, more sustainable choices.
We know you want to do your part to promote sustainability. And at Clearview Energy, we’re here to help you do just that.

Clearview Energy is an electric and natural gas supplier for residential and business customers in states which allow you to purchase the electricity or gas you use from someone other than your distribution utility. As a Clearview customer, you can choose from a variety of plans from short-term to long-term guaranteed pricing. You buy your electricity and/or natural gas from Clearview Energy, but your utility is still responsible for delivering the energy to you safely. For more information on what plans are available in your area, see our
Service Areas. All of Clearview’s electricity plans support renewable energy production nationally.

Clearview Energy is an electric and natural gas supplier for residential and business customers in states which allow you to purchase the electricity or gas you use from someone other than your distribution utility. As a Clearview customer, you can choose from a variety of plans from short-term to long-term guaranteed pricing. You buy your electricity and/or natural gas from Clearview Energy, but your utility is still responsible for delivering the energy to you safely. For more information on what plans are available in your area, see our
Service Areas. All of Clearview’s electricity plans support renewable energy production nationally.

Clearview offers competitive rates and a variety of energy plans. We have a plan that’s right for you and your family or business!
Basic service is market-priced electricity provided by your local distribution utility (LDU) if you have not chosen a competitive supplier. You may use the pricing and term information for Basic Service as a means of comparison to Clearview’s product offerings. You may or may not be charged a fee from your LDU when you choose a Competitive Power Supplier. Please contact your LDU for information on Basic Service rates and any associated fees if you have questions.

ABOUT COLITE
Our Story
The Evolution of Sustainability
Founded in 2018, Colite Technologies is a trusted commercial solar company dedicated to helping commercial and industrial customers incorporate sustainable energy solutions into their operations. Our unique portfolio of commercial solar PV systems, off-grid renewable lighting systems, and EV charging equipment empowers businesses across the U.S. to improve energy efficiency and minimize carbon footprints.
All of our projects are fully turnkey, designed, engineered, and installed by Colite Technologies. Based in Columbia, South Carolina, our in-house team is supported by a Professional Engineer and Master Electricians. From initial engineering design to final installation, our NABCEP-certified employees bring expertise, professionalism, and a personalized approach to every project. As sustainable energy solutions continue to evolve, Colite Technologies remains committed to adopting the latest innovations, ensuring our clients benefit from cutting-edge technology and the highest standards of quality and efficiency.

Extra Space Storage – Northwood

Extra Space Storage – Northwood Rooftop Solar Project
Roof Mounted Solar PV System
- Provides Huge Boost for the Environment and Serious Savings
- 101.2 kW rooftop solar system
- Generates 152.42 megawatt hours of power annually
- 25-year lifespan
- Offsets 68.7% of the facility’s energy use
- First year savings of nearly $16,000
- Non-penetrative installation – roof’s original warranty unaffected!
Save Money By Going Green With Solar Energy
Helps Extra Space Storage Meet Their Sustainability Goals
With 101kW of power generation, this system reduces the facility’s CO2 output by 2,501 metric tons. This is the same amount of CO2 generated by driving 6.3 million miles in passenger car or by burning 2.7 million pounds of coal.
Major Cost Savings on Power Bills
The Northwood rooftop solar project offsets 68.7% of the total power needs of the facility, saving almost $16,000 in the first year; as energy costs rise so too will the annual savings. The project is eligible for federal and state energy tax incentives, helping jumpstart Extra Space Storage’s savings.
Long Lasting American Made Photovoltaic Panels
The 238 high performance panels were each made in the USA and will deliver 25 years of life. The onsite monitoring system allows for real time tracking of power production, making it easy to see the system’s performance at a glance and troubleshoot any problems if they arise.
Leak-Proof Rooftop Solar PV System Installation
Our ballasted racking system allowed the system to be installed without penetration. This was imperative for Extra Space Storage as it allowed them to retain their roof’s existing warranty.

URL: https://colitetech.com/blog/what-businesses-should-know-about-solar-module-degradation-and-long-term-performance/

Every business investing in solar wants one thing: predictable, long-term solar performance. When companies evaluate commercial solar for cost savings, energy independence, or operational stability, consistency matters. But even the highest-quality systems face a natural, gradual decline in output over time—a predictable process known as Solar Module Degradation.
Understanding how this works, what impacts it, and how to plan for it is essential for forecasting energy savings, optimizing system design, and ensuring the best return on investment over the next 25–30 years. Without this knowledge, businesses risk underestimating long-term performance projections, misinterpreting year-over-year generation changes, or overlooking maintenance strategies that could preserve thousands of dollars in energy value.
This guide breaks down everything companies need to know about Solar Module Degradation—including how the solar panel lifespan, environment, and system conditions affect energy generation year after year.
What Is Solar Module Degradation—and Why It Happens
All commercial solar systems experience a slow reduction in energy production as they age. Solar Module Degradation is the unavoidable decline in performance that occurs over the life of a module, and while it cannot be fully eliminated, it can be effectively minimized with quality equipment and proper long-term planning.
Why Solar Modules Degrade
Solar modules work continuously in environments that expose them to significant natural stress. Several environmental and mechanical factors contribute to gradual efficiency loss:
-
UV exposure that slowly breaks down module materials.
-
Thermal cycling—the constant heating and cooling that stresses internal components.
-
Moisture intrusion that impacts encapsulants or junction boxes over time.
-
Mechanical wear caused by wind, storms, or repeated structural pressure.
-
Microcracks in cells that reduce conductivity gradually.
These elements all influence the solar degradation rate, determining how quickly the output declines and how the system will perform throughout its lifecycle.
How Much Performance Loss Should Businesses Expect?
For modern commercial systems, degradation rates are significantly lower than they were a decade ago. Today’s panels degrade at an average rate of 0.3%–0.8% per year, depending on manufacturing quality, climate, and module technology. This means that after 25–30 years, most commercial systems still retain 85–90% of their original rated output.
This stability is one of the biggest reasons companies choose commercial solar—because even with Solar Module Degradation, long-term predictability remains strong.
When businesses model their energy savings or calculate payback periods, these degradation rates become central to financial projections and operational planning.
Understanding the Solar Degradation Rate
Your system’s solar degradation rate determines how fast performance decreases year over year. Different module technologies experience different degradation behaviors:
-
Monocrystalline panels: Typically show the lowest degradation rate.
-
Polycrystalline panels: Slightly higher, but still stable long term.
-
Thin-film panels: Higher initial degradation but stable performance after the first phase.
Choosing the right module during your Commercial Solar Installation is a major factor in minimizing long-term performance loss. Businesses evaluating installation options can find guidance here:
Solar Panel Lifespan—How Long Do Modules Really Last?
While warranties commonly reference 25 years, the actual solar panel lifespan can extend far beyond that. Many modules continue operating efficiently for 30–35 years or more, especially when supported by proper maintenance.
Warranty Benchmarks Matter
Typical commercial module warranties include:
-
Product warranty: 10–12 years covering manufacturing defects.
-
Performance warranty: 25–30 years guaranteeing 80–90% of the original capacity.
This performance warranty directly reflects expected Solar Module Degradation and helps businesses understand what level of long-term solar performance they can count on.
Types of Solar Module Degradation
Not all degradation looks the same. Understanding the types helps facility managers anticipate system changes and address them promptly.
Light-Induced Degradation (LID)
Occurs during the initial days of sunlight exposure. This chemical interaction between silicon and light causes a small power drop but stabilizes quickly.
Potential-Induced Degradation (PID)
Caused by voltage leakage within the module, often influenced by high humidity, grounding issues, or module design. PID-resistant modules minimize this risk.
Light and Elevated Temperature Degradation (LeTID)
Affects some high-efficiency modules, especially in high-heat environments. Performance improves when preventive design measures are taken.
Mechanical Degradation
Storms, snow loads, rooftop vibration, dust buildup, and poor racking systems contribute to:
-
Microcracks
-
Frame corrosion
-
Hot spots
-
Loosened connectors
Over time, these issues add up, increasing commercial solar efficiency loss and decreasing predictable energy output.

For businesses, the impact of degradation goes beyond reduced electricity generation. It directly affects financial savings, operational reliability, and strategic planning.

-

-
More dependable lifecycle cost analysis
Operational Stability
A system designed with realistic degradation modeling experiences predictable solar system performance over time, allowing companies to plan for future operations confidently and avoid surprises.

Installation Quality

Maintenance Practices
Routine inspections, performance monitoring, and quick repairs help sustain long-term solar performance and keep losses low.
How Businesses Can Reduce Solar Module Degradation
While degradation can’t be stopped, businesses have significant control over how quickly it occurs.
Invest in High-Quality Modules
Lower-cost equipment frequently degrades faster. Look for modules with:
-
Tier 1 manufacturer ratings
-
Strong 25–30 year performance warranties
-
PID-resistant and LeTID-resistant technologies
Choose a Skilled EPC Partner

Renewable Lighting Case Study:
South Carolina Research Authority
Colite Technologies and SCRA have partnered to replace a total of 32 older on grid lighting with renewable lighting systems over six phases. Reduced power usage will result in electricity costs savings of roughly $12,600 over the life of the units and close to 196 metric tons of carbon dioxide emissions will be avoided.
Colite Technologies and SCRA are replacing 32 on-grid lights with renewable systems across six phases, saving about $12,600 in electricity costs and avoiding nearly 196 metric tons of CO₂ over their lifespan.
Project Overview
- The South Carolina Research Authority (SCRA) was chartered in 1983 as a public, nonprofit corporation to foster innovation in SC’s economy.
- Carolina Research Park was established over 25 years ago as a state-of-the-art industrial park in Columbia, SC.
- A significant portion of the existing on grid lighting in the park is nonfunctional; repair and replacement would be costly and time consuming.
- SCRA replaced a total of 32 older on grid lighting with renewable lighting systems over six phases.
- Lighting systems are totally off grid and self-sustaining no costly rewiring or trenching required.
- Project is eligible for 26% federal investment tax credit and 25% South Carolina state tax credit for renewable energy systems.
- Reduced power usage will result in electricity costs savings of roughly $12,600 over the life of the units.
- Close to 196 metric tons of carbon dioxide emissions will be avoided over 20 years.
- Certain units feature a WiFi connected camera surveillance system for enhanced security in key areas of
the park. - Custom branded signage draws visitors’ attention and promotes the SCRA name.
Save Money By Going Green With Solar Energy
Helps SCRA Meet Their Sustainability Goals
Over their 25-year life, these 32 renewable lighting systems operating on average 12 hours a day will avoid over 196 metric tons of carbon dioxide emissions—equivalent to greenhouse gas emissions from driving 499,126 miles in an average passenger car or burning 217,720 lbs. of coal.
Major Cost Savings on Power Bills
These self-contained units are completely off-grid, generating all of their electricity from the sun, and incorporate battery storage to ensure reliable operation throughout the night. No extension of the grid and no monthly utility energy costs
Long Lasting American Made Photovoltaic Panels
The 32 high performance panels were each made in the USA and will deliver 25 years of life. The onsite monitoring system allows for real time tracking of power production, making it easy to see the system’s performance at a glance and troubleshoot any problems if they arise.
On the Cutting Edge of Innovation
The technological and economic viability of renewable powered lighting for outdoor applications such as parking lots, roadways, pedestrian pathways, and parks has definitely arrived.

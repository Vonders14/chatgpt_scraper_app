Success Story
Oakwood Friends School Solar Project
The Oakwood Friends School always wanted solar but had been told that their 21 shaded buildings, 11 electric meters, and constrained non-profit budget made it impossible.
EarthKind educated the staff and Board issued RFPs, oversaw the project development, and solved utility, government, & contractor issues.
Oakwood gained a PV solar system that provides 100% of their electricity – with ZERO CASH outlay, along with $30+k/year in utility cost savings.

Clean Energy Results
“EarthKind Energy exceeded our expectations. Many thanks for a ‘job well done.’” – Robert A. Landman US Navy, Military Sealift Command
Reduce Costs
One of the oldest coed boarding schools in the US, Oakwood Friends School has always been ahead of the trends.
EarthKind initiated the concept for a 600KW solar PV system, conducted the energy use & cost analysis, worked with the Committee & Board to develop the project, wrote the RFP, managed the bid process, helped choose the installer, and was Oakwood’s “Owner Representative” throughout a 2 year process that will save Oakwood over $1 Million – with zero cash outlay.
Reduce Carbon
The City of Binghamton hired EarthKind in 2009 to write grants and install their first onsite renewable energy generation system at the City's Water Treatment Plan. EarthKind secured a $320,922 federal recovery grant that reduced the City’s investment by 85%. The system is saving the City hundreds of thousands of taxpayer dollars, and eliminates more than 23 tons of carbon dioxide production annually.
In 2015, a new City administration retained EarthKind to craft the strategy & vision, assemble the team, and author a proposal that won an initial $100k in NY Prize Community MicroGrid awards, and resulted in another $1 Million of MicroGrid grant funding.
Capture Incentives
EarthKind initiated and led the NYS Solar Thermal Consortium (STC), bringing together more than 130 stakeholders from industry, labor, academia, non-profit and government agencies to identify the hurdles and create a plan to develop a vibrant Solar Thermal industry and position the state to become a national leader in the Solar Thermal industry.
The PSC ordered NYSERDA to create a $30M solar hot water incentive fund in 2011, and in 2014 Ron led another effort that provided $3.5M in “fuel-neutral” solar hot water incentives for oil and gas customers.
Help Do The Right Thing
Ronnybrook Dairy Farm is a third generation family farm in New York's Hudson Valley, offering a full line of dairy products made in small batches, delivered at peak freshness with a philosophy to “keep a small piece of America natural and alive”.
EarthKind secured a USDA grant and then initiated New York State’s first “Heat Purchase Agreement” - installing a large solar hot water system that reduces the farm’s oil and propane costs - without any initial cash outlay by Ronnybrook.

About The Founder
Ron Kamen has decades of expertise & leadership in catalyzing exponential change in sustainable energy adoption with governments, non-profits, and businesses. He has developed successful public-private partnerships in clean power & sustainability programs, projects, marketing, outreach, and policy.
While President of the NY Solar Energy Industries Association, Ron led the campaign that culminated in the $1 Billion NY Sun program. He built and managed successful wind power & solar energy marketing, sales, and public education campaigns, and is a recognized coalition builder with the renewable energy industry, distribution utility companies, Energy Service Companies (ESCOs), government leaders, and environmental organizations.
Sustainable Westchester retained Ron and his firm, EarthKind Energy, to create its Clean Transportation Program, which moved Westchester to become #1 in Electric Vehicles (EVs) adoption in New York State. Ron was then asked to create a “Heat Smart” program that secured over $1 Million of NY State funding for clean heating and cooling, and gained the support of both U.S. Senators, Westchester County Executive, full County Legislature, and the Business Council of Westchester, as well as environmental and industry organizations.

Case Studies
EarthKind has demonstrated outstanding success in representing a diverse range of clients, including commercial developers, governmental entities, nonprofits, and homeowners, in sustainable construction and clean energy projects. Here are a few notable examples:
1. High-Efficiency Heat Pumps and Solar Projects - The Young Companies:
EarthKind represented The Young Companies in installing high-efficiency heat pumps at two mixed-use, multifamily locations. The outcome of EarthKind's efforts included securing a cash incentive of $95,897. Additionally, EarthKind issued solar Requests for Proposals (RFPs), negotiated contracts, and obtained an impressive $293,910 in solar cash incentives. The comprehensive approach extended to securing an additional $175,127 in tax incentives, resulting in solar projects with a payback period of less than one year. EarthKind's work on EV charging stations yielded cash incentives of $95,148 and an additional $119,471 in tax credits, ensuring an immediate return on investment for the EV charging stations.
2. HVAC Utility Incentives - Harrison Playhouse Lofts:
Engaged by a real estate developer during the renovation of the Harrison Playhouse Lofts, EarthKind Energy identified overlooked utility incentives for high-efficiency HVAC systems. The successful result was the securing of a cash incentive of $140,166, optimizing the overall financial benefits of the project.
3. Residential Geothermal Project (Westchester County, New York):
EarthKind represented a residential client constructing a new 13,000 square foot home with geothermal heating and cooling in Larchmont, NY. The client received an impressive $158,730 from the utility, marking the largest residential geothermal incentive in the state's history. Additionally, EarthKind worked with the client’s CPAs to capture an additional $315,364 in tax credits.
4. Solar, Heat Pumps, EVs & Charging Stations - New York State Bridge Authority (NYSBA):
Serving as NYSBA’s clean energy consultant since 2015, EarthKind created a solar project providing 20% of NYSBA’s electricity, secured incentives for EVs and charging stations, developed a “Clean Fleet Transition Plan,” and crafted a “Building Decarbonization Plan” to transition away from fossil-fueled heating.
5. Solar with Zero Cash Outlay - Oakwood Friends School:
Overcoming initial skepticism, EarthKind facilitated the installation of a PV solar system at the nonprofit Oakwood Friends School with zero cash outlay. The system provides 100% of their net electricity, realizing $1+M in projected utility cost savings over the project’s 30-year lifespan.
6. Connecticut Solar Project – Peak Wellness:
EarthKind analyzed the solar potential for Dr. Carlon Colker's office complex in Greenwich, CT. The project, managed by EarthKind, not only reduced electricity costs and gained annual ZREC cash incentives but also generated a 20% annual return on investment.
7. New Jersey LED Lighting Project - PCS Managed Services & IT Support:
EarthKind initiated the replacement of PCS’ aged lighting system with state-of-the-art LEDs. The project resulted in an 80% reduction in lighting costs while virtually eliminating monthly maintenance expenses.
EarthKind's expertise and comprehensive approach optimizes financial incentives for our clients, leading to the successful implementation of cost-reducing sustainable clean energy projects that avoid “leaving money on the table” and save our clients countless hours of research, project development, and management time.

Success Story
Oakwood Friends School Solar Project
The Oakwood Friends School always wanted solar but had been told that their 21 shaded buildings, 11 electric meters, and constrained non-profit budget made it impossible.
EarthKind educated the staff and Board issued RFPs, oversaw the project development, and solved utility, government, & contractor issues.
Oakwood gained a PV solar system that provides 100% of their electricity – with ZERO CASH outlay, along with $30+k/year in utility cost savings.

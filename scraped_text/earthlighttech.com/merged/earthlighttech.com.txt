Looking for a trusted residential solar company in Connecticut, Massachusetts, or Oregon? Earthlight Technologies specializes in high-quality solar panel installations that help homeowners reduce their energy bills, increase property value, and contribute to a cleaner environment.
Switching to solar offers long-term financial and environmental benefits. By generating your own electricity, you can protect yourself from rising utility rates, reduce dependence on the grid, and take advantage of valuable state and federal tax incentives. Many homeowners see a significant return on investment, with lower monthly energy costs and increased property value. Plus, solar energy reduces your carbon footprint, making it a sustainable choice for your home and the environment.
Our expert solar installers handle everything from custom system design to permitting, installation, and activation, making your switch to solar seamless. We conduct thorough site assessments to determine the best solar solution for your home, whether it's a roof-mounted, ground-mounted, or tracker solar panel system. Our team also takes care of all local permitting and utility approvals, ensuring a hassle-free installation process.
No matter your home’s size, location, or energy needs, Earthlight Tech offers custom solar solutions to help you maximize savings and enjoy the amazing benefits of going solar.
Comprehensive Residential Solar Solutions
At Earthlight Technologies, we go beyond standard solar installations to provide complete energy solutions that enhance efficiency, reliability, and long-term savings for homeowners.
Battery Storage Solutions
For homeowners looking to maximize energy independence, battery storage systems, like the Tesla Powerwall and FranklinWH battery system, allow you to:
Store excess solar energy for backup power during outages.
Use stored energy when utility rates are highest, reducing electricity costs.
Improve energy security and reduce reliance on the grid.
Off-Grid Solar Cabin Solutions
Looking to power a backyard office, guest house, recreation space, or she-shed? Our off-grid solar cabins provide a self-sustaining energy solution for spaces that need reliable power without a grid connection. These customized systems are ideal for:
Backyard retreats and home offices: Work or relax with renewable, uninterrupted energy.
Guest houses and accessory buildings: Power additional living spaces with solar-generated electricity.
Recreation spaces and workshops: Keep your she-shed, game room, or creative studio powered year-round.
Ongoing System Maintenance & Support
We offer comprehensive solar maintenance and support services to ensure your solar system continues to operate at peak performance with:
Real-time system monitoring to track energy production and detect potential issues.
Routine maintenance and troubleshooting to keep your system running efficiently.
Upgrade options for expanding your solar setup or integrating battery storage in the future.
Take Control of Your Energy Bills
Switching to solar is a smart financial decision, and various tax credits, rebates, and financing programs make it more affordable than ever. At Earthlight Tech, we help homeowners navigate available incentives to maximize savings and reduce upfront costs.
From federal tax credits to state-specific rebates and net metering programs, our team ensures you take full advantage of the financial benefits available in your area. We also offer flexible financing options, including solar loans and leasing programs, making it easier to start saving with solar—no matter your budget.
With our expert guidance and industry knowledge, you can feel confident that you’re getting the best possible return on your investment while making the switch to clean, renewable energy.

And if you are going solar soon, replacing the roof first avoids the cost and hassle of removing panels later.

One point of coordination
A predictable project timeline
A smooth transition from roof work to solar panel installation
A reliable foundation for long-term energy performance
Even if you only need roofing today, choosing Earthlight sets you up for a stress-free solar project whenever you’re ready.
Seamless Warranty Protection for Roof + Solar
Whether your project involves just a roof or a full roof and solar installation, Earthlight helps ensure your roofing and solar warranties are aligned and work together to protect your home.
Benefits include:
Clear warranty coverage with trusted roofing partners
A secure, warrantied surface for solar installation
Long-term protection for both your roof and your energy system
Homeowners who complete a roof and solar panel installation together enjoy the added confidence of warranties designed to support both investments.

What Is a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)?
Pay for the solar electricity your system produces—not the system itself.
A solar PPA lets you go solar without buying the equipment. Instead of paying upfront for panels and installation, you agree to purchase the electricity your system generates at a set price per kilowatt-hour (kWh).
That price is locked in through your agreement, giving you stability compared to utility rates that can change over time. You only pay for the energy your system produces, so your costs align directly with your solar output.
The system is owned by a third party, but installed on your property. You enjoy clean, renewable power at predictable rates, while your provider handles installation, monitoring, and maintenance.
Who is a Solar PPA Right for?
A solar PPA is ideal for homeowners who want the benefits of solar without the upfront cost or long-term financing. It’s a smart choice if you:
Want to lower monthly electricity bills right away
Prefer fixed, predictable payments instead of rising utility costs
Don’t want to deal with equipment ownership or maintenance

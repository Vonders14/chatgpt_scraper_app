WILMINGTON HOUSING AUTHORITY (WHA)
Location: Wilmington, DE
System Size: 1.67 MW DC
Installation Type: Brownfield Ground-Mount
Installation Year: 2018
Ecogy Delaware II consists of a 1.67MWdc brownfield ground-mount for the Wilmington Housing Authority, the largest direct provider of affordable housing in Delaware. This project offsets energy costs at multiple high-rise buildings which couldn't access solar.
The project consisted of hiring and training WHA residents all while WHA is guaranteed to save over a million dollars over the life of the project. The project was selected as a model to expand low-income access to solar in the urban environment through the Department of Energy Solar In Your Community Challenge.

mARYKNOLL
Location: Ossining, NY
System Size: 873.6 kW DC
Installation Type: Parking Lot Canopy
Installation Year: 2021
Ecogy Maryknoll is a 873.6 kW DC community solar project, likely the largest in Westchester. The system is comprised of 12 parking lot canopies hosted by the Maryknoll Fathers and Brothers of Ossining.
All electrical wiring will be underground so no utility poles will be needed. The system will employ bifacial glass panels, allowing light filtering through to produce energy on both sides and 100% of the energy generated by this system will be grid injected and available for Community Solar subscribers.

The Ecogy Ethos
Ecogy’s goal is to create renewable energy solutions that are distributed, de-carbonized, digitized and democratized. Ultimately, we are working towards creating a global electric grid that is reliable, resilient, sustainable, affordable, secure, and flexible.
Ecogy Energy prioritizes equitable development over portfolio size, a mantra that began as an idea and transformed into a company-wide ideology, informing and directing project development, hiring, customer service, and partner choice.
Environmental Justice
Ecogy believes that a just renewable energy transition must address systemic inequality while simultaneously creating clean energy jobs and combating climate change. Our projects aim to serve disadvantaged communities, affordable housing communities, and LMI individuals.
Ecogy has developed over 26 projects that serve low-to-moderate income community members in 5 states, D.C., and the Caribbean. These projects range from affordable housing communities to senior living communities to housing authorities, but all revolve around the central mission of providing renewable energy access to the historically underserved.
In addition to developing projects that directly benefit LMI community members and disadvantaged communities, Ecogy has dedicated lobbyists. These lobbyists stay up-to-date with prospective legislation or renewable energy programs, participate in working groups including the Low Income VDER Working Group, and generate comments that advocate for LMI adoption of and access to solar.
Local Contributions
Ecogy partners with local electricians, arborists, landscapers, roofers, and pavers in order to maximize economic benefits for the local communities we serve.
Ecogy strives to uplift our clients by hosting ribbon cutting ceremonies with local officials and publishing press releases that shine a spotlight on our clients’ sustainability efforts.

How it works…
Ecogy
Finances the construction of a solar system; you pay zero upfront and ongoing costs.
Ecogy is solely responsible for the installation, maintenance, and removal of the system at the end of its lifetime.
Your Organization
Agrees to host the system for an annual lease payment, reroof subsidy or upfront payment or purchase the energy generated by the system at a significantly lower fixed cost than your current energy bill.

Ecogy’s Development Cycle
-
Full Site Evaluation
Ecogy will coordinate with you to collect all of the electrical, structural and site-specific information required to design and engineer our solar system. As part of this process, one of our licensed drone pilots will survey your property to collect high-resolution imagery and topographical measurements.
-
Site Plan and Electrical Design
Once our surveying team has collected all of the necessary information about your property, they will work with our in-house design and engineering team to develop preliminary site plans including solar system layouts and electrical one-line diagrams. These plans will follow local electrical, building and fire codes and will be reviewed by your local electrical utility.
-
Structural Analysis
Insuring that your building is structurally suitable to host a rooftop solar system is of paramount importance to Ecogy. A detailed structural analysis will be completed to determine the exact weight that your building can hold including loads such as snow, wind and roof material.
-
Electrical & Structural Engineering Stamp
The final step to our structural and electrical design and engineering process is to receive a stamp on our system layout and electrical diagrams from locally licensed Professional Engineers.
-
Interconnection Application
When our design has been completed and stamped, Ecogy will initiate the interconnection application process with your local utility. Every solar project is unique, so your utility will usually take 3-4 months to review and study the impact of our proposed solar generation on the electrical grid. This is required to make sure that our operations maintain the safety and reliability of the grid. After this review is complete, Ecogy receives approval to interconnect by signing an Interconnection Services Agreement.
-
Permit Application
After your utility approves our design, Ecogy begins the process of receiving local building and electrical permits from the city or town that will have jurisdiction over the project. In some cases, your town may require Ecogy to obtain a special use permit in order for the Planning Board to review our installation and its potential impact within the community. Ecogy takes pride in focusing our efforts on roof-mounted, parking lot canopy and brownfield solar projects which tend to be welcomed additions to any neighborhood.
-
Utility Interconnection & Permitting Approval
Yay!
-
Construction
With all of our interconnection and local permits in-hand, Ecogy will procure all required electrical and racking equipment, deliver it to site and begin construction of your solar system. We contract with local construction firms with firsthand knowledge of the community landscape who build to our specification - allowing for exceptional quality and more efficiency in the permitting and planning process. We will coordinate closely with property representatives along the way to make sure that we work on a schedule that fits best with your operations. The construction timeline will vary depending on the size and complexity of the system and can take anywhere from 1-4 months.

What We Offer:
In addition to helping businesses, nonprofits and municipalities build solar PV systems, Ecogy Energy deploys capital for distributed-generation projects across the United States, US Virgin Islands and the Caribbean. This includes…
-
Project Finance
Ecogy has completed transactions for projects both operating and in development through traditional take-out financing at various stages of development. Ecogy is also able to offer working capital or a custom milestone payment schedule to bring projects to fruition.
-
Tax Equity
Ecogy's internal sponsor and tax equity allows for effective monetization and accurate valuation from Day 1.
-
SREC Contracts
Ecogy's internal management system creates efficiencies in managing a portfolio of varying projects with differing incentives such as SREC contracts both contracted and uncontracted. Ecogy trades SRECs daily and is always happy to consider transacting SRECs in U.S. markets.
-
Project Documentation
Ecogy's experience owning and operating systems for a decade has created an extensive database of boilerplate investment grade project documents. Our internal management system creates a streamlined approach to managing these documents.
Interested in partnering on a renewable energy project? Reach out - Ecogy finances distributed generation projects of all sizes at any stage of development.

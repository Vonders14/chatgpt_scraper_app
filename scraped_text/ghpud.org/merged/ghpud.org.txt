The Grays Harbor Public Utility District (PUD) employs between 160 and 165 staff. From the customer service professionals who assist customers with their accounts to the crews who physically maintain the utility infrastructure, the goal is to fulfill the utility mission of delivering high-quality utility services to the PUD’s customers.
Events
- In addition to daily work, staff are also visible throughout Grays Harbor, showing the PUD flag at a number of events
- Each May and August, the PUD takes part in the Master Gardeners Home and Garden Show and the Grays Harbor County Fair in Elma
- The PUD booths are among the most visited at the events and the information given out by staff helps customers with energy safety and conservation and a better understanding of how the PUD serves the community
- PUD staff are also happy to help inspire the next generation of utility workers by attending career days at local schools, colleges, and gatherings
Community Involvement
- The majority of the staff are residents of Grays Harbor and as such they serve their community by being active in charitable organizations, working in city government, coaching youth sports, and taking part in community events
- PUD staff want to help make Grays Harbor a great place to live and raise a family
- They want Grays Harbor to be known as a place where people care about one another, a place where people help people

About Public Utility Districts
A Public Utility District (PUD) is a not-for-profit utility formed by a vote of the people
- PUDs are public entities, governed by a nonpartisan, locally elected board of commissioners
Commissioners Responsibilities
- Appointing the utility General Manager
- Setting rates
- Establishing policy
- Approving budgets
- Providing direction for the operation of the utility
- Commissioners serve a 6-year term

- Commission meetings are held on the first and third Monday of each month (unless otherwise announced)
- Board Workshops: At 3:30 p.m. on meeting days, the Commissioners and Senior Leadership Team hold a workshop session during which issues impacting the PUD are reported on and discussed. Workshop sessions are open to the public.
- Time of Board Business Meeting: Meetings begin immediately following the workshop OR as early as 4:00 p.m.

Surplus Materials, Vehicles & Equipment
The district offers surplus material, vehicles, and equipment to local educational, governmental and no-profit agencies first. These groups include a variety of educational institutions (such as colleges and school districts) as well as local public agencies (such as counties, cities, or municipal corporations). Agencies interested in surplus can email purchasing@ghpud.org for more information.
Any surplus items not purchased by qualifying agencies are made available to the public for purchase. The majority of our surplus items are sold using an online public auction. The district currently uses GovDeals for its online auction service. This auction site will have any current surplus material, vehicle, or equipment available to the public.

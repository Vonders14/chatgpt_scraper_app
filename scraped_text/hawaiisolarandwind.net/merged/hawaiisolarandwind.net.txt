ABOUT
Introducing Hawaii Solar and Wind
Is your home or business ready to harness solar power and take control of your energy consumption? Turn to the experts at Hawaii Solar and Wind, a trusted solar panel installer based in Wailuku, HI. As a locally owned and operated business, we are dedicated to providing exceptional services to both residential and commercial customers throughout the area. Our licensed and insured technicians cater to your specific needs with customized solutions, at competitive prices.
Our mission is to build on a tradition of trust. Through dedication, collaboration, customer satisfaction, and value creation, we strive to exceed your expectations in every way. With our expertise in solar installation, battery bank services, ground-mounted system services, and off-grid solar system services, we have all your renewable energy needs covered. Our team is highly skilled in solar electric system repair and installation, PV system repair, grid-tied systems, and inverter services. We also offer solar EV charging system installation and battery backup service. We are here to ensure that your journey towards energy independence is seamless and hassle-free. We submit all the required documents to the Federal Government for your Solar Rebate and have it mailed directly to you.

Let us be your trusted partner in providing quality solar solutions tailored to your unique requirements.

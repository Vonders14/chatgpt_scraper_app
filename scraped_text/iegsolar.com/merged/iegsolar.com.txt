- Here are some of the things you’ll be able to do with our travel Solar Harvesting Operation Node:
- Charge your laptop
- Charge your cameras
- Charge mini appliances
- Power your printer
- Lighting for up to 3 rooms
- And more!
Electricity! Don’t leave home without it!
HOW IT WORKS
Infinity Energy has created the Solar Harvesting Operations Node (SHON), which is a distinctive port box that houses a powerhouse box that acts as a generator of electricity using small solar panels. We use solar panels to charge low voltage batteries for current use and storage for future use. This allows for portable electricity. The users interact with the hassle-free Solar Harvesting Operation Node to charge their cellphones, read, listen to the radio, charge their laptops, mini-fridges and watch entertainment all from their remote location. These items are attached to the SHON to provide access to the stored energy. The simplistic process is user-friendly.

URL: https://iegsolar.com/blog-post/corrosive/

Infinity Energy – Global educates international users on how to store the excess energy from their generators. These corrosive batteries above are an example of the improper use of battery storage in remote countries. These countries must use what they have to store the energy created by their diesel generators. These are also very dangerous as the corrosion gives off harmful gases that could explode.
Our recommendation is to create an outdoor unit that is similar to a miniature shed. This provides the batteries the air that they need and protection from the rain. Our next recommendation is to change from the diesel generators to solar generators to change the connections to non-exposed hoses to prevent corrosion. Lastly, we recommend switching to sealed-lead batteries to dramatically cut maintenance, reduce replacement costs and end the use of water in the batteries.

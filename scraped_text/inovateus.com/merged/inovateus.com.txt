Solar and Energy Storage Development
Inovateus Solar provides high-quality, personalized solar and energy storage development services for commercial, industrial, municipal, and utility solar projects.
Our team has over 410 years of collective experience in the areas of solar energy, construction management, logistics, and procurement. Brilliant Capital financing enables our development capabilities to be paired with a full suite of EPC services. Our commitment to sustainability and experience within the sector allows Inovateus to deliver the most value for your solar and energy storage investments. Our Project Portfolio.
Solar/Energy Storage Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) Services
In addition to solar development, Inovateus provides quality EPC solar and energy storage solutions for businesses, cities, utilities, co-ops, and leading solar developers.
Inovateus Solar is leading the U.S. in sustainable EPC services with zero-waste construction practices, pollinator ground cover, and design methods which emphasize a holistic approach to our impact on the land and environment. Our integrated project teams incorporate sustainable practices in their approach to project development, permitting, design, engineering, construction, interconnection, and commissioning, final inspection.
Sustainable O&M
In addition to developing and constructing projects, Inovateus partners with a national network of experienced operations and maintenance (O&M) professionals to ensure maximum solar uptime at the lowest levelized cost of energy (LCOE). Our added value is Inovateus’ focus on sustainability. We support the landowners and project developers by minimizing and often eliminating O&M landfill waste, recycling decommissioned equipment, and by preserving land for agriculture, recreation, or other non-solar development. Stewardship & Sustainability
Inovateus Solar was founded with the mission of creating innovative energy solutions for our clients. Today, we are the top Midwest solar energy provider to businesses, municipalities, schools, and utilities. We believe that following the best sustainable practices for solar development, construction, and operations benefits our clients, our company, and the health and well-being of everyone on Earth.
As part of our environmental, social, and governance (ESG) plan, Inovateus has set a goal of our operations being carbon-neutral by 2025. To achieve this goal and to become solar industry leaders in sustainability, Inovateus launched a stewardship team focused on:
- Zero-waste construction
- Recycling modules and construction materials
- Improving energy efficiency
- Reducing energy consumption at all facilities and job sites
- Procuring ethically sourced sustainable equipment
- Planting Bee & Butterfly pollinator-friendly ground cover
- Sustainable end-of-life decommissioning
Please download our annual sustainability and ESG report, which describes our specific ESG goals, best practices, and yearly and culminative milestones.

When Tom founded Inovateus in 2008, he built a company that blended Midwestern values with an indomitable entrepreneurial spirit that allowed employees to flourish while fulfilling our mission to “build a brilliant tomorrow.”
Since then, Inovateus has grown from a small Midwest solar contractor to becoming a leading national solar developer and EPC with over 515+ MW’s of solar projects in 23 states.
Join Inovateus in building a brilliant tomorrow. Our entire team is committed to investing in your energy future through experienced solar development, reliable EPC & O&M services, Brilliant Capital financing, and a strong dedication to environmental stewardship.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Inovateus Solar completed three solar projects for Northern Indiana Public Service Company (NIPSCO). The projects, which are the utility’s first solar installations, include a ground-mount solar array at NIPSCO’s Merrillville, Indiana, headquarters. Consistent with NIPSCO’s steps toward a more sustainable energy future, Inovateus Solar achieved zero-waste construction by recycling equipment packaging and avoiding any construction waste being sent to landfills.
A 120-kWAC array with 432 solar panels was installed at NIPSCO’s Merrillville headquarters, which will generate 216,958 kWh of clean power annually. Another 100-kWAC system with 360 panels was installed at the energy company’s LaPorte local operating office, and a similar-sized system was installed at their Valparaiso local operating office. Each of those projects will produce 181,841 kWh of solar power per year.

Residential Installations
Your Island Pacific Energy Account Executive meets with you to discuss the best opportunities for solar power for your home.
Once information about your home is collected, your Island Pacific Energy Account Executive analyzes the data to determine the most appropriate photovoltaic solar energy system for your home.
Island Pacific Energy offers a variety of custom tailored options to pay for your system. Your Account Executive can help you select the best option for you that is designed to save you the most money.
Island Pacific Energy's engineers custom design your system including detailed system layout and electrical diagrams taking into account factors such as location, shading, electric usages, roof area, etc.
Once the system design is approved by you, Island Pacific Energy procures the equipment, submits the necessary building permits and completes the required utility interconnection agreements.
Island Pacific Energy coordinates with you to select the best date and time to install the PV system on your home. Construction typically takes one to two days.
Once construction is complete, the system is verified and tested. The system is then inspected by the local building and electrical inspector.
The interconnections agreement is processed and local utility is notified the system is complete and ready for operation.
When all testing, permitting and interconnections are completed, the system is energized and you begin producing your own power.
Your Island Pacific Energy Account Executive meets with you to review the system, provide you with all the documentation including manuals, warranties and guarantees, and answers any questions you may have.

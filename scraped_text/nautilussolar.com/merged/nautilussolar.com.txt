Revolutionizing Our Planet, One Solar Farm at a Time
Save Money with Community Solar
Sunshine may power our solar farms, but it is the 45,000+ customers that power Nautilus Solar Energy®. When you sign up, you'll:
- Save money on your monthly electric bill
- Support clean, renewable energy initiatives in your state
No upfront costs. No rooftop panels. No hassle.
To produce the same amount of power as our farms, coal plants produce millions of pounds of CO2.
Coal, nuclear, and natural gas can’t compete with the renewable energy produced by our solar farms. And while there are many ways to avoid carbon emissions, choosing community solar over power from a gas or coal plant is among the easiest and most cost-effective way to do your part for the planet. For example, if you made no changes in your lifestyle and joined Nautilus Solar, your impact would benefit the planet as much as 200 mature trees absorbing CO2. So, you could plant a forest and wait about 50 years, or you could spend 8 minutes joining Nautilus Solar.
Your bills won’t be higher. They will actually be lower.
Doing the right thing becomes a great thing when you are paying less. Over the course of the year, customers are guaranteed a discount of 5% or more(10% or more for most states) on their electricity. Three things make this possible. First, tax credits incentivize the building of community solar farms. Second, your local solar farm is backed by Nautilus Solar, with 150 solar farms and over 45,000 subscribers, we know how to create solar power efficiently. Thirdly, community solar power is cheaper to produce than coal, gas, or nuclear power.
Invest just a few minutes, not tens of thousands of dollars.
Now, you can make a big difference: no matter how much you make, the size of your home, or whether you rent or own. And, you won’t need to make a major investment in solar panels on your roof or put a new electric car in your driveway to have the same effect. That’s why Nautilus Solar is the easy choice in alternative energy. All it takes is a few minutes and no dollars to join and you can do your part for the planet.
Get started today!
The chance to do a good thing for the environment is right now because each of our solar farms can only serve a limited number of customers. So sign up today!

Building solar farms that serve nature and their communities
When you build a solar farm the right way, the local environment and community are well served. The only way to achieve these twin goals is to build, maintain, and operate our own solar farms throughout their entire lifecycle. It starts with choosing sites that allow us to have the least impact on the productivity of the land or disrupt wildlife.
Often, our sites are built on lands that are in some way already compromised or unusable like quarries or landfills. And, after 40 years when our farms may be decommissioned, we restore the land to a healthy habitat according to our contract. We never delegate our responsibilities to you, to your community, or to the natural environment.
Our Name and Symbolism
The Nautilus name stems from the USS Nautilus, the world’s first operational nuclear-powered submarine, reflecting our co-Founder Jim Rice’s background as a Navy Submarine Veteran. Similarly, our logo is inspired by the chambered nautilus shell, symbolizing grace, growth, expansion, and renewal – all key elements of our journey at Nautilus Solar Energy.

How solar advantages work for you, your community, and nature.
Nautilus Solar Energy® has a deep commitment to our customers, their communities, and the planet. So, we use our extensive experience and expertise to create efficient, sustainable solar farms with benefits that go beyond electric power.
How a solar farm becomes a reality.
Step 1
We receive upfront tax incentives for building solar farms and adding clean energy to the grid.
Step 2
We find unusable land or unneeded land for our sites.
Step 3
Landowners receive a new income stream, which can often lead to tax relief too.
Step 4
Local residents and businesses, including the underserved, can become a customer and receive lower-cost electricity and promote clean energy.
Step 5
Solar farms are built and begin operating creating new jobs for the local community.
Step 6
Solar farms feed power into the local grid, reducing the reliance on the need for coal or gas power plants to produce electricity.
Step 7
Customers receive solar credits that give them discounts on their electricity bills.
Step 8
Local communities gain new tax incentives, a more resilient grid, a healthier environment, and greater energy independence.
Step 9
The planet doesn't suffer from thousands of pounds of CO2 entering the atmosphere as reliance on burning fossil fuels is reduced.

How Community Solar Works?
Wait. I can get the benefits of solar without panels on my roof?
1. Solar Farms Built Close to You
These aren’t far-off operations. As a community solar developer, we build and operate solar farms centrally located within your region.
2. A Community of Subscribers
Residents like you subscribe for free to take advantage of the solar farm’s energy output. No ownership, just service.
3. Integration with Existing Power Grid
This locally-generated solar energy is integrated into your regular electric grid. No new infrastructures, no disruptions.
4. Unchanged Power Flow, Added Benefits
Your electricity is delivered using the same power lines, so it remains uninterrupted. The difference? You’re part of an energy company with a larger, clean and green initiative. Plus, you enjoy a minimum of 10% savings on your monthly electric bills. No panels, no fuss—just collective progress.
How do Nautilus Solar subscriptions work?
In addition to Nautilus Solar, you will remain a customer of your current utility. And as a Nautilus customer, you'll get access to the energy we produce for less.

Community solar is a program that lets multiple residents and businesses share the benefits of a solar farm without installing solar panels on their own roof. These programs are often state-incentivized and designed to make solar energy accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford rooftop solar systems.
When you join a community solar project, you:
-
Support renewable energy generation in your community
-
Receive savings on your monthly electricity bill.
Your share of a solar farm generates electricity that is credited to your utility bill. This energy is provided at a discounted rate, allowing you to save money while supporting solar.

-

Businesses

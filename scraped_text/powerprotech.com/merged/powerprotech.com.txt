Service Network
Our Nationwide Reach
Your location has a direct impact on the optimal solution that PowerSecure can provide for you. Fortunately, renewable energy technicians are experts in the various markets, conditions and issues that exist in different regions of the country. If you contact the specific PowerSecure representative for your region, we’ll be in touch and ensure that your solution is tailored to your specific needs, no matter where you’re located.
PowerSecure Is Here for You
At PowerSecure, we specialize in developing reliable and sustainable energy solutions for a broad range of industries, including data centers, healthcare facilities, utility providers, major retailers, government agencies and other power-hungry users.
From energy efficiency upgrades to distributed energy resources (DERs) built around renewable power sources to emergency and commercial backup power to autonomous microgrids that can deliver reliable energy even during grid outages — we design all our solutions from the ground up based on your unique energy and savings goals.
We also handle all local permitting requirements to ensure your energy project complies with the latest regulatory requirements and building codes. In addition, our team of energy experts can help you qualify for any number of municipal, state and federal incentives to help shorten the payback period of your investment.
If you work in an industry that needs reliable access to uninterrupted electricity, PowerSecure can custom design a turnkey solution that delivers the energy you require — at a price you can afford. Equally important, our team of microgrid experts also provide round-the-clock monitoring, complete with onsite renewable energy maintenance and emergency servicing to ensure you benefit from optimal uptime regardless of grid conditions.
Whether you need to troubleshoot a solar photovoltaic (PV) installation, battery energy storage solution (BESS) or advanced microgrid, we can dispatch a local energy technician team to repair the issue immediately so that you can continue generating, storing and using power with confidence.
If you’re looking for a service provider that offers end-to-end support with proprietary hardware and tightly integrated software, contact us today. PowerSecure is a national leader in large-scale, bespoke microgrid energy solutions for major stakeholders who require reliable, uninterrupted energy to power critical services across health care, IT, business and national security.

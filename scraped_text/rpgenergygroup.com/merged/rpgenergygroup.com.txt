Air Nozzle Retrofit
Customer Type: Engineered Fastening Manufacturer
Location: Kentucky, USA
720,650 kWh annual savings
503 metrics tons of CO2 avoided
Project Overview
Our customer relies heavily on compressed air to operate their manufacturing process. With the addition of engineered air nozzles, RPG improved efficiency, performance, and safety in various industrial and commercial compressed air applications. Given this particular project, the local site elected to replace old, outdated air nozzles with engineered steel nozzles at each process line. Operating costs and compressed air load at this process were reduced by 50% as a result of the engineered nozzle implementation. These nozzles provide better blow-off capability, meaning airflow is now more concentrated and powerful. Another important benefit is the reduction in noise of the process line creating a safer work environment.

Barrel Insulation
Customer Type: Outdoor Equipment Manufacturer
Location: Mississippi, USA
308,880 kWh annual savings
219 metrics tons of CO2 avoided
Project Overview
At RPG Energy Group, we provide barrel insulation jackets for a variety of heating elements within different processes. With better insulated machinery, the customers' process equipment can provide the same heat output with less energy as well as better control the surrounding temperatures for employees.
Plastic injection molding is an extremely energy intensive process, but also a necessary process utilized by one of our manufacturing customers. Not only did the proposed barrel insulation greatly reduce electricity required to keep the barrel warm, the insulation also improved the safety of each station. For example, barrels were not directly exposed outside of protective shrouds, as well as working conditions were much cooler. HVAC use in this vicinity was greatly reduced and less cooling needed, another indirect benefit of applying insulation to injection molding machines.

Solar UV

Location: Mississippi, USA

Waste Heat to Power (ORC)
Customer Type: Automotive Manufacturer
Location: Tennessee, USA
355,000 kWh annual savings
248 metrics tons of CO2 avoided
Project Overview
At RPG Energy Group, we provide waste heat to power solutions, such as ORC (Organic Rankine Cycle) systems that capture waste heat from industrial processes and convert it into usable electricity. This not only improves overall efficiency but also reduces the reliance on external energy sources, offering both economic and environmental benefits.
RPG Energy Group recently installed an ORC system at an outdoor automotive manufacturing facility converting waste heat generated by the production process into clean electricity. This solution resulted in 355MWh reduction in energy consumption and repurposed previously wasted heat, contributing to the facility’s sustainability goals. The project demonstrated the potential of waste heat recovery in reducing energy consumption and carbon emissions, making it a win-win for both operations and sustainability.

Real Time Energy Monitoring
Customer Type: Industrial Manufacturing
Location: 35 Project Sites, USA
Electricity, Natural Gas, Water
Project Overview
RPG Energy Group offers energy monitoring solutions that give businesses real-time visibility into their energy usage, allowing for more informed decisions and immediate improvements in efficiency. With advanced platforms like Factory Manager from Rockwell Automation, companies can track energy consumption, identify inefficiencies, and take action to reduce costs and improve operational performance.
Several manufacturing plants recently implemented RPG Energy Group's energy monitoring solution, utilizing the most advanced technology and methodology to monitor and analyze energy usage across their entire facilities. Individual sites might have a version of their own energy monitoring systems, but not a consistent corporate standard accessible from one dashboard. Now, at the click of a button, corporate and applicable team members, can view, extrapolate, and act on historic and future utility consumption / spend. This new real time monitoring system is vital to understanding baseline energy usage and spend across all manufacturing facilities.
